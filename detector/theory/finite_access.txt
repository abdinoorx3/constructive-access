TARGET AND COMMON ASSUMPTIONS
Circle dphi=omega dt+sqrt(2gamma)dW, gamma>0 fixed. Exposure duration tau>0.
A=tau^-1 integral_0^tau cosphi dt; observed X=A+N(0,nu²), nu>0 fixed initially.
Read noise independent of target and other read errors. Target M(omega)=
omega²/gamma is the sharp minimum compatible with the ideal observed law,
not an upper bound on arbitrary extra hidden entropy. The latter is already
unbounded by appending invisible dissipative subsystems.

EXACT MOMENTS
Stationary phase uniform: EA=0 and
V(omega)=tau^-2 Re[tau/z-(1-exp(-z tau))/z²], z=gamma-iomega.
At omega=2pi m/tau, a0=1-exp(-gamma tau),
V=[(gamma tau+a0)omega²+(gamma tau-a0)gamma²]/[tau²(gamma²+omega²)²].
Thus V~(gamma tau+a0)/(tau²omega²), and
|V(omega)-V(2omega)|~3(gamma tau+a0)/(4tau²omega²).

Uniformly in the initial phase law, Ito's formula gives
omega tau A=sinphi_tau-sinphi_0+gamma integral sinphi dt
             -sqrt(2gamma) integral cosphi dW.
Therefore EA²<=H2/omega²,
 H2=[2+gamma tau+sqrt(2gamma tau)]²/tau².
Also EA4<=H4/omega4,
 H4=8[(2+gamma tau)^4+12gamma²tau²]/tau4.
Fourth bound uses E[(integral cosphi dW)^4]<=3tau², proved from Ito's
fourth-moment identity, not an unjustified conditional Gaussian assumption.

UNIFORM FINITE UPPER-CONFIDENCE OBSTRUCTION
For any n predetermined stationary exposure windows, even correlated or
overlapping, Gaussian-mixture KL to pure-noise Q=N(0,nu²I) is at most
nV/(2nu²), hence tends0 while M(omega)->infinity.
If U is an extended upper confidence bound uniformly covered at1-alpha over
all omega>0, then Q(U=infinity)>=1-alpha. Every finite-omega noisy law has
positive density everywhere and is equivalent to Q. Thus U cannot be finite
almost surely under any finite-omega model. The stronger claim of infinity
probability>=1-alpha at every fixed model is NOT made; valid lower bound is
1-alpha-TV(Pomega,Q). No adaptive timing/precision protocol covered here.

PASSIVE IID TRIALS: MATCHING FIXED-PRECISION COUNTS
Fresh stationary preparation per trial, known pair omega versus2omega,
equal-prior target error epsilon in(0,1/2). For bounded zero-mean exposure:
chi²(P||Q)=E exp(AA'/nu²)-1
          =sum[k>=2](EA^k)²/(k!nu^(2k)) <=Cnu0 V²,
Cnu0=exp(1/nu²)-1-1/nu² for any bounded zero-mean exposure. In the stationary
circle, phase shift by pi preserves the path law and maps A to -A. Its odd
moments vanish; hence the stronger Cnu=cosh(1/nu²)-1 applies. The count bounds
use this symmetry-tightened constant. Tensorized KL and Pinsker triangle yield
necessary n>=2(1-2epsilon)²/[Cnu(Vomega+V2omega)²].

For sample-X² midpoint test, Var(X²)<=Snu(V),
Snu(V)=(1+4nu²)V-V²+2nu4. Sufficient for BOTH conditional errors<=epsilon:
n>=ceil[4 max(Snu(Vomega),Snu(V2omega))/(epsilon DeltaV²)].
Simpler valid sufficient numerator4(1+6nu²+3nu4) is retained as fallback.

Bounds may have loose constants or vacuous necessary counts below1.

PREPARED IID TRIALS: MATCHING FIXED-PRECISION COUNTS
Actual known phase phi0=pi/2 under both hypotheses. At resonance,
mu(omega)=-a0 omega/[tau(gamma²+omega²)].
DeltaMu=a0 |omega| |2omega²-gamma²|/
        [tau(gamma²+omega²)(gamma²+4omega²)] ~a0/(2tau|omega|).
Centered X is sub-Gaussian with proxy1+nu². Mean midpoint test sufficient:
n>=ceil[8(1+nu²)log(1/epsilon)/DeltaMu²], for nonzero gap.
Ito H2 plus Gaussian-mixture KL gives necessary:
n>=16nu²(1-2epsilon)²omega²/(9H2).
Thus prepared count is Theta(omega²)=Theta(M).
Known initial E cosphi0=0, E sinphi0=r!=0 also works, multiplying mean by r
and sufficient constant by r^-2. Merely preparing Y0=0 with symmetric phases
does NOT establish the needed orientation. Prepared records are not inserted
into a stationary entropy score; they discriminate generators' stationary
ideal-law minimum values. Preparation energy is not assumed zero.

DECLARED PRECISION-ENERGY LAW: THE MEASUREMENT EXPONENTS MATCH
Assume (do not derive as universal physics) read variance nu_i²=kappa/E_i,
kappa>0, fixed predetermined finite energy schedule, independent read errors.
For either stationary or fixed phase preparation,
KL(Pomega||Q)<=H2 sum(E_i)/(2kappa omega²).
Pinsker triangle for omega versus2omega yields necessary measurement energy
sum E_i>=16kappa(1-2epsilon)²omega²/(9H2).
Correlated latent exposures allowed in this lower bound; adaptive energy
schedules and changes to the generator during exposure are not covered.

For passive independent trials choose per-read E=c omega² under BOTH
alternatives, c>0 fixed. Then EX4<=[H4+6kappa H2/c+3kappa²/c²]/omega4
=Bc/omega4. Sample-X² midpoint test sufficient:
n>=ceil[4Bc/(epsilon omega4 DeltaV²)], bounded as omega grows along resonance.
Total measurement energy therefore O(omega²), matching the universal bound.
Prepared trials at fixed nu already use Theta(omega²) readings at fixed
per-read energy, also Theta(omega²) measurement energy. These are the same
asymptotic order, not equal constants or equal optimal procedures.
