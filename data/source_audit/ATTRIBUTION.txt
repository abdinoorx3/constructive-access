IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
These selected files are experimental measurements, not the repository's
separate simulated data. The file named groundtruth contains a simultaneous
LOWER-NOISE MEASUREMENT, not exact latent positions. Its original filename
and every original byte remain unchanged. Serialized force functions in the
file are not measured force trajectories and are not executed by our loader.
Nominal units and sampling interval are documented in the authors' source;
the selected files do not contain individual timestamps or an independently
validated clock/mixing/detector error contract.

Changes made by this research release: NONE to the three HDF5 originals.
Training/evaluation splits, bounded transformations, covariance tables and
other derived results are separate files produced by Abdinoor / 2D Labs.
Those derived analyses are exploratory and are not thermodynamic confidence
certificates or new physical measurements.

REDISTRIBUTION SCOPE
This release redistributes only these three licensed raw HDF5 originals and
the accompanying attribution/provenance needed to identify them. Other source
PDFs, paper XML, third-party notebooks and scripts downloaded during the
literature audit are NOT redistributed as part of the experimental data
bundle. Their original source links remain in the source audit. The separate
Dryad switching-trap archive was not retrieved and is not included.
