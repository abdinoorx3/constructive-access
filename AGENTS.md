# Unified Constructive Access reference and toolkit

Abdinoor / 2D Labs / 12 October 2026

This release is self-contained. Preserve all earlier research directories and
public releases when they are present. Do not submit, publish, contact third
parties or operate physical apparatus without explicit authorization. Read-only
research, local derivation, simulation and reproducibility checks are permitted.
Goal: a self-contained, coherent scientific reference and installable toolkit,
starting from experimental questions and ending with independently checkable
inference, physical examples, and explicit empirical validation requirements.
Existing mathematics must receive attribution. New unification is not evidence
of historical priority, community acceptance, a new physical law, or solved
engineering grand challenges. Generality is stated by mathematical model class.

Root owns reference.tex, README.txt, integrated claims/provenance, packaging,
logs, and final validation. Agents own assigned chapter/module files only.
Every chapter defines notation locally and uses row-state probability convention
unless a displayed quantum density-operator convention is explicitly declared.
Use \chapter, \section, theorem/proposition/lemma/corollary/definition/remark
(environments supplied by root); unique labels prefixed by chapter name.
Citations use \cite{key}; supply bibliography entries in assigned .bib file
or plain \bibitem blocks in research, then root consolidates.
A positive exact contrast, finite-error identification, and perfect separation
are different claims. Action charges are energy only after physical calibration.
Use real, reproducible tests; report counterexamples and absent physical evidence.
Do not edit files assigned to another agent. Ask root to integrate shared APIs.
