JOINT DETECTOR CALIBRATION FOR A DIFFUSION ENTROPY CERTIFICATE
Research proof record, 2026-10-13 UTC. Abdinoor, 2D Labs.
Status: analytic proofs checked by independent internal review. No external
review or physical experiment. This is a model-class theorem, not a new law.

1. CONTRACT AND TARGET

The hidden process is a stationary smooth uniformly elliptic ordinary-reversal
diffusion on a smooth compact connected manifold without boundary. Its stationary
law pi has positive smooth density. Write L=S+A, L^dagger=S-A, Af=v.grad f,
Gamma(f,g)=grad f . D grad g, e=v.D^{-1}v, and sigma=E_pi e. Thus the convention
is d[f(X)]_t=2 Gamma_f(X_t)dt. Let f be smooth real, |f|<=1. The essential extra
structural premise is the exact POINTWISE identity

    Gamma_f(x)=gamma*(1-f(x)^2),   gamma>0 unknown and constant.

This identity is not inferred from a stationary marginal, observed covariance,
or an average volatility estimate. Hidden dimension and drift are otherwise
unrestricted within the class. Known physical bounds are
||L f||inf<=M1+, ||L^dagger f||inf<=M1-,
||L^2 f||inf<=M2+, ||(L^dagger)^2 f||inf<=M2-.
Set M*=min(M1+,M1-), M2*=min(M2+,M2-).

Each target trial is an independent stationary record with nominal centers
-h,0,h. At each center t the common causal boxcar exposes
A_t=tau^{-1} integral[t-tau,t] f(X_s)ds, 0<=tau<=tau_U<=h. At tau=0 use f(X_t).
K=2m+2 center replicas and one readout at each endpoint are obtained. Conditional
on the entire hidden path, all detector randomizations within a trial are
independent, and each bounded readout Z has conditional mean A_t. Independence
of noises does NOT imply independence of hidden states or exposures. A shot
readout in {-1,1} with mean A_t is admissible. No variance calibration is needed.
Unknown common multiplicative gain is handled in Section 7, not silently set1.

Independent stationary trials are an acquisition assumption. Overlapping triples
from one path are not independent trials. Preparation, replica channels, exposure,
reference signals, elapsed time and energy must be accounted for separately.

2. REPLICA SCORE AND IDEAL PHYSICAL INEQUALITY

For the K center readouts define e_k as the normalized elementary symmetric
polynomial (average over all distinct k-subsets), with e_0=1. Conditional on the
hidden path, E[e_k]=A_0^k. Distinct replicas are essential: Z_0^k is generally
biased for A_0^k. Fix m>=0 and lambda real; b=1-lambda*h,

 beta_g=h*(M2+ +M2-)/4,
 beta_K=h*(M1-*M2+ +M1+*M2-)/2,
 beta=2*|lambda|*beta_g+beta_K.

The finite polynomial score is

 T_m=h^{-2} sum[j=0..m] {-Z_- Z_+ e_2j
          +b*(Z_-+Z_+)*e_(2j+1)-b^2*e_(2j+2)}
          -beta*sum[j=0..m] e_2j.

Its expectation equals the same polynomial in the exposed latent values. Let
T_m^ideal denote that polynomial at Y_-,Y_0,Y_+. Then

    E T_m^ideal <= gamma*sigma.                       (2.1)

Proof. Write w_m(y)=sum[j=0..m]y^(2j), q(y)=-lambda*y.
For the ideal triple the uncorrected score is
w_m(Y0)*[-(q-(Y_+-Y0)/h)*(q-(Y_--Y0)/h)].
The earlier finite-time semigroup proof gives conditional bias at most
2|q| beta_g+beta_K<=beta. Conditional on the full center state, the corrected
ideal expectation is at most
w_m(f)*[2q Sf-q^2-(Sf)^2+(Af)^2]
 =w_m(f)*[(Af)^2-(q-Sf)^2] <=w_m(f)*(Af)^2.
The pointwise Cauchy inequality (Af)^2<=Gamma_f*e and structural identity give
w_m(f)*Gamma_f=gamma*(1-f^(2m+2))<=gamma. Integrate. This also proves
the inequality if individual corrected records or expectations are negative.
Only the final covered lower bound may be clipped to zero.

3. A GENERAL MULTITIME BLUR LEMMA

Let F(t_1,...,t_d)=E prod_i f(X_ti), times ordered with nonnegative gaps.
The derivative in the jth gap obeys

 |partial_gap_j F| <= a_(d,j)
 :=min(j^2*M1-, (d-j)^2*M1+),       1<=j<d.        (3.1)

Proof of the word bound. A backward semigroup word g with k factors f has
||g||inf<=1 and ||Lg||L1(pi)<=k^2 M1+. For k=1 this is immediate. Markov
semigroups commute with L on these smooth domains and contract stationary L1.
For multiplication by f, use
L(fg)=f Lg+g Lf+2Gamma(f,g), E Gamma_f=-E fLf<=M1+,
E Gamma_g=-E gLg<=||Lg||1, and pointwise Cauchy for Gamma. Thus the next
bound is B+M1+ +2sqrt(M1+ B), at most k^2 M1+ by induction. At a gap,
the past conditional product is signed but bounded in absolute value by1;
differentiate the suffix and integrate its absolute generator. This gives the
suffix bound. Reverse stationary time and use L^dagger for the prefix bound.
Smooth compactness justifies differentiation also by limits at repeated times.

For any exposure supported within [t_i-tau,t_i], sorted displacements from
ordered nominal times lie in [-tau,0]. Each gap changes by at most tau. Therefore
any d-fold exposed product differs from its ideal moment by at most

    tau * sum[j=1..d-1] a_(d,j).                     (3.2)

Products of exposure averages are integrals over independent AUXILIARY sampling
times. This mathematical integration does not assert independent signal values.

For common UNIFORM boxcars and nonoverlapping nominal clusters, obtain a sharper
explicit coefficient B(p_1,...,p_r), where p_i is the number of factors in each
chronological exposure window. Delete zero clusters and set d=sum p_i. For
every within-cluster gap at global prefix k in a cluster with p factors add

    a_(d,k)/(p+1).

For every cross-cluster gap between p and q factors, at prefix k, add

    a_(d,k)*[1-1/(p+1)-1/(q+1)+2*p!*q!/(p+q+1)!].  (3.3)

Then blur <=tau*B(p_1,...,p_r). For d<=1 the coefficient is0.
Proof: ordered uniform internal gaps have mean1/(p+1). A cross gap's change is
tau*(U_min(q)-U_max(p)); its expected absolute value is the bracket in (3.3).
Use E(U_max)=p/(p+1), E(U_min)=1/(q+1), and
E[(U_min(q)-U_max(p))_+]=integral_0^1 t^p(1-t)^q dt=p!q!/(p+q+1)!.
Apply (3.1), integrate along the straight line between gap vectors, then average.

4. SHARPER SECOND-MOMENT BLUR AND GEOMETRY CALIBRATION

Write C(t)=E[Y0 Yt], D0=E[A0^2], Dh=E[A0 Ah], q0=E Gamma_f.
Stationarity gives C(-t)=C(t), C'(0+)=-q0, q0<=M*, and
|C''(t)|<=M2* for t>=0 (right derivative at0). Hence

 0<=C0-D0<=delta0:=M* tau_U/3,
 |Dh-C(h)|<=deltah:=M2* tau_U^2/12.                (4.1)

The first follows from C(t)<=C0, |C(t)-C0|<=M*|t|, and E|U-V|=tau/3.
The second uses h>=tau and the centered exposure-offset difference, whose
variance is tau^2/6, in the second-order Taylor remainder. It also holds at2h.
More precisely D0=C0-q0*tau/3+R0, |R0|<=M2*tau^2/12, but (4.1) avoids an
unknown q0 inside the calibration envelope.

For any monomial in Section3, use its uniform coefficient or support bound.
For degree2 replace the bound by delta0 for a repeated window, and deltah for
different windows separated by at least h. Denote resulting certified blur
radii for cluster patterns by D(pattern). With zeros deleted, set

 deltaN=h^{-2} sum[j=0..m] {D(1,2j,1)
       +|b|*[D(1,2j+1)+D(2j+1,1)]+b^2*D(2j+2)}
       +beta*sum[j=0..m]D(2j).                    (4.2)

Do not omit the last term: the bias correction's own moment is blurred.
Adjoint bounds need not coincide, so the two B patterns are written separately.

Define population quantities

 P=E T_m-deltaN,
 Abar=1-D0-delta0,
 Hbar=(D0-Dh+delta0+deltah)/h+M2* h/2.

Then P<=gamma*sigma, Abar<=Atrue:=1-C0, and Hbar>=gamma*Atrue.
The last statement follows from
|C0-C(h)-h*q0|<=M2*h^2/2 and q0=gamma*(1-C0).
No local-volatility measurement or stationary arcsine law is used.

5. FINITE CONFIDENCE AND SAME-RECORD CALIBRATION

Suppose simultaneous confidence bounds from the actual acquisition satisfy
p<=P, a<=Abar and u>=Hbar with probability at least1-alpha. If u>0 define

    L=[p]_+*[a]_+/u;                              (5.1)

otherwise return0 with an inconclusive flag. Then P(sigma>=L)>=1-alpha.
On the confidence event, if p and a are positive, gamma*Atrue<=u and
p<=gamma*sigma imply p*a/u<=sigma*a/Atrue<=sigma. Otherwise L=0.
For a>0 the covered geometry bound gamma<=u/a also follows. It is possible
for the procedure to be inconclusive even though the model is true.

One exact construction uses mean intervals for T_m, e2, and
(Z_-+Z_+)*e1/2 from the same independent trials. Form P,Abar,Hbar by interval
arithmetic, selecting the lower endpoint for p,a and upper endpoint for u.
Individual bounded-mean inequalities are unconditional; a union bound gives
their simultaneous coverage regardless of within-trial correlations.
Do not apply an ordinary iid score inequality AFTER selecting its coefficients
from those same data, unless the moment-region or finite-family argument covers
that selection. Data splitting is not necessary when simultaneous coverage is
proved. Data splitting is also not forbidden if chosen and charged.

For bounded records in [-B,B], e_k lies in [-B^k,B^k]. Conservative exact score
range and variance bounds can therefore be obtained algebraically. Either
Hoeffding or a valid empirical-Bernstein theorem may supply the simultaneous
intervals. A sample variance must not be inserted into a known-variance bound.
All numerical endpoints must be outward enclosures; uncertainty is not zero
because a numerical solver terminates.

6. FINITE POLYNOMIAL HIERARCHY AND ITS SHARP CIRCLE LIMIT

For the stationary circle dphi=omega dt+sqrt(2gamma)dW, f=cosphi,
Sf=-gamma*f and (Af)^2=omega^2*(1-f^2). Its entropy is sigma=omega^2/gamma.
The ideal zero-step uncorrected weighted expectation divided by gamma is

 J_m(lambda)=sigma*(1-E[Y^(2m+2)])
       -(lambda-gamma)^2*E[w_m(Y)Y^2]/gamma.        (6.1)

For lambda=gamma, this increases to sigma because
E Y^(2m+2)=binom(2m+2,m+1)/4^(m+1)=O(m^{-1/2}).
For a grid point with |lambda-gamma|<=eta, the extra loss is at most
(m+1)*eta^2/gamma. Thus a finite dense grid and simultaneous confidence, with
selection of the largest covered L, remove the need to know gamma beforehand.
The gamma upper bound in Section5 also converges to gamma when moment error,
blur/h and h vanish and C0<1. This is actual parameter calibration, not an
oracle gamma silently inserted into the score.

A conservative geometric-stage construction improves on the initial
superexponential sample schedule. Use raw target and reference readouts bounded
by1, as in the binary detector example. Fix positive parameter envelopes and valid
generator caps, and a known integer B>=0 with kappa>=kappa_min>=2^(-B).
At stage k take m=k, h=2^(-k), tau<=2^(-4k), K=2k+2,
n=n_ref=2^((8B+16)k), and the lambda grid [0,k] with mesh2^(-k).
Bounded references use a known coarse shutter bracket at the same shrinking
scale. Eventually the lambda grid contains a point within2^(-k) of true gamma.
Allocate fixed total alpha over the finite family (or use summable stage alpha).

Raw score ranges are poly(k)*2^(2k), and degree normalization is at most
2^(2B(k+1)). Hoeffding radii after normalization are thus bounded by
poly(k)*2^(-(2B+6)k). The logarithm of the grid/mean family is O(k).
Differentiating kappa^(-d) gives the same negligible order for reference gain
uncertainty, up to fixed/polynomial factors. Statistical and gain calibration
errors therefore vanish. Generic summed blur is O(k^4*2^(-2k)); finite-step
bias uniformly on the lambda grid is O(k^2*2^(-k)); the nearest grid point's
misspecification is O(k*4^(-k)). The geometry upper bound u/a converges to
gamma. Circle hierarchy residual is O(k^(-1/2)), so the maximum covered
certificate converges in probability to sigma, with signed error
sigma-max L=O_P(k^(-1/2))=O_P((log n)^(-1/2)). This is a sufficient rate,
not an optimal or minimax claim. Direct uniform concentration gives convergence
at fixed advertised confidence; coverage alone would not imply it.

Certified numerical precision must increase too. For example k^2+(4B+12)k
binary digits suffice to make elementary enclosure errors negligible after
degree/time amplification. Log-series truncation and square-root enclosure
must actually refine; printing a fixed approximation to more digits is not
the required refinement. The current fixed-90-bit API remains valid for
each finite design, but its unchanged output has not been claimed to satisfy
the asymptotic limit. Increasing replica access, shrinking exposure and
reference trials are substantive acquisition resources. Neither fixed positive
exposure consistency nor free preparation/reference/measurement energy follows.

7. UNKNOWN GAIN AND EXPOSURE: CONCRETE REFERENCE CALIBRATION

If raw bounded detector R has conditional mean kappa*A (unknown constant
kappa>0), the preceding unbiased Z is R/kappa. The gain is not an additive
zero-mean noise variance. A degree-d raw product has mean kappa^d times the
corresponding blurred moment. Keep degrees separate, cover their raw means,
and project their intervals over a simultaneously covered positive kappa region.
For the raw degree-(2j+2) contrast
S_j=h^(-2)*[-R_-R_+e_2j+b(R_-+R_+)e_(2j+1)-b^2 e_(2j+2)],
the entire contrast may first receive a mean interval and then be divided by
kappa^(2j+2); this retains its covariance cancellations. The bias correction
uses separate degree-2j means. Signed endpoints must be minimized/maximized
over kappa; division of every endpoint by kappa_L is wrong for negative means.
After these projected bounds, Sections4-5 apply conservatively. A joint region
can retain the common kappa rather than rectangularizing, improving precision.

A sufficient physical reference protocol: the same detector reads a known
constant signal1, with mean kappa, and a known step from0 to1 at time0. If a
coarse metrological exposure bracket obeys0<s<tau_min<=tau<=tau_U, a step
reading centered at time s has mean p_step=kappa*s/tau. Simultaneous bounded
reference-mean intervals give the joint restrictions

 kappa in[kappa_L,kappa_U], p_step in[p_L,p_U],
 p_step*tau=kappa*s.

When p_L>0, a conservative exposure interval is
[kappa_L*s/p_U, kappa_U*s/p_L], intersected with the coarse bracket. If p_L<=0
the upper bound may be inconclusive. Without s<tau_min the correct response is
kappa*min(1,s/tau); retain that exact relation instead of claiming a ramp.
Multiple reference times can be treated through their simultaneous region.
An empty confidence region produces an inconclusive/model-check flag, not a
claim that the physical model has zero uncertainty. Common response between
reference and target, known time origin, known step shape and independent
detector randomizations are premises requiring actual calibration access.

Cover every raw target and reference mean with one declared error allocation.
The union event is valid even when target moments share records. References
may be separate trials; adaptive design requires a separate valid confidence
argument. Optimization over the region is safe because the true parameters
are simultaneously included, not because conditional independence magically
survives an estimated plug-in. The structural Gamma identity and generator
caps remain physical assumptions; these references do not validate them.

8. FIXED RAW CONTRASTS PRESERVE COVARIANCE BEFORE GAIN PROJECTION

Choose nominal kappa0>0 as a predeclared fixed design constant, or using a pilot
independent of the inference records. Include in the original jointly
covered mean vector all S_j, required raw elementary means U_j=e_2j, the
calibration contrast V=(e2-(R_-+R_+)e1/2)/h, and
R_l=sum[j=0..l] S_j/kappa0^(2j+2), for every prefix l<=m to be reported.
Here S_j includes h^(-2), and all e_k are polynomials of raw R readouts.
The actual sample variance of R_l preserves covariances before concentration.

On the event E S_j in[L_j,U_j], E R_l>=L_Rl, define
I_j=[kappa_U^(-d_j)-kappa0^(-d_j),
     kappa_L^(-d_j)-kappa0^(-d_j)], d_j=2j+2,
c_j=min of the four products of interval endpoints I_j and[L_j,U_j].
The exact identity
sum E S_j/kappa^d_j = E R_l +sum(kappa^(-d_j)-kappa0^(-d_j))*E S_j
shows the left side is at least L_Rl+sum c_j for every covered kappa.
Subtract beta times the covered upper weighted U_j sum and deltaN_l to bound
P_l. Maximize with the old componentwise bound and over all covered prefixes.
No further alpha is required AFTER the common vector (including R_l) has been
covered. One cannot add an uncovered R_l after looking at the data for free.
Each prefix remains unbiased using the full K=2m+2 replica elementary average.
This is standard fixed-contrast concentration plus interval projection, not a
new confidence inequality.

9. ENDPOINT ENTROPY BASELINE UNDER IDENTICAL DETECTOR ACCESS

Assume 0<=b<=1 and let s be a covered lower bound on
N0=[2b C(h)-C(2h)-b^2 C0]/h^2, after gain correction and RAW order-zero blur
correction, without the derivative-score beta penalty. Then

    sigma >= 2*h^3*[s]_+^2/(2b+sqrt(2))^2.

This baseline needs ordinary stationary diffusion and the calibrated detector,
but not the pointwise Gamma shape. Proof: compare L to its reversible part S,
with identical stationary pi, diffusionD and f. Girsanov gives path KL on[0,t]
=sigma*t/4. Pinsker plus bounded endpoint product f0*ft gives
|C_L(t)-C_S(t)|<=sqrt(sigma*t/2). Zero-lag C0 is identical, and
2b C_S(h)-C_S(2h)-b^2 C0 =-||(exp(hS)-bI)f||_pi^2<=0.
Therefore h^2[N0]+ <=(2b+sqrt2)*sqrt(sigma*h/2), giving the claim. This is
the prior ci_joint_calibration reversible-support/entropy-bridge construction
in C rather than binary-agreement normalization. The denominator requires an
upper outward enclosure if computed finitely. Exposure must be corrected
before claiming the common ideal zero-lag cancellation.

10. SHARP HIDDEN STOCHASTIC-DRIVE FAMILY

Let Z be any reversible smooth elliptic diffusion on a compact connected
manifold without boundary, generator L_Z, positive stationary density pi_Z.
On circle x Z let
L=gamma*partial_phi^2+c(z)*partial_phi+L_Z, gamma>0, c smooth real.
The stationary law is uniform_phi x pi_Z. ALL coordinates are subject to
ordinary/even hidden-state reversal. D is block diagonal, so
S=gamma*partial_phi^2+L_Z, A=c(z)*partial_phi,
v=(c(z),0), sigma=E_piZ[c^2]/gamma.

For f=cos(phi): Gamma_f=gamma*(1-f^2), Sf=-gamma*f,
Af=-c(z)*sin(phi). At stationarity phi andZ are independent, U=(f,Gamma_f)
is a function of Y=f, and the population conditional quantities are
g(Y)=-gamma*Y,
K(Y)=gamma^2*Y^2-E[c^2]*(1-Y^2).
Hence the observed-geometry lower functional is exactly E[c^2]/gamma;
the endpoints Y=+-1 have zero stationary mass. Because that functional is
fixed by the ideal continuous Y law, it lower-bounds every compatible model
in the prior ordinary compact hidden-diffusion class. This joint diffusion
attains it. Thus the global compatible-law minimum is exactly E[c^2]/gamma,
not only a lower bound under a circle-fit parameterization.

The full Y law is reversible: reflection R(phi,z)=(-phi,z) preserves the
stationary law, fixes f, and conjugates L to L^dagger. Reflection of the
stationary reversed hidden process has the forward hidden law and the same
detector values, proving equality of all forward/reversed Y distributions.

The ideal hierarchy formula is exactly(6.1) with omega^2 replaced by E c^2.
Thus the finite consistency construction extends provided the same valid
generator caps and detector-access assumptions are supplied. The same
sufficient acquisition and precision schedule applies under valid uniform
generator and positive-gain envelopes: the stationary arcsine marginal and
ideal hierarchy formula are unchanged, while the finite-lag and exposure
bounds already hold for the general hidden class. Explicitly,
Lf=-gamma cosphi-c sinphi,
L^2f=(gamma^2-c^2)cosphi+(2gamma*c-L_Zc)sinphi;
adjoint formulas reverse the sine term. With C=||c||inf andG=||L_Zc||inf,
both M1 norms equal sqrt(gamma^2+C^2), both M2 norms equal
sup_z sqrt((gamma^2-c(z)^2)^2+(2gamma*c(z)-L_Zc(z))^2).
Simple safe choices are M1=gamma+C, M2=(gamma+C)^2+G. Parameter uncertainty
requires valid upper envelopes, not substitution of fitted hidden parameters.

Example: Z is a second circle, L_Z=eta*partial_z^2, eta>0,
c(z)=epsilon*cosz. Then E c=0, E c^2=epsilon^2/2, and exact sigma is
epsilon^2/(2gamma). Both first caps equal sqrt(gamma^2+epsilon^2);
both second caps equal sqrt((gamma^2-epsilon^2)^2+(2gamma+eta)^2*epsilon^2).
The second expression maximizes at c^2=epsilon^2 because its square is
gamma^4+(2gamma^2+4gamma*eta+eta^2)c^2+c^4, increasing on c^2>=0.
There is no mean angular current, but nonzero conditional currents and a
positive ordinary-reversal entropy minimum remain for epsilon!=0. This is
an analytically validated family, not another numerical or physical experiment.
An odd-variable physical reversal changes the target and is excluded.

11. FAILURE CASES AND POSITIONING

Unknown unrestricted hidden diffusivity cannot be calibrated by this theorem.
Rare-state countermodels can preserve almost all finite records while making
global generator and diffusivity suprema arbitrarily large. The calibration
step does not turn E[Gamma|Y] into a pointwise upper envelope. A false structural
identity, uncalibrated memory/correlated shot noise, a changed reference response,
nonstationary target preparation, or uncharged dependence invalidates the
certificate. Replica inference, bounded-mean confidence, moment-region inversion,
and semigroup/Dirichlet identities are established methods. The narrower result
here is their explicit propagation through the structural diffusion entropy
score with a simultaneous nuisance guarantee and a sharp limiting hierarchy.
