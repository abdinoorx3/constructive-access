1. Physical simulator and retained records
Use the stationary circle dphi=omega dt+sqrt(2gamma)dW with uniform phase.
Three exposure windows begin at 0,h,2h and have common width 0<tau<=h.
For window j, A_j=tau^-1 integral_window_j cos(phi_t)dt.
Every binary replica R lies in {-1,+1}, with conditional mean kappa*A_j. All replicas
are independent conditional on the complete latent path. This is a bounded
stochastic detector with Var(R|path)=1-kappa²*A_j². The simulator declares gain
0<=kappa<=1. Inference treats the common positive gain as unknown and uses
additional constant and timed-step reference records to cover it. An unknown
offset, unequal detector gains or correlated replicas are not covered.

The two endpoint windows have one replica each. The center has 2m+2 replicas.
m0 gives 4 bits/16 outcomes; m1 gives 6 bits/64 outcomes; m2 gives 8 bits/256
outcomes. The m2 moment degree is 8, not 6. Exchangeable center permutations
can instead be collapsed to (left sign,center plus count,right sign), giving
12,20,28 categories. These counts preserve the complete sufficient statistics
of the fixed symmetric polynomial score. The ordered-law option remains for
independent checks, but an arbitrary later labeled-replica score cannot be
recovered from collapsed counts.

For an ordered record r with D=2m+4 bits,
 P(r)=2^-D E product_i(1+kappa*r_i A_window(i)).
This is a finite polynomial in exposure moments, so an exact finite moment
calculation suffices. Brownian path discretization is unnecessary.

2. Exact finite Fourier/additive-functional hierarchy
During window j let I_j accumulate integral cos(phi_t)dt. Define
 C[a,k]=E[I_left^a_left I_center^a_center I_right^a_right cos(k phi_t)],
 S[a,k]=the corresponding sine moment.
The stationary initial condition has C[0,0]=1 and all other needed entries 0.
On an active window,
 dC[a,k]/dt=-gamma k² C[a,k]-omega k S[a,k]
             +(a_j/2)(C[a-e_j,k+1]+C[a-e_j,k-1]),
 dS[a,k]/dt=-gamma k² S[a,k]+omega k C[a,k]
             +(a_j/2)(S[a-e_j,k+1]+S[a-e_j,k-1]).
Cosine is even in its mode; sine is odd; S[a,0]=0. In a free gap the last
terms vanish. To recover final mode-zero moments through total degree D,
only |k|+sum(a)<=D is needed. Every coupling lowers polynomial degree and
shifts mode by one, so this cone closes exactly for the requested targets.
The real dimensions for m0,m1,m2 are 60,140,252. Normalize final moments by
tau^sum(a). All ODE coefficients and input durations are rational when the
simulator parameters are rational.

3. Rigorous numerical enclosure
The code applies rational Taylor polynomials to each exposure and free-gap
semigroup. For x=t||G||infinity, exp(x)<=3^ceil(x). An Nth-degree action
has infinity-norm remainder at most
 ||v||infinity *3^ceil(x)*x^(N+1)/(N+1)!.
Existing input error is multiplied by the same exponential norm bound.
The final moment radii account for normalization by tau^degree. Polynomial
coefficient absolute sums propagate those radii into event-probability errors.
This is an enclosure of the exact circle model, not a finite path-mesh model.
The current exploratory cap is 512 Taylor terms, with explicit failure above it.

Exact event probability centers sum to one. They are converted to a nonnegative
63-bit dyadic law summing exactly to one; its distance to the ideal law is bounded
by half the sum of the absolute center-to-dyadic displacement plus probability
radii. For n independent records, the total-variation discrepancy is at most
min(1,n*delta_law). Any simulation coverage comparison must include this charge
rather than treat the nominal dyadic simulator as the exact real-valued model.

The categorical sampler draws uniform 63-bit integers, searches an integer CDF,
and accumulates counts. There is no floating-probability threshold. Uniform
independent random bits remain the ideal sampling assumption; NumPy PCG64 is
its practical pseudorandom implementation. This does not prove literal ideal
randomness for a deterministic PRNG.

5. Physically counted resources
One record uses one independent stationary preparation, three exposure windows,
D binary detector readouts, and modeled evolution duration 2h+tau. Replicas do
not count as free readings. A passive batch costs n times those quantities.
Preparation/reset duration, shutters, calibrated physical clock, replica
construction, energy and geometry calibration are additional unmeasured costs.
Unknown actual exposure must be carried in the inference region or bounded
through a proved exposure correction. The simulator's true tau is not an
inference input merely because it is known to the producer.

6. Covariance/blur baseline and structural distinction
Gain-normalized independent twins expose M0=E[A_center²]. Cross-window products expose
M1=E[A_left A_center] and M2=E[A_left A_right]. Every raw pair product has a
factor kappa². Let z=gamma-i omega. Exact
stationary circle formulas are
 M0=Re[tau/z-(1-exp(-z tau))/z²]/tau²,
 C_A(ell)=Re[exp(-z ell)(exp(z tau)+exp(-z tau)-2)/(z² tau²)]/2,
 for nonoverlapping windows ell>=tau.
Thus a model-aware covariance baseline should use these blur formulas and the
same twin/lag observations, not naive realized variance or an uncorrected
point-sample fit. A jointly covered nuisance set can be sought for bounded
(gamma,|omega|,tau,kappa), using simultaneous moment confidence constraints; aliases
and nonidentifiability must be retained rather than hidden by a point fit.

This baseline assumes the complete circle covariance family. The intended
new theorem instead permits arbitrary hidden dynamics satisfying the structural
Gamma_f=gamma(1-f²) relation plus its calibrated derivative bounds. The two
model classes are different; circle-fit precision must not be claimed as a
broad-class guarantee. A general endpoint certificate at the same detector
access is an additional relevant comparator. Root/theory own the final
comparison and inference definitions.

7. Fixed symmetric score and controlled references
For r=2m+2 center replicas and plus count k, e_j is the mean product over all
j-element subsets of the r replicas, with e_0=1. Its exact formula is
 e_j=sum_l (-1)^(j-l) choose(k,l)choose(r-k,j-l)/choose(r,j).
Set b=1-lambda*h and retain raw statistics
 S_j=[-Z_left*Z_right*e_(2j)+b*(Z_left+Z_right)*e_(2j+1)
      -b²*e_(2j+2)]/h², j=0,...,m,
 U_j=e_(2j), j=1,...,max(m,1),
 V=[e_2-(Z_left+Z_right)*e_1/2]/h.
The three terms within each S_j have the same gain degree 2j+2. V has degree2.
This preserves cancellation before calibration uncertainty is propagated.
All estimates and confidence rules are predeclared; endpoint signs remain
ordered. Conditional replica independence gives the moment identities even
though all replicas are correlated after marginalizing the latent path.

A controlled constant reference f=1 has binary mean kappa. A calibrated
0-to-1 step leaving s units at1 inside the causal boxcar has mean kappa*s/tau
when 0<s<tau. Both reference batches cost their own detector readings and
controlled preparations, with independent fixed-quota records. They assume
the same gain and width as the target detector and no unknown offset.
The reference operations are additional apparatus access; they are not
available automatically from stationary target observations.
