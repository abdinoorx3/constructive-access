OBSERVED DIFFUSION GEOMETRY: PRIMARY-SOURCE AUDIT
Audit: 2026-10-12 UTC. Owner: geometry_literature.
Status: internal literature assessment; no historical-priority certification.

Finding
-------
The proposed weighted conditional local-defect inequality belongs to an
established family of kinetic/variance-sum-rule methods. It must not be
introduced as the first way of extracting dissipation from time-symmetric
fluctuations, the first use of local diffusion geometry, or the first minimum
over hidden models compatible with observations. Those claims are precluded
by the sources below. The closest equation-level predecessor is Di Terlizzi's
2025 force-free kinetic inference paper, not only the 2024 variance sum rule.

The defensible proposed addition is more specific: a conditional, locally
weighted short-time inequality for a nonlinear scalar detector, explicit
retention of pathwise quadratic variation, and a sharp minimum over all
compatible finite-dimensional smooth ordinary-reversal hidden diffusions for
the complete cosine-circle path law. None of the inspected primary results
states that whole assertion. Search absence is not a proof of originality.
The population theorem, its technical access contract and finite-resolution
implementation need their own mathematical review.

1. Closest identity: force-free kinetic inference
------------------------------------------------
I. Di Terlizzi, Force-Free Kinetic Inference of Entropy Production, Physical
Review Letters 135, 237101 (2025), DOI 10.1103/fsph-437v. Primary full text:
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.07540v2 (23 November 2025 version).
Main Eqs. (5), (15)-(18); Supplement S1, Eqs. (S12)-(S16), S4, S11-S12.

For stationary Cartesian overdamped coordinates with constant diffusion matrix,
Eq. (5) writes entropy as minus diffusion-weighted position-covariance curvature
plus an effective-force/score covariance. For diagonal diffusion, Eq. (16)
decomposes each coordinate contribution as

 sigma_i = -C_i''(0)/D_i + D_i E[(partial_i log pi)^2].

Its Eq. (18) retains positive traffic terms under partial observation. Thus
kinetic inference from a single scalar trace, including reversible scalar
Gaussian traces, is already explicit prior work. The paper's statement that
the full inflow cannot be inferred from a marginal does not preclude a lower
bound on that inflow.

Immediate corollary, not a separate invention: with smooth density p_i(y),
appropriate decay/boundary conditions and constant diagonal diffusion,
E[partial_i log pi | X_i=y] = partial_y log p_i(y). Conditional Jensen gives

 sigma_i >= -C_i''(0)/D_i + D_i E[(partial_y log p_i(X_i))^2].

This is the unweighted, signed, constant-D specialization of our conditional
construction. Any manuscript should show this reduction and explicitly call
it a corollary of the established identity plus conditional Jensen. The
positive part before averaging can retain more information, but it does not
make the underlying curvature/score identity new.

What remains different in the proposed theorem: arbitrary smooth scalar
f(X), state-dependent/correlated hidden diffusion, conditioning on
V=Gamma_f(X), a pointwise cancellation before weighting by 1/V, and a full-law
compatibility argument independent of hidden dimension. In the circle,
E[g(Y)^2/V] diverges near Y=+/-1, as does the corresponding piece of E[K(Y)/V];
the combined (g^2-K)/V is finite. Do not split that expression into a
difference of separately finite global averages. A transformation arccos(Y)
creates folding endpoints/local-time issues, so merely applying a globally
constant-D coordinate formula to that nonsmooth transformed detector is not
an adequate proof.

S11 uses fitted sums of exponentials for correlation derivatives and a KDE
for effective forces. S12 studies additive white/short-correlated measurement
noise and excludes contaminated small lags before fitting. These are useful
practical baselines. They do not establish a uniform semigroup-remainder
certificate for every hidden model in our class; their numerical error bars
must not be equated to a finite-sample lower confidence theorem.

2. Variance sum rule and Harada-Sasa
-----------------------------------
Di Terlizzi et al., Science 383, 971-976 (2024), DOI 10.1126/science.adh1823,
arXiv:2302.08565. Main VSR/entropy relations and Supplement S1-S2 inspected.
Di Terlizzi, Baiesi and Ritort, New Journal of Physics 26, 063013 (2024),
DOI 10.1088/1367-2630/ad4fb9, arXiv:2403.10442. Sections 2-3, particularly
Sec. 3.3 Eq. (30), inspected. These relate displacement curvature, force
variance and dissipation in overdamped Langevin systems. Scalar symmetric
fluctuations as thermodynamic evidence are established. The present detector
geometry and conditional cancellation should be positioned within that line.

Harada and Sasa, Physical Review Letters 95, 130602 (2005),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.130602, arXiv:cond-mat/0502505.
Eq. (5) relates mean heat dissipation to a velocity-correlation spectrum minus
the dissipative response spectrum, with a mean-velocity contribution. Response
to a perturbing force is part of its access. The proposed local conditional
statistics are passive under their stated detector assumptions; this changes
the access contract, not the legitimacy of the older equality. It would be
incorrect to claim that Harada-Sasa cannot quantify hidden dissipation in the
settings where its response and force/mobility conditions are available.

3. Reversal, geometry and short-time inference
---------------------------------------------
Da Costa and Pavliotis, Journal of Physics A 56, 365001 (2023),
DOI 10.1088/1751-8121/acdf98, arXiv:2212.05125.
Proposition 3.7 gives the reversible/irreversible drift decomposition;
Proposition 3.12 its symmetric/antisymmetric generator form; Theorem 4.1 the
stationary entropy integral in the regular case; Section 6.2 Eq. (34) the
distinct generalized reversal involving a phase-space involution.
The assumed sigma=E[v^T D^-1 v] and L=S+A are standard. The odd-variable
exclusion is substantive: equilibrium underdamped systems have zero
appropriately reversed physical entropy despite ordinary reversal behaving
differently. An even scalar detector does not certify that all hidden
variables have even parity.

Otsubo, Ito, Dechant and Sagawa, Physical Review E 101, 062106 (2020),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.101.062106, arXiv:2001.07460.
Sec. II Eqs. (23)-(30) proves short-time current TURs and their optimal-current
saturation for Langevin dynamics; Sec. III and Appendix A.2 cover estimators
and data splitting. Optimizing weighted short-time statistics or using a
training/evaluation split is not new. Their current objective uses observable
time-directed currents. A reversible scalar law makes those means vanish;
our symmetric conditional defect addresses different retained information
within a restricted hidden-model class.

Frishman and Ronceray, Physical Review X 10, 021009 (2020),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.10.021009, arXiv:1809.09650.
Sec. II Eqs. (6)-(8) develops finite-basis force/current projection; Sec. III
Eqs. (10)-(15) treats spatially varying diffusion; Appendix E Eq. (91) gives
hidden-variable conditional averaging; Appendices F-G study noise and finite
time steps. Conditional force regression, inference of diffusion from
increments, and projected-current entropy bounds are established. Appendix E
explicitly limits the inferred entropy to observable currents. In particular,
regressing squared increments on Y recovers conditional average diffusivity,
not necessarily the instantaneous pathwise V=Gamma_f(X) needed for division
inside the new conditional theorem. This distinction is physical and
statistical, not a change of notation.

Hyvarinen, Journal of Machine Learning Research 6, 695-709 (2005),
https://jmlr.org/papers/v6/hyvarinen05a.html.
Theorem 1, Eq. (3), derives the score-matching integration-by-parts objective;
Eq. (4) is its sample version. A completed-square variational representation
2qg-q^2 <= g^2 is elementary least-squares duality, and should not be advertised
as a new operator. Distinguish ordinary density-score matching from the
present regression test function q for the symmetric conditional drift.
Any new statistical contribution must lie in a valid bias/concentration
contract for the particular triple statistic and physical calibration.

4. Minimum compatible dissipation is an existing research objective
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Ehrich, Journal of Statistical Mechanics 083214 (2021),
DOI 10.1088/1742-5468/ac150e, arXiv:2105.08803.
Sec. 4 Eq. (51) minimizes entropy over hidden transition matrices fitting the
observed jump statistics; Eq. (52) compares this with time-series
irreversibility. Sec. 5 explicitly discusses full observable statistics,
dependence on known hidden-state count and the unresolved effect of enlarging
the hidden model. Thus full observed-law compatibility can constrain entropy
more strongly than reversed-record KL; that principle is established.

Nitzan, Ghosal and Bisker, Physical Review Research 5, 043251 (2023),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043251, arXiv:2212.01783.
Sec. III.1.2 Eqs. (16)-(18) gives full waiting-time-law compatibility and a
moment hierarchy for minimum entropy, assuming the same underlying topology.
Sec. III.4 and the conclusion test simpler topologies numerically and retain
the universal topology-reduction statement as open. These are clear prior
examples of the compatible-model optimization objective. Our proposed
cosine-law solution would close a different, broad diffusion-class instance,
not create this objective for the first time.

Lucente et al., Physical Review Research 4, 043103 (2022),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.043103, arXiv:2205.08961.
Sec. III explains the scalar Gaussian time-reversal obstruction and the role
of an equilibrium generalized Langevin realization. The present cosine trace
is not Gaussian, and its compatible class explicitly excludes generalized
or odd-variable reversal. Do not summarize the older no-go as a claim that
every reversible scalar path law has a reversible ordinary overdamped hidden
diffusion realization. Reversibility of an observed law and existence of a
reversible generator in a specified realization class are distinct statements.

5. Additional current search result
-----------------------------------
Cross-Spectral Witness for Hidden Nonequilibrium Beyond the Scalar Ceiling,
arXiv:2604.03775, version dated 24 August 2026, inspected as an unrefereed
preprint. Theorems 1-2 and Sec. VI/Appendix K use two-channel coherence under a
channel-separable linear hidden-sector model; the entropy bridge additionally
assumes one-way coupling. This is a different access/realization class. Shared
cross-channel dependence is not automatically physical dissipation. No result
from that preprint is used as a premise of our theorem.

6. Recommended claim wording and remaining tests
------------------------------------------------
Use: 'A conditional diffusion-geometry bound, together with a sharp
compatible-realization result for the complete cosine-circle observed law.'
Avoid: 'universal entropy reconstruction', 'new physical law', 'first inference
from reversible fluctuations', 'complete hidden dynamics recovery', or
'laboratory-ready model-free measurement'.

The practical addition must verify: exact continuous-path versus finite-time
access; a calibrated V or pointwise upper envelope; simultaneous finite-h
bias for g and K; sensor-noise/exposure handling; statistical coverage after
selection; and physical clock/acquisition cost. A frozen linear variational
score avoids a biased plug-in square but does not remove these obligations.
Finite-h remainder bounds based on generator derivatives are conditional
calibration assumptions, not quantities automatically identified by the same
coarse observations. A model-assisted oracle should not be described as a
data-only universal certificate.

Search method and limits
------------------------
Focused web searches covered conditional/local entropy, quadratic variation,
short-time drift/current inference, variance sum rules, hidden-model minima,
and related recent work. Primary HTML was downloaded to temporary storage and
math alttext inspected; the score-matching PDF was read as text. DOI metadata
for the new journal references was checked against Crossref. Bibliography
keys were checked against the seventh cumulative edition. Full texts are not
redistributed. sources.json records reviewed files, SHA-256, source size,
precise pointers and access level. No search can exclude all earlier work,
and internal audit does not substitute for expert external review.
