1. Precise scope

smooth positive invariant density pi, smooth uniformly elliptic D, smooth v with
div(pi v)=0. Generator L=S+A, S=pi^-1 div(pi D grad), A=v dot grad. Smooth

Entropy convention sigma=E[v^T D^-1 v], local noise covariance 2D dt.

2. The proof
V=Gamma_f(X0), U=(f(X0),V). Quadratic variation [Y]'=2V makes this an ideal
path-law observable. Conditional on X0, past/future independent. Smooth compact
semigroup expansions in supremum norm give
 g=lim E[(Yh+Y-h-2Y0)/(2h)|U]=E[Sf|U]
 K=lim E[(Yh-Y0)(Y-h-Y0)/h²|U]=E[(Sf)²-(Af)²|U].
Limits exist in L-infinity; no observed-Markov assumption. Put a=Sf,c=Af.
 d=g²-K=E[c²|U]-Var(a|U).
Then d_+<=E[c²|U]<=V E[e|U]. Truncate V>=epsilon, divide/integrate, and
monotone convergence give I(Y)=E[1_{V>0}d_+/V]<=sigma. On V=0, gradf=0,
c=0, and d_+=0. This is a zero numerator convention, not division by zero.

Path-law invariance is rigorous: dyadic squared-increment sums converge to
quadratic variation uniformly a.s. for the current bounded-coefficient Itô
semimartingale (L2 supremum error O(mesh), dyadic meshes summable). Its derivative
is continuous and gives V. Thus common Y law fixes (U,Y-h,Y0,Yh), conditional
laws modulo null sets, uniform limits, and the integrated truncated limit.
Finite-window V estimates do not inherit this exact argument automatically.

3. Exact slack/equality
Let m=E[c²|U],w=Var(a|U). Then
 sigma-I = E[e;V=0]+E[e-c²/V;V>0]+E[min(m,w)/V;V>0].
Every term nonnegative finite. Equality iff e=0 on V=0, v proportional to
D gradf on V>0, and on every positive-V observation fiber either m=0 or w=0.
The last condition isolates reversible-drift heterogeneity as an information
loss, while the metric term isolates dissipation in unobserved directions.

4. Attained global compatible-model minimum
Fix complete observed process law of Y=cosphi with dphi=omega dt+sqrt(2gamma)dW,
uniform phase and gamma>0. For circle,
 V=gamma(1-Y²), g=-gammaY,
 K=gamma²Y²-omega²(1-Y²).
Thus d_+/V=omega²/gamma a.s. (endpoints Y=±1 have zero probability).
Every compatible admissible hidden diffusion, independent of finite hidden
dimension/topology, has sigma>=omega²/gamma. Circle is feasible and has this
entropy, hence the minimum is attained and equals omega²/gamma.
This minimum concerns COMPLETE path law, not covariance, finitely sampled data,
or arbitrary generalized-reversal physical models.
The scalar path law itself is reversible: reversal changes omega to -omega;
phase reflection does the same while preserving cos. Observed-path KL is zero.
Positive minimum comes from admissible hidden diffusion geometry, so it does
not contradict data processing or claim visible time asymmetry.

5. Scalar recalibration invariance
For smooth invertible z=psi(y), psi' nonzero, U_z and U_y determine each other.
With p=psi',r=psi'', Vz=p²V, az=pa+rV, cz=pc.
gz=pg+rV, Kz=p²K+2prVg+r²V², hence dz=p²d and I(Z)=I(Y).
Fixed numerical truncations need not match after change of diffusivity units.
Noninjective recalibration and singular inverse not claimed invariant.

6. Refining access and exact variational dual
If instantaneous conditioning fields G⊂H both include V, then
 d_G=E[d_H|G]-Var(E[a|H]|G), so I_G<=I_H<=sigma by convexity of positive part.
This is a population comparison; estimating finer conditioning costs data.
Exact dual:
 I=sup E[w(U)(2q(U)g(U)-q(U)²-K(U))],
 bounded measurable q,w, w>=0,wV<=1.
Take q=g and w=1_{V>=epsilon,d>0}/V for attainment in increasing limits.
Root has a finite-resolution construction for frozen q,w; selecting q,w on
inference data is not licensed by this population optimization identity.

11. Root-requested extension, independently proved
Let rho(phi)>0 and D(phi)>0 smooth, even and periodic on circle, rho normalized
against dphi. S=rho^-1 dphi(rho D dphi), v=J/rho, A=v dphi. For f=cosphi,
Sf,V=Dsin²phi,e=J²/(rho²D) are all even and hence measurable functions of Y.
Conditional Var(Sf|Y,V)=0, and in one dimension metric Cauchy is equality away
from detector critical points (zero probability). Therefore I=sigma exactly,
with sigma=J² integral dphi/(rho D). Every other admissible hidden diffusion
with that full observed process law has sigma at least this value, and the
reference circle attains it. Reflection preserves rho, commutes with S,
changes A sign and leaves f fixed: all these scalar observed laws reversible.
Thus nonzero global minimum among ordinary overdamped hidden models persists
for a full reflection-symmetric family, not just constant-speed Brownian phase.
Uniform rho=1/(2pi),D=gamma,J=omega/(2pi) recovers omega²/gamma exactly.

12. Essential integration order
For cosine circle, E[g²/V] and E[K/V] separately diverge. The finite object is
the conditional pointwise difference d=g²-K, then positive part, then weighted
integral with truncation before the limit. Never subtract divergent integrals.
This warning has been added explicitly to the manuscript.

13. Stronger compatibility statement
The attained minimum remains true if compatibility specifies only the
stationary law of U=(Y,V) and the local conditional functions g,K, rather
than the whole scalar process law. Every such model shares I, and the sharp
reference model is still feasible. This larger compatible class is a useful
statement of exactly which observable population data fix the minimum.
Those functions are not a finite set of low-resolution correlations.
