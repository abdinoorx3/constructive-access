Frozen quantization
Readings round to j/2^20, delta=2^-20 (half-grid extra evaluation allowance).
The rounded-center rule is genuinely the predetermined state function:
q=-j0/d; retain 5|j0|<=4d; a=1-max(|j0|/d-delta,0)^2.
This envelope rigorously bounds true Gamma=1-Y0² under the reading-error
contract. Using merely a=1-(j0/d)^2 could fail and is not done.
Exact score formula and its Fraction checks are in code/experiment.py and
tests/test_experiment.py. Primary floors to1/256, baseline to2^-24.
Score uniform error 20483/1179648 = 0.0173636542426;
endpoint uniform error 77457/10485760000 = 7.38687515259e-06.
Ranges are expanded and the error is subtracted after concentration.
Exact integer aggregate sums and sumsquares; safe chunking avoids overflow.
NumPy sampler/transcendentals remain numerical approximations to the ideal
uniform/Brownian law. Exact scoring does not certify exact real-valued sampling.

Resource and interpretation
Each method uses the same 600000 readings, 200000 stationary preparations,
16000 modeled evolution time per dataset. Bank: 240 million triples.
Calibration, preparation/reset time, detector exposure, energy, clock and
physical heat are unmeasured. The gain uses additional calibrated geometry
and derivative bounds; it is not universal same-prior-information dominance.
Weak drive failure decisively refutes a general dominance claim.
This finite-data result approaches 33% of exact ideal minimum 9 in the tested
strong-drive case. It does not recover the ideal minimum or prove optimality.
