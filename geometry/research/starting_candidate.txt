1. Bottleneck
The endpoint defect gives a dimension-independent physical lower bound, but its
sharp discrete kernel is not CTMC-embeddable. The exact circulant minimum only
closes a declared architecture; the five-state hub countermodel proves that a
complete scalar covariance does not enforce that architecture. Another generic
endpoint divergence optimization will not by itself remove this mismatch.

The highest-leverage extra access may be a state-resolved quadratic variation
and a conditional two-sided short-time correlation. This changes the observed
information and can retain the local diffusion geometry lost by a global range
bound. It is a more concrete next dependency than claiming universal embedding
sharpness from existing moments.

2. Proposed small theorem to prove/adversarially audit
Assume an ordinary-reversal stationary overdamped diffusion on a compact smooth
periodic domain, with smooth coefficients, positive stationary density pi and
uniformly positive diffusion matrix D. Let L=S+A in L2(pi), where
 S = pi^-1 div(pi D grad), A=v dot grad,
 sigma=E_pi[v^T D^-1 v].
Let Y=f(X) for smooth real f. Crucially require a justified POINTWISE closure
 Gamma_f(x)=grad f(x)^T D(x) grad f(x)=a(f(x)),
not merely the identity E[Gamma_f|Y=y]=a(y), which holds by definition for a
conditional average and is insufficient for the argument.

Define observable conditional symmetric drift and past/future product
 g(y)=lim_{h down 0} E[Y_h+Y_-h-2Y_0 | Y_0=y]/(2h),
 K(y)=lim_{h down 0} E[(Y_h-Y_0)(Y_-h-Y_0) | Y_0=y]/h^2.
Candidate bound:
 sigma >= E[ (g(Y)^2-K(Y))_+ / a(Y) ].
Initially prove its truncated version on {a(Y)>=epsilon}, epsilon>0. This
avoids dividing by a vanishing local diffusion coefficient. Give a separate
justification for taking epsilon down to zero.

Population argument to audit, not a priority claim:
- Conditional on complete X_0, stationary past and future are independent.
- The two-sided product tends to (Lf)(Ldagger f)=(Sf)^2-(Af)^2.
- Hence K(y)=E[(Sf)^2-(Af)^2 | Y=y], and g(y)=E[Sf | Y=y].
- Conditional Jensen gives g(y)^2-K(y)<=E[(Af)^2|Y=y]; nonnegativity permits
  taking the positive part on the left.
- Pointwise Cauchy gives (Af)^2<=Gamma_f v^T D^-1 v.
- The pointwise closure allows division by the same observable a(Y), followed
  by conditional expectation and integration.
Root independently checked this population chain. That internal check is not
an independent prior-art audit, finite-sample theorem, or release result.

3. Exact first experiment — analytic before simulation
Use the stationary uniform circle
 d phi=omega dt+sqrt(2 gamma)dW mod 2pi, Y=cos phi.
Here
 a(y)=gamma(1-y^2), g(y)=-gamma y,
 K(y)=gamma^2 y^2-omega^2(1-y^2).
The proposed population lower bound equals omega^2/gamma, exactly the circle's
physical entropy. Thus it closes the endpoint gap in a concrete continuous-time
model without asserting a universal covariance-only minimum.

For finite h, set r=exp(-gamma h), c=cos(omega h), s=sin(omega h). Exact formulas:
 g_h(y)=(r c-1)y/h,
 K_h(y)=[(r c-1)^2 y^2-r^2 s^2(1-y^2)]/h^2.
Therefore the untruncated plug-in population expression is
 exp(-2 gamma h) sin^2(omega h)/(gamma h^2),
which approaches omega^2/gamma from below in this circle model. A future test
should derive these independently from the transition semigroup, verify the
omega=0 negative control, and retain truncation near |y|=1. Restricting to
|y|<=1-epsilon multiplies the limiting circle value by the exactly known
stationary fraction 1-(2/pi) arccos(1-epsilon).

Do not assume the finite-h expression is a valid lower bound in all diffusions.
A general finite-h bias enclosure is an additional theorem to prove, perhaps
under explicit generator derivative bounds. Derivative access is stronger than
three endpoint moments at one fixed lag.

4. Disproof and failure conditions
- Replace pointwise Gamma closure by its conditional mean and construct a hidden
  diffusivity mixture correlated with Af; the candidate may overestimate sigma.
  This is the first adversarial architecture test.
- Add independent detector noise, exposure averaging, or detector memory.
  Raw quadratic variation/drift estimates then generally violate the contract.
- Check generalized/odd-variable reversal separately. Equilibrium underdamped
  oscillations must not be presented as physical dissipation evidence.
- Construct smooth noncircle models with significant finite-h bias; reject any
  uncorrected finite-h generalization if it crosses their known entropy.
- Even under valid closure, the numerator and denominator are estimated. A
  confidence interval for an averaged variance does not establish the needed
  pointwise upper or equality. A practically usable theorem needs an auditable
  closure assumption or a genuinely stronger measurement/calibration protocol.
- Short-time products are statistically expensive: increments are O(sqrt(h)),
  their product is O(h), and division by h^2 amplifies fluctuations. Sensor
  precision, exposure, sample dependence, truncation, and full elapsed time must
  enter the eventual acquisition comparison. Formal derivative recovery is not
  yet an experimental advantage.

5. Prior-art audit required before promotion
Compare precisely against variance-sum-rule work (Di Terlizzi et al. 2024 and
its proof), Harada-Sasa relations, local/conditional short-time entropy inference,
projected diffusion/carre-du-champ identities, and conditional acceleration or
force reconstruction. Cauchy/Jensen and two-sided Markov factorization are
standard ingredients. The potentially useful contribution would be a correct
access contract, a sharp realizable case, finite-error implementation, and a
matched-resource gain, not invented novelty for those ingredients.

6. Next acceptance gate
First: complete the population proof with measurable conditioning and truncated
limits; either disprove or delimit the conditional-average substitution.
Second: derive a finite-h bias envelope and a sensor-noise contract.
Third: only then freeze a cost-matched experiment against the seventh-edition
endpoint certificate on the exact circle and a valid noncircle example.
If the required pointwise calibration is experimentally inaccessible, pivot to
an inequality with a directly calibratable upper envelope for Gamma_f or to an
explicit access limitation; do not silently replace it with an average.

7a. A pointwise upper envelope suffices
The equality Gamma_f(x)=a(f(x)) assumed above can be weakened to a justified
pointwise bound Gamma_f(x)<=a(f(x)). Indeed, writing
 e(x)=v(x)^T D(x)^-1 v(x)>=0,
one has (Af)^2<=Gamma_f e<=a(Y)e. Therefore the same conditional-Jensen
argument gives sigma>=E[(g(Y)^2-K(Y))_+/a(Y)], first on {a(Y)>=epsilon}.
A conditional average of Gamma is still insufficient: it need not dominate
Gamma pointwise and can be correlated with the hidden e or Af.
Thus equality was a convenient sufficient contract, not a necessary one.

7b. Measure local quadratic variation as an additional coordinate
In the ideal continuous observation model, define V=Gamma_f(X_0) and retain
U=(Y_0,V). Keep the SAME symmetric conditional drift and two-sided product,
now conditioned on U:
 g(U)=lim_{h down 0} E[Y_h+Y_-h-2Y_0 | U]/(2h),
 K(U)=lim_{h down 0} E[(Y_h-Y_0)(Y_-h-Y_0) | U]/h^2.
Then the candidate is
 sigma >= E[ 1_{V>=epsilon} (g(U)^2-K(U))_+ / V ], epsilon>0.
This no longer requires Gamma to be a function of Y alone.

Audited population chain:
1. U is a deterministic function of X_0. Conditional past/future independence
   given the complete X_0 is unchanged by subsequently conditioning on U.
2. Smooth compact-domain semigroup expansions give
   g(U)=E[Sf|U],
   K(U)=E[(Sf)^2-(Af)^2|U].
   Conditional Jensen therefore gives
   (g(U)^2-K(U))_+ <= E[(Af)^2|U].
3. The pointwise diffusion inequality is (Af)^2<=V e(X_0). Because V is
   U-measurable, E[(Af)^2|U]<=V E[e(X_0)|U].
4. Divide on {V>=epsilon}, integrate, and use nonnegativity of e to obtain the
   displayed bound. If sigma is finite, monotone convergence as epsilon falls
   to zero is justified and the resulting bound remains finite. At V=0,
   positive-definite D forces grad f=0 and hence Af=0. Jensen gives
   (g(U)^2-K(U))_+=0 there, so the ratio may consistently be defined as zero.

There is no need to assume E[Af|U]=0. That identity need not hold after
conditioning on local volatility. The symmetric past/future definition of g
is essential to avoid making such an unsupported replacement.

Why V is an ideal observable, and why this is not yet an apparatus claim:
For the convention dX=b dt+sqrt(2D)dW, the scalar observed path has
 [Y]_t=2 integral_0^t Gamma_f(X_s) ds.
When Gamma_f(X_t) is continuous, half the derivative of this quadratic variation
is V_t. Exact continuous-path access therefore determines V_t, even when the
hidden state itself is inaccessible. Quadratic variation is a limiting path
functional, not the square of a single short increment or an ordinary finite
sample variance. Time averaging or a conditional regression of squared
increments generally estimates a different quantity.

The population conditioning is legitimate because the ideal V_0 is a function
of X_0. In finite data, a V estimate built from the same increments as g or K
can introduce endogeneity and selection bias; treating that estimate as exact
would invalidate the argument. Independent high-frequency calibration,
carefully separated windows, or a joint confidence construction would require
new proofs. Detector noise, finite exposure, unresolved memory, and finite-h
bias still obstruct immediate application. A valid upper envelope for local
volatility could be more practical than trying to recover its exact value.

Assessment: the logical population chain survives. The next mathematical
bottleneck becomes finite-resolution access to V and a finite-h, statistically
covered conditional defect. This is a stronger and more general future
research target than imposing closure by Y alone. It still needs a close
prior-art comparison and independent adversarial review before any novelty or
release claim. No additional empirical evidence has been produced.

8. Global minimum for a complete observed diffusion law — 2026-10-12T23:10:59Z
PROVISIONAL NEXT-PHASE CONSEQUENCE. NOT INCLUDED IN THE CURRENT RELEASE.

Claim to formalize and independently review
Consider the class of all stationary smooth ordinary-reversal overdamped hidden
diffusions satisfying the regularity/integrability contract above, in arbitrary
finite hidden dimension, with a smooth scalar deterministic detector Y=f(X).
Fix the COMPLETE NOISELESS CONTINUOUS-TIME stationary law of Y, with a calibrated
physical clock. Suppose that law is exactly the law of
 Y_t=cos(phi_t), d phi_t=omega dt+sqrt(2 gamma)dW_t mod 2pi,
with uniform stationary phase and gamma>0.
The proposed weighted local-defect theorem would imply that the infimum of
stationary entropy over this entire compatible hidden-diffusion class is
 omega^2/gamma,
and the circle itself attains it. This would be a global compatible-model
minimum within the declared broad diffusion class, not merely a circulant
architecture minimum.

Why the population logic survives
- A full continuous scalar-path law determines its quadratic-variation law.
  Under smooth continuous coefficients, V_t=Gamma_f(X_t) is half its derivative.
  Thus the joint law of U_t=(Y_t,V_t), past and future Y values, and all conditional
  expectations entering g(U),K(U) is the same in every compatible model.
- Conditional versions may differ on null sets, but the integrated bound does
  not. Hence the proposed bound is an invariant of the specified observed law.
- For the cosine-circle law, V=gamma(1-Y^2), g=-gamma Y, and
  K=gamma^2Y^2-omega^2(1-Y^2). The ratio equals omega^2/gamma almost surely;
  Y=+/-1 has zero stationary probability. Truncations increase to this value.
- Every compatible hidden diffusion must therefore have entropy at least that
  value. The declared circle is one compatible diffusion whose entropy equals
  it, proving attainment once the general population theorem is established.

A useful equivalent perspective: full-law compatibility with the cosine circle
actually forces Gamma_f=gamma(1-f^2) almost surely under each compatible hidden
stationary law, by uniqueness of quadratic variation. With positive density and
smooth coefficients this may strengthen to a pointwise identity on the support.
The extra diffusion-geometry relation is supplied by observed-path access,
rather than assumed from a covariance fit. This removes the earlier logical
reason that covariance alone could not enforce the physical minimum.

Strict scope and noncontradictions
- Equality of a few lagged moments, or even the complete scalar covariance C(t),
  does NOT imply equality of the continuous path law or local quadratic variation.
  The previously constructed five-state hub counterexample matches covariance,
  not this path law; it is also a jump process. There is no contradiction.
- No corresponding conclusion is established for generalized/odd-variable
  reversal, inertial equilibrium models, jumps, singular/non-Markov latent models,
  noisy or history-dependent detectors, uncertain clocks, or a record sampled
  only at finitely many times.
- The complete scalar observed law may itself be reversible. The conclusion uses
  the smooth ordinary-overdamped hidden-model restriction and its local diffusion
  geometry; it is not the entropy of the scalar time-reversed record alone.
- The law is an ideal infinite-information research object. Proving its minimum
  does not mean a finite laboratory dataset can certify that exact law or recover
  the same entropy. Finite-h and finite-sample coverage remain separate gates.
- No claim that the observed process is Markovian is needed or justified. The
  Markov property used in the proof belongs to each complete hidden diffusion.

Acceptance gate before promotion
1. State a precise model class, quadratic-variation reconstruction convention,
   conditional-expectation versions, generator-domain assumptions, and the
   truncated limiting argument. Prove the population bound in that class.
2. Independently audit path-law invariance and circle attainment. Attempt to
   construct a lower-entropy compatible smooth ordinary diffusion; any success
   must invalidate an explicit step, not merely match its covariance.
3. Compare against conditional/local short-time entropy inference, variance sum
   rules, coarse-grained diffusion reconstruction, and prior variational minimum-
   dissipation results. Do not claim novelty from this note or internal agreement.
4. Derive a finite-h bias bound, detector-noise/exposure contract, and a covered
   estimator of the conditional defect/local volatility with honest physical
   acquisition cost. Only then assess practical gain against current endpoint
   inference at matched resources.

Assessment: this is a coherent stronger next target. If formalized and new in
the relevant sense, it would close a global physical-realization problem for a
specified observed continuous law while clearly identifying the extra access
needed. It is neither a released theorem here nor a claim of a new physical law.
