9. Numerical precision issue identified and repaired in the construction
The finite module rounds square roots outward on a fixed 90-bit grid.
A literal final radius computed this way has a nonzero absolute numerical
floor and is not an asymptotically consistent procedure as m tends infinity.
The proof concerns exact Bernstein radii; a new small wrapper obtains an
executable consistent certificate without altering frozen finite code:
enclose fixed H,B0,ell=log(2/alpha),sqrt(2ell) once by rational constants,
then form rho_bar=Hbar*kbar/m+2Bbar*ellbar/(3m4) with exact rational division.
All enclosure errors are multiplied by vanishing powers of 1/m. This radius
dominates the mathematical Bernstein radius and converges to zero exactly.
The manuscript now distinguishes this wrapper from fixed absolute rounding.
Generic deterministic stored-mean error eta_m can be subtracted using the
ideal-score concentration event directly, giving gap<=populationloss+2rho+2eta.
