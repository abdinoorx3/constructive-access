Derivation
The frozen weighted score is r/a[-(q-d+/h)(q-d-/h)-2|q|beta_g-beta_K].
Under the ordinary stationary diffusion and pointwise geometry envelope contract,
its expectation is at most sigma. Sup-norm semigroup bounds give
beta_g=h(M2+ + M2-)/4 and beta_K=h(M1- M2+ + M1+ M2-)/2.
Both finite-difference drifts are bounded by their M1 constants, so a symmetric
product telescoping identity avoids an unnecessary h^2 remainder.

Statistical bridge
Hoeffding is available with a bounded conditional-mean calibration. IID data
also admit the actual Maurer--Pontil empirical-Bernstein inequality, and a
known-variance Bernstein path when a global Gamma bound is additionally known.
Per-record score perturbations expand the range and subtract their uniform
radius; the empirical-variance calculation uses exact stored first/second sums.
No same-data fitting or automatic choice of the best confidence method is covered.

Primary source inspected directly
Andreas Maurer and Massimiliano Pontil, Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample
Variance Penalization (2009), arXiv:0907.3740v1, Theorem 4, pp.2--3.
URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.3740
The theorem states upper expectation minus empirical mean <=
sqrt(2 V_n log(2/delta)/n)+7 log(2/delta)/(3(n-1)) for iid[0,1] data.
Apply it to reflected affine-rescaled score for a lower expectation bound.
V_n is the ordinary unbiased sample variance, equivalent to the pairwise form.

Noise scope
Conditionally unbiased detector values with independent endpoint noises given
the hidden triple add +Var(noise0)/h^2 to K, hence conservatively reduce the
score when the center rule is independent of these noises. The implementation
rejects the unsupported noisy-center-selection contract. Deterministic bounded
reading error is a different route: preserve the exact frozen central rule,
calibrate a true-geometry envelope, and pay 4(R/h+Q)deltaY/(h a_min).
For the rounded cosine center the envelope
a(z)=gamma[1-max(0,|z|-deltaY)^2] is valid.
