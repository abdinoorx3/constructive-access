SHARED-RECORD COVERED COMBINATION
Status: application of the established union bound; retrospective bank reanalysis.

Purpose
The finite-step geometry penalty hides weak drive in the primary benchmark,
while the endpoint score remains sensitive. Combine the two on the same records
using alpha_G=alpha_E=1/40, max(L_G,L_E), total statistical failure<=1/20.
No independence between the scores is required. The complete component rules
and allocation must be fixed for prospective test data.

This analysis was selected after the original primary results. It is not a
separately frozen confirmatory experiment. No frozen experiment, contract, raw
record or primary result was modified. The original individual-.05 methods
retain their original interpretation; their unadjusted max is not asserted
to have .05 coverage. This new rule recomputes both radii at .025.

Mathematical/statistical bridge
Geometry uses the exact finite_resolution.certify_totals implementation.
Endpoint uses Z_s=-(sY0-Ypast)(sY0-Yfuture), E Z_s=Delta_s and
Z_s in[-(1+s)^2,1]. The bounded stored score range includes its uniform
error; Maurer--Pontil2009 Thm4 is applied to the reflected score, then
score error is subtracted. The positive defect d transfers through the
seventh-edition range-two bound sigma>=Phi(d)/h. The shared API checks the
common normalized readout contract and calibrated time. A shared pre-test
joint calibration failure is counted once; otherwise caller may sum budgets.
