NEW ROUTE RELATIVE TO THIRD EDITION
The previous one-window bound excludes <=3 hidden states but a four-state
classical model can match its integrated scalar contrast. Keep 2N-1 observation
times instead. A single fixed preparation/readout and repeated homogeneous
Markov channel gives p_k=alpha P^k f. The matrix M_ij=p_(N+i-j), i,j=0,...,N-1,
factors through the n hidden states with stochastic left factors and right
factors in [0,1]. This holds for arbitrary stochastic P, thus includes CTMCs and
IID random timing increments. No hidden preparation support or rate bound is
needed; the same channel/readout/preparation must actually be used.

ROBUST ANTICHAIN PROOF
Suppose M_ii<=epsilon and M_ij>=beta for i!=j. At response threshold tau, define
S_i={k:B_ki<tau}. The diagonal bound gives alpha_i(S_i^c)<=epsilon/tau. Inclusion
S_i subset S_j implies M_ij<=tau+(1-tau)epsilon/tau. Therefore strict beta above
that value makes all subsets incomparable. Sperner gives
N <= choose(n,floor(n/2)). Choosing tau=sqrt(epsilon) gives the sufficient
condition beta>2sqrt(epsilon)-epsilon. Exact zero epsilon uses zero supports.
The cardinality bound is sharp for arbitrary stochastic factors but no claim
of sharpness for homogeneous Hankel realizations is made.

EXPLICIT QUBIT
L(rho)=-i(omega/2)[X,rho]+gamma(Tr(rho)I/2-rho), preparation|0>, detector|1>.
Delta=2pi/(N omega), r=omega/gamma.
p_k=(1-exp(-2pi k/(rN))*cos(2pi k/N))/2.
Diagonal p_N=(1-exp(-2pi/r))/2. For N>=4 all offdiag >=sin^2(pi/N).
Sufficient coherence threshold: p_N<(1-cos(pi/N))^2.
This scales as N^-4. Inverse central-binomial(N) grows only logarithmically in
N. Thus arbitrary finite state exclusions exist at finite coherence/precision,
but this transparent hierarchy is inefficient at high dimension.

STATISTICAL DESIGN
Intersection-union test: reject if diagonal count is low AND every offdiag
count is high. A classical null violates at least one inequality. Hence type I
is bounded by the largest individual tail, not the sum. Power is bounded by
the sum of alternative misses. IID within settings is required for supplied
binomial calculations; independence across different settings is not needed
for this union/intersection argument. Parameter search uses floating proposals
but every reported comparison is rational/integer. Record calibration bias can
be included as null +/-delta and alternative adverse +/-delta.

FAILURE CONTROLS
Changing readout with observation step lets one hidden state mimic any curve.
Nonhomogeneous dynamics/history-dependent preparation can defeat the shared
factorization. Arbitrary total-time jitter cannot be bounded without rate or
record calibration; IID timing increments can be absorbed into one channel.
An N-state deterministic discrete cycle exactly reproduces ideal qubit p_k.
No thermodynamic cost follows from hidden state count alone. Finite-data
realization bounds do not exclude all classical explanations or prove a new
physical law.
