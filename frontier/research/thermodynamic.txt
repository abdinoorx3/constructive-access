SURVIVING RESULT
For a stationary finite homogeneous even-state CTMC Q, common noninvasive
binary detector with independent readout noise, and independently chosen
positive lag U, the dimensionless stationary entropy production obeys

  sigma E[U] >= 4 inf_{q in reversible correlation moment set}
                         sum_k w_k KL_Ber(p_k || q_k).

p_k is the probability of matching binary endpoints at lag t_k. The complete
proof and assumptions are in manuscript/thermodynamic.tex. This is a physical
entropy bound only with the corresponding even-state local-detailed-balance
contract. It is not a bound on quantum entropy production or on equilibrium
underdamped systems with odd variables. Physical entropy rate is k_B sigma;
under an isothermal steady dissipative realization power is k_B T sigma.

PROOF CHECKPOINT
1. Q* is the pi-adjoint; S=(Q+Q*)/2 is a reversible generator with same pi.
2. Stationary trajectory KL(Q||S)=t d(Q,S). Pair flux a,b contributes
   a log(2a/(a+b))+b log(2b/(a+b)) <= (a-b)log(a/b)/4.
   Factor1/4 is sharp near a=b. It is an established JS/Jeffreys inequality.
3. The S correlation is m^2+sum b_l exp(-lambda_l t), b_l>=0,
   sum b_l<=1-m^2. Independent detector noise allows f_i in [-1,1].
4. At positive commensurate lags missing spectral mass can be padded by
   an atom x=0 in the Hausdorff moment set. This is a closure; x=0 need
   not correspond to a finite relaxation rate.
5. Random lag has the same distribution under Q and S, so path KL is
   E[U] d(Q,S). Data processing to (lag, matching endpoint bit) gives result.
6. A convex outer relaxation of the reversible moment set keeps validity.
   Relaxation q1>=...>=qK>=1/2 is exact weighted binomial PAVA.
7. For two lags, if simultaneous confidence bounds p1<=u<v<=p2 hold,
   rate lower bound is 4 JS_w(Ber(u),Ber(v))/(w t1+(1-w)t2).
   This can be positive when both p1,p2>=1/2 and when observed two-time
   time-reversal KL is identically zero. All stationary binary pair laws
   have equal off-diagonal probabilities, so this latter zero is exact.

ADDED PREPARATION / HOUSEKEEPING FRONTIER
Root proposed and thermodynamic_bridge and frontier_redteam independently
checked the nonstationary extension. Q retains a positive stationary law pi,
but actual initial law nu is arbitrary. Reference S starts in pi. Exit rates
of Q,Q*,S agree POINTWISE, hence the path chain rule is
  KL(Q_nu || S_pi) = D(nu||pi) + integral nu_s rowKL(Q||S) ds
                  <= D(nu||pi) + (1/2) integral sigma_hk(nu_s) ds.
Here sigma_hk(nu)=sum_i nu_i sum_j qij log(qij/q*ij) is the standard
housekeeping/adiabatic rate. Each row is nonnegative because equal exit rates
turn it into exit_rate times a KL between jump-destination probabilities.
Random independent lag averages the integrated term. Thus any valid record
separation J from the stationary reversible reference cone obeys
  D(nu||pi) + (1/2) E integral_0^U sigma_hk(nu_s) ds >= J.
A known upper bound Dmax on initial divergence gives integrated housekeeping
>=2 max(0,J-Dmax). If Q detailed-balanced, D(nu||pi)>=J. If nu=pi the earlier
factor1/4 gives the stronger stationary result. The unknown stationary mean
cannot be replaced by the actual transient mean; use full mean range or
independent calibration. Root's dual compiler handles that constraint.

Factor1/2 is sharp uniformly: use outgoing distributions
p=(1-eps^2,eps^2), q=(1-eps,eps), whose ratio KL(p||mean)/KL(p||q) tends1/2.
Realize this as row0 of a positive 3-state generator with offdiagonals
q01=1-eps^2,q02=eps^2,q10=1-eps,q20=eps,q12=1,q21=1-eps+eps^2.
Its stationary law is uniform. A sufficiently short interval starting at0
approaches the rowwise sharpness. Tests check positivity, stationary law,
factor ratio, integrated chain-rule bound and endpoint data processing.
This does not assert final observed bounds always attain their lower limit.

Physical interpretation: D(nu||pi) is initial information relative to the
stationary law. It becomes beta times nonequilibrium free energy only with a
calibrated equilibrium Gibbs reference. The standard decomposition is
sigma_total = sigma_hk - dD(nu_t||pi)/dt for homogeneous Q, not sigma_hk alone.
Primary source: Esposito and Van den Broeck, PRE82,011143(2010),
DOI10.1103/PhysRevE.82.011143, arxiv1005.1683, Eqs32,33,37-41. The decomposition
is established prior work; the operational record-separation budget is a
candidate synthesis. Inspected anonymous2026 source excludes nonstationarity.
