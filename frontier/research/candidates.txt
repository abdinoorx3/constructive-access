HIGH-LEVERAGE SELECTION — INTERNAL REVIEW, 12 OCTOBER 2026

Recommended principal result

An operational frontier for the resources required by a classical explanation
of restricted dynamical observations. Its three coordinates are hidden state
count, initial nonequilibrium preparation, and maintained entropy production.
The frontier is conditional on common homogeneous Markov dynamics and declared
observation access. It is not a universal classical-versus-quantum criterion.

Why this is stronger than extending one dimension witness

1. Finite noisy data can exclude a specified finite hidden dimension, with
   rate-cap-free algebraic certificates rather than fitted invisible poles.
2. Without a size, preparation, or stationarity constraint, arbitrary finite
   reset traces can be approximated by reversible finite classical systems,
   even irreducible ones. The missing assumption is constructively exposed.
3. Stationary scalar correlation shapes constrain all even-state classical
   models, independent of hidden dimension, through a reversible moment cone.
   They can force positive dissipation even when ordinary two-time reversal
   statistics, negative-correlation tests, and monotonicity tests vanish.
4. If stationarity is relaxed, a valid record separation still requires
   initial relative entropy or integrated housekeeping entropy. This explains
   rather than conceals the reversible approximation escape.

These are connected theorems about one inference task, not four unrelated
applications. A useful experimental endpoint is an independently checkable
statement of the form: every compatible model in this declared class needs
at least this much hidden memory, preparation divergence, or dissipation.

What survives the novelty hurdle

Spectral sectors, nonnegative-rank dimension witnesses, exact sinusoidal
quantum matrices, Poisson approximation, reversible comparison, and the
stationary sigma/4 bridge have substantial prior art. The broad separation
idea is not new. The precise candidate additions are the explicit nonzero
antichain margin, normalized finite Erlang robustness certificate, finite
reversible deterministic-readout compiler with operational transcript bounds,
and confidence-box-to-continuous-moment-cone dual inference. Historical
originality of those narrower additions remains to be checked externally.

The empirical bottleneck

An apparatus must justify common homogeneous dynamics, reproducible resets
or stationarity, a fixed noninvasive detector model, and physical reversal
parity. A finite Markov fit alone does not establish these conditions.
Hardware calibration and a matched comparison against existing thermodynamic
or dimension-witness methods are the next decisive contributions. Bigger
symbols, more abstract fields, or more pages cannot replace that evidence.

Recommended stopping rules for individual branches

- Reject universal energy-from-oscillation claims: odd-variable equilibrium
  systems and reversible transient preparation supply counterexamples.
- Reject unrestricted finite-record classical exclusions: the compiler gives
  a constructive operational closure result.
- Retain exact-continuum state counts only alongside finite-data gaps and
  actual sample costs; exact poles are not observed directly.
- Promote an experimentally measured advantage only after access and resource
  matched baselines, rather than comparing tomography to targeted testing as
  if they solved the same estimation problem.
