METHOD
Read all ten substantive chapter sources and the main reference's introductory
material and notation. Trace assumptions from abstract law identifiability to
finite instruments, precision, conditional adequacy, physical resource bounds,
continuous dynamics, applications, suite construction, and toolkit promises.
Check theorem proofs and counterexamples, especially whether a later chapter
silently weakens an earlier premise. Check symbolic conventions locally rather
than requiring a single letter to have one meaning across all mathematical
subjects. Automated label/reference scan found no duplicated labels and no
unresolved cross-references in the ten chapters plus reference.tex.

CORRECTIONS REQUESTED AND VERIFIED IN THE SOURCE
1. precision_design.tex: the multiplicative classical TV coupling bound now
   explicitly requires all radii in [0,1], with truncation of looser bounds.
   This is necessary: two nominally valid loose radii of 2 otherwise make the
   displayed product bound incorrectly equal zero.
2. precision_design.tex: the Bellman theorem explicitly uses finite classical
   instruments with nonnegative kernels and action normalization. Its row mass
   x_j 1 convention cannot silently be used for arbitrary signed quantum bases.
3. continuous_thermodynamics.tex: the singleton-reversal proof handles the
   one-state chain separately before dividing by its exit rate.
4. continuous_thermodynamics.tex: the local triangle expansion and associated
   information order explicitly use joint (F,eta)->(0,0), not merely F*eta->0.
5. continuous_thermodynamics.tex: the current estimator and confidence radius
   require positive gate Dirichlet form before division by it.
6. workflow.tex: after assigning pairs, experiments assigned no pair are
   discarded before taking the minimum defining their sampling radius. A
   family with one shared target explicitly requires no acquisition.
7. workflow.tex: the suite, events, nominal laws and radii are fixed before
   classification. Calibration-derived random inputs require iid records
   conditional on pre-acquisition calibration information and bounds on those
   conditional laws. This supplies the required unconditional intersection
   guarantee, rather than assuming arbitrary conditioning preserves Hoeffding.

CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER FINDINGS
entry.tex -- accepted.
The two-state drive/read example matches the finite-instrument conventions.
A drive alone has deterministic output, a read alone is initially symmetric,
and drive/read has action charge 3 with overhead 1. Twelve records yield the
stated sufficient affinity bound 0.5*(3/4)^6 < 0.1. Counted operations are not
called joules. Four outputs (obstruction, witness, precision, resource) remain
distinct throughout the later chapters.

foundations.tex -- accepted, self-consistency check.
Target factorization is set-theoretic, not an implied measurable or efficient
inverse. Simulation kernels are candidate independent. Resource ordering is
conditioned on matched resource accounting. The real-target radius is local to
the declared compatible set. The finite target-suite theorem needs a finite
candidate family and permitted fresh preparations under the same fixed model.
Its existence conclusion is correctly strengthened only later by sampling
assumptions and explicit probability bounds.

finite_access.tex -- accepted, self-consistency check.
Chronological words, backward effect propagation and classical row states are
consistent. Quantum density-operator composition is explicitly translated to
that representation without imposing classical positivity. The d-1 bound is
in supplied representation dimension. The grammar is an action-only masking
construction. Nonnegative additive action charges and lexicographic length are
retained in the cost search and certificate. The positive-threshold
undecidability result retains its one-readout/no-reset grammar and does not
contradict amplification using independently reset records in later chapters.

precision_design.tex -- accepted after corrections 1 and 2.
The two-point lower bound uses a uniform pairwise bound before taking suprema;
no invalid minimax exchange occurs. The row-space criterion has both law
factorization and access sufficiency. The LP minimax equality is global over
the full positive orthant and full Cartesian error box, whereas the earlier
compatible-set radius is data local. Fixed nuisance values remain fixed across
records. The robust score and affinity bounds have different error objectives.
The conditional bounded-score extension is justified by the conditional MGF
argument, and full hidden-state/instrument calibration is required. Bellman
risk is classical, finite horizon, positive prior, prescribed target loss and
budget; it is not an optimality claim for the cheapest exact witness.

adequacy_energy.tex -- accepted.
The true candidate's prediction bounds are full-history conditional bounds,
not marginal forecasts. The union-null target guarantee protects one fixed
valid candidate and hence needs no candidate-count correction; simultaneous
protection of all valid candidates is correctly distinguished. Stopping wealth
before the first predictable invalid envelope establishes the intersection
bound on a joint calibration event without invalid conditioning. The energy
tail uses the actual counting process and a bounded stopping time. Resource
contract failure is treated by a joint-event argument and is not assumed
predictable. Gross expenditure, net dissipation, expected expenditure and a
high-probability cap remain separate. The handoff theorem preserves these
premises and adds a joint calibration failure only once.

continuous_thermodynamics.tex -- accepted after corrections 3--5.
The graph affinity quotient has dimension m-c+1 under connected total support
and a bulk-balancing reference. Its source statement is not an observation
sufficiency statement. Survival selection and nonstationary preparation are
separate from steady entropy production. Rational uniformization supplies an
error-bounded stochastic approximation but never certifies exact continuous
model equality from approximate equality. Singleton renewal blindness is
restricted to a singleton detector. The four-state aggregate counterexample
prevents extrapolation to arbitrary binary observations. The switch identity
uses common stationarity and a separately balanced added channel. The gate
coefficient is sufficient in general and the converse is restricted to the
triangle. Its remainder/CI uses positive Dirichlet form and independent
preparations. The drive KL/energy identity is explicitly tied to the triangle's
channel-resolved physical realization, not used as a universal energy law.

applications.tex -- accepted.
Chemical decomposition ambiguity persists even with complete occupancy paths
when the full generators agree; this is correctly distinguished from a
sensor-hidden difference between generators exposed by a balanced gate.
Selective intervention changes stationarity and the perturbed current, and
native-current reconstruction uses the calibrated response model. Net current,
unidirectional throughput and molar flux remain separate. The XX chain's
population blindness and stronger sign symmetry use their stated instrument
classes and preparation. Ancilla extensions preserve the center-only symmetry;
spatial bond asymmetry changes access. Reservoir bias is not conflated with a
processor's coherent error. The coherent/stochastic diagnostic is a specified
small dictionary, and the short-witness/sample-complexity distinction is
reinforced by the replacer-channel lower bound. Ramsey design has a fixed
nuisance and compares Bayes sign risk at matched maximum duration; it is not
presented as global Fisher or energy optimality. Hidden independent dissipation
correctly rules out an unrestricted total-energy inference from visible laws.
The detailed historical coefficient computations were not all reexecuted in
this audit; the source derivations and their interfaces were reviewed.

workflow.tex -- accepted after corrections 6 and 7.
The common compatible-target output faithfully extends the abstract quotient
and calibrated intervals. Coverage/resource composition is a union bound on
unconditional intersections with G and adds its failure once. Suite selection
is finite set cover over positive robust gaps, not a global experimental
minimum or a total acquisition-energy optimum. Its sample count uses
exp(1)>2 to upper-bound Hoeffding rationally. Pairwise assigned event intervals
cannot retain two different targets because the robust gap exceeds 2r_e.
The target theorem is conditional on an included fixed true candidate, finite
menu, predeclared events, valid law envelopes and iid records; all-rejected
abstention does not validate an omitted alternative.

toolkit.tex -- accepted as a mathematical-interface statement.
The two-model rational classical JSON scope is distinguished from the broader
advanced APIs and finite-family suite workflow. Exact access certificates,
finite-error reports, calibration gap failure, non-iid declarations and
transcript limits receive different outputs. The calibrated two-model example
uses conditional iid data under pre-acquisition calibration information, in
agreement with the corrected suite theorem. The source explicitly does not
infer physical calibration or energy from a matrix/contract digest. This pass
does not substitute for the separate software implementation and forgery tests.

research_map.tex -- accepted.
Established automata, predictive representations, classical/quantum linear
models, decision theory, thermodynamic inference and adaptive sensing are
credited. Proposed integration and specific physical coefficients are not
called a newly validated physical law or externally accepted discipline.
Earlier detector classifications and information-order comparisons are
identified as research lineage, with detailed inherited calculations retained
in the provenance archive. External specialist review, physical calibration,
matched comparisons and independent replication remain distinct milestones.

NOTATION AND SCOPE
Rows are used for classical state laws; the XX effect-space display explicitly
uses columns for images, so its C^T stationary-expectation equation is
consistent. varpi denotes the primary stationary distribution; locally defined
rho, Pi, and nu serve preparations or joint/controller stationary laws.
alpha/beta are reused for algebraic vectors and statistical error allocations,
with explicit local definitions. Sigma as finite alphabet versus entropy
notation is likewise local. No unresolved convention mismatch was found.
The finite sampling, calibration and energy results do not assume that
equivalence of observed laws fixes omitted physical channels or mechanisms.

not an independent full rerun of every historical symbolic/numerical result,
