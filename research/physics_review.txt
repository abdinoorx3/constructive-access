Purpose and achieved scope

The chapters turn the physical branches into a coherent entry-to-application
sequence. They define the source of a stationary arrow separately from its
observed record and preparation, derive a graph/interface cycle criterion,
explain the original boundary intuition without requiring higher-dimensional
geometry, and then convert continuous dynamics into finite access contracts.
The source criterion is attributed established cycle/cohomology mathematics,
not a newly discovered physical law.

The continuous chapter includes a rational stochastic uniformization
approximation and exact row-TV envelope, its transfer to record laws, complete
singleton renewal obstruction, an observable switched commutator/current proof,
the exact triangle contrast with exponential derivation, and the autonomous
gate quartic coefficient with a block-matrix derivation. Its finite-lag current
interval explicitly includes bias, calibration premises and independence;
its large generic constant is disclosed. Physical channel-resolved entropy,
chemical free-energy expenditure, gross counts versus net expenditure, and a
counting-process energy tail are kept distinct. Detector bandwidth and hidden
intermediates are carried into the physical realization requirements.

The application chapter uses a consistent target/access/intervention/precision/
resource progression across chemistry, quantum symmetry and gate diagnostics,
Ramsey sensing, and energy inference. It contains the full five-dimensional
XX accessible-effect closure and Kraus action, the exact stationary state and
two magnitude-witness Taylor expansions, and the reflection proof extending
orientation blindness to ancillary feedback protocols. Bond asymmetry is
explicitly a change of physical access. It remains a reservoir-bias example,
separate from coherent gate error.

Critical distinctions preserved

* A stationary arrow is not the existence of time or monotone state entropy.
* A bulk/interface source theorem does not prove detector visibility.
* Survival/preparation can create endpoint asymmetry without sustained EP.
* A singleton detector is not an arbitrary aggregate or scalar count.
* Reversible frozen controls must share the same stationary law.
* An additive balanced pathway differs from scaling a driven channel.
* Equal full permitted generators remain equal under common additive gates.
  Chemical channel decomposition instead needs selective access (or suitable
  independently known thermodynamic priors).
* A short exact witness is not a useful sample bound or perfect discriminator.
* Quantum central magnitude activation does not remove reflection sign blindness.
* Ramsey's nuisance parameter is fixed, not independently redrawn each shot;
  the objective is finite-prior Bayes sign classification, not Fisher information.
* A gate with zero added steady channel EP is not a free apparatus.
* Arbitrarily strong processing cannot infer an independently hidden fuel cycle.
* All worked numbers are hypothetical model calculations. Physical calibration,
  device/biological measurements, external peer review, priority and adoption are
  not represented as accomplished.
