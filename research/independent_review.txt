Initial integration audit
=========================

The correct common object is a scientific target on a declared model family,
together with its accessible transcript laws. A finite linear representation is
a computational realization of that object, not the definition of all physical
systems. The following distinctions must survive the integration:

1. An exact equality certificate is relative to the full declared preparation,
   operation, outcome and stopping rules. It does not cover omitted controls,
   unmodeled memory, arbitrary quantum ancillas, or continuous control values.
2. A minimum-charge nonzero word can have vanishingly small signal. The word's
   declared additive optimum does not certify the least total sample count,
   least finite-error experimental charge, or least apparatus energy.
3. Target identification is weaker than mechanism reconstruction. Two retained
   models may justify the same target even when their internal states differ.
4. Full conditional prediction envelopes are required for sequential testing.
   One-time stationary marginals are insufficient under dependence.
5. A fixed nuisance parameter is not redrawn each record. Convexifying one-record
   model families can change the experiment and erase usable repeated-record
   information.
6. No finite-model adequacy calculation establishes that nature belongs to the
   family. Invisible extra subsystems can change hidden current or dissipation
   while preserving every accessible record law.
7. Physical energy is an additional model and calibration contract. It cannot
   be derived from an arbitrary action count or merely from sensor-law accuracy.

Recommended integration statements
==================================

Target projection: Let C_t be retained model sets and g the target. If the true
model remains in C_t simultaneously with probability at least 1-alpha-delta,
then g(C_t) simultaneously covers the true target with that probability. A
decision correct for every target in g(C_t) inherits the same guarantee. This is
the standard confidence-set projection principle. It is useful for unifying
categorical target labels, scalar target intervals, and cautious decisions.
Empty sets require rejection/abstention, never a vacuous conclusion.

Two-point resource lower bound: For two targets g_0,g_1 and a common experiment
with record laws P_0,P_1, every real estimator T satisfies

  max_h E_h |T-g_h| >= |g_0-g_1| (1-TV(P_0,P_1))/2.

To prove it, integrate |T-g_0|+|T-g_1| >= |g_0-g_1| against the overlap measure
min(dP_0,dP_1), whose mass is 1-TV. Estimator randomization is a common channel
and does not increase total variation. If D_C is the supremal pairwise TV over
the same declared resource-feasible policy class, replace TV by D_C and then
take the supremum over model pairs. No minimax interchange or achievability is
asserted. Infinite invisible target diameter excludes a finite uniform absolute
error bound, by taking arbitrarily separated finite pairs. This is established
two-point decision theory, not a new physical law.

Resource composition: A separately calibrated acquisition bound can be joined
to a target error guarantee by a union bound. A shared joint calibration failure
probability must be counted once; independence between inference and energy is
unnecessary. None of these algebraic implications verifies the physical premise.
