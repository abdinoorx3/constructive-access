The strongest defensible positioning

A unified reference and toolkit for identifying target properties of hidden
dynamics under specified physical access. The distinctive practical object is
the complete argument from an access contract to an independently checkable
obstruction, intervention, statistical decision, calibration allowance, and
physical resource claim. Generality resides in that common interface. Exact
finite representations support constructive algorithms; other model classes
need their own effective approximation and error contracts.

This is a useful subject organization across existing fields. Calling it
Constructive Access does not establish that a new accepted field exists. Its
immediate contribution can be judged by whether another scientist can specify
an apparatus, find an identification failure, construct a better experiment,
and reproduce the resulting guarantee without rebuilding the algebra and
statistical interfaces from several papers.

Existing intellectual foundations

1. Statistical decision and comparison of experiments: Blackwell's experiment
   ordering supplies an established ancestor of access refinement. The target
   matters; no scalar information score orders every scientific decision.
2. Aggregated Markov models: Kienker (1989), Larget (1998), and subsequent
   ion-channel literature already treat observationally equivalent hidden
   mechanisms and the effect of additional experimental conditions.
3. Automata, realization, and predictive states: Tzeng, Kiefer et al., Jaeger,
   Littman et al., and Thon--Jaeger supply finite linear equivalence, witness
   construction, and controlled prediction. A minimal predictive realization
   is not uniquely identified physical ontology.
4. Quantum finite-state equivalence: Faigle--Schoenhuth and Wang et al. supply
   classical/quantum linear methods; Riechers--Elliott (2025) is close recent
   primary work on unified canonical representations and minimality.
5. Thermodynamic inference: Qian--Elson gives three-time correlation current
   inference; Barato et al. gives equilibrium kinetic information; recent
   transition-record methods recover some hidden kinetics and entropy bounds.
6. Optimal recovery, finite decision design, uniformization, concentration,
   and e-processes supply the statistical and computational foundations.

What the trajectory adds, with status kept separate

The ring classifications, response-order distinctions, controlled triangle,
autonomous gate coefficient/activation result, and central quantum-access
classification are specific model results. Their proofs and independent
internal checks are in preserved sources. Historical priority is unresolved.

The prescribed-action-cost filtered certificate and its independent rational
checker are concrete computational artifacts. The distinction from earlier
minimum-weight methods is the exact supplied objective and exported closure
obligations, not the invention of algebraic equivalence or minimum words.

The calibration/adequacy/resource composition makes an operational inference
contract. Its ingredients are established probability and experimental-design
methods. Its value is that users can audit the connecting assumptions and
receive ambiguity or model rejection instead of an unsupported answer.

The latest applications supply small reproducible physical diagnostics:
selective reaction-channel inference, coherent/stochastic channel distinction,
fixed-nuisance Ramsey design, and a visible-record equivalence family with
unbounded hidden energy. They do not establish in-vivo flux recovery, scalable
fault-tolerant computing, total AI power minimization, or optimized NV hardware.

Claims refined or rejected

- Existence of time from entropy increase -> separate dynamical evolution,
  source dissipation, record irreversibility, and equilibrium compatibility.
- Universal data cap -> target- and access-dependent equivalence and error.
- Every scalar/coarse binary record is reversible -> only stated singleton
  renewal settings; a two-by-two four-state partition is a counterexample.
- Every modulation reveals a hidden mechanism -> same full-generator channel
  decompositions survive common additive state-level controls.
- Zero gate-channel steady entropy -> no inference of free sensing, free
  preparation, undriven total system, or zero apparatus work.
- Short exact witness -> no inference of reliable low-sample identification;
  tiny signal and calibration errors can dominate, and qubit count matters.
- Candidate nonrejection -> no model truth guarantee or family completeness.
- Better Fisher information -> no automatic improvement in sign Bayes risk.
- Four application grand challenges solved -> explicit model deliverables
  plus calibrated external acceptance tests for each proposed transfer.
- RSI, P=NP, neural-network latent ontology, a new universal physical law ->
  not consequences of the established results.

Application acceptance tests

Reaction-channel inference: independently measure the selective response,
demonstrate identifiable native rates against an orthogonal flux assay, and
check disturbance and conversion to molecule/concentration units.

Quantum diagnosis: validate the dictionary, SPAM, temporal memory, and crosstalk;
compare to robust phase estimation/coherence or cycle-benchmarking baselines
at the same fault target, error, device calls, resets, and elapsed exposure.

Ramsey sensing: measure contrast, dephasing, readout offset and drift; test
fixed versus adaptive policies on a fixed unknown sample, not a resampled
nuisance; compare at the same decision loss and time/energy boundary.

Energy inference: add measured fuel or power access that constrains hidden
dissipation, calibrate conversion to gross/net work, include apparatus costs,
and compare at fixed computational performance and accuracy.

The release can be mature as a finite-model reference and toolkit once its
entry workflow, proofs, negative controls and independent checks are complete.
Physical maturity and community adoption are separate outcomes that require
the above external events. No validation stage is inferred from enthusiasm,
the number of pages, or the number of internally checked claims.
