Status and attribution

This is supporting integration of established stochastic lumpability,
intertwining, probabilistic bisimulation and total-variation contraction. It
provides exact checkable interfaces and failure controls for the access program;
it is not promoted as the central new physical result. Relevant prior work:

  Peter Buchholz, Exact and ordinary lumpability in finite Markov chains,
  Journal of Applied Probability 31(1), 59-75 (1994), DOI 10.2307/3215235.

  Robert Paige and Robert E. Tarjan, Three Partition Refinement Algorithms,
  SIAM Journal on Computing 16(6), 973-989 (1987), DOI 10.1137/0216062.

  Gaoang Bian and Alessandro Abate, On the Relationship between Bisimulation
  and Trace Equivalence in an Approximate Probabilistic Context, FoSSaCS 2017,
  321-337, DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-54458-7_19; arXiv:1701.04547.

The distinction between local equivalence and contextual congruence also has a
substantial quantum-process literature, including Lalire (2006),
arXiv:quant-ph/0603274, and Deng/Feng, Open Bisimulation for Quantum Processes.
No classical result here is asserted for arbitrary quantum interactions.

1. Contract

A finite classical instrument has nonnegative n x n matrices M_{a,y}, with
sum_y M_{a,y} stochastic for each action a. Preparation mu is a probability
row vector. Every policy is a finite tree: actions may depend on the complete
retained outcome history, and every outcome branch must be retained. Stopping
can be outcome-dependent. Randomized policies are mixtures with a common,
hypothesis-independent private seed and obey the same bounds.

Let C be a row-stochastic n x k interface and let B_{a,y} be a k-state
instrument with the same actions/outcomes. Define joint outcome/interface
defects

  epsilon_a(x)=1/2 sum_{y,z}|(M_{a,y}C-CB_{a,y})_{xz}|.

These are numbers in [0,1]. They include the post-measurement/interface state,
not merely the immediate distribution of y. Controls, readout updates,
preparation and couplings must all be included in the declared contract.

2. Adaptive error propagation

Theorem. Let P be the concrete terminal-record law for preparation mu and Q
the quotient record law for preparation nu under the same finite policy tree.
For each internal history h let mu_h=mu M_h be the UNNORMALIZED concrete row
at that history and let a(h) be its selected action. Then

 TV(P,Q) <= min{1, TV(mu C,nu)+sum_h mu_h epsilon_{a(h)}}.

The sum is the expected accumulated local defect before stopping, evaluated
under the concrete model; it can be tighter than horizon times a global defect.

Proof. Let nu_h be the quotient branch row and d_h=mu_h C-nu_h. At an action a,

 d_{hy}=mu_h(M_{a,y}C-CB_{a,y})+d_h B_{a,y}.

Nonnegativity of mu_h gives sum_y ||mu_h(M_{a,y}C-CB_{a,y})||_1
<=2 mu_h epsilon_a. Nonnegativity and outcome normalization of B give
sum_y ||d_h B_{a,y}||_1<=||d_h||_1. Summing this recursion over the finite
tree bounds the sum of leaf ||d_h||_1 by the initial norm plus all local
defects. A terminal record's probability difference is d_h 1 because C1=1.
Taking half the sum of absolute probability differences proves the result.
All branches, including rejected-looking outcomes and stopped branches, count.

Corollary. Exact intertwining M_{a,y}C=CB_{a,y} and matching interface
preparations imply equality for every permitted finite adaptive record. Two
different concrete models with the same quotient therefore remain
indistinguishable in contexts obeying the interface contract. This is a
sufficient certificate. It is not a claim that every trace-equivalent pair
admits a common stochastic quotient of this form.

3. Tensor composition and shared preparations

For independent local action instruments and product interface C1 tensor C2,
the product residual on a state pair (x1,x2) obeys

 epsilon_joint <= epsilon_1+epsilon_2-epsilon_1 epsilon_2.

Indeed each side of the local comparison is a normalized distribution over
(outcome,next interface state). Couple the two local pairs maximally and
independently. Both pairs agree with probability at least
(1-epsilon_1)(1-epsilon_2); total variation is no greater than the coupling
disagreement probability. This remains an upper bound rather than equality.
For more factors it becomes 1-product_i(1-epsilon_i), in particular at most
the sum of defects. Subsequent adaptive context uses the preceding theorem.

Arbitrary interactions are not product instruments. Each added interaction
must satisfy the joint interface contract or incur its own computed defect.
Moreover the initial condition is equality of the FULL joint interface
preparation. Equality of local marginals alone is insufficient. For example,
the two preparations 1/2(00+11) and 1/2(01+10) have identical one-bit marginals;
a CNOT followed by reading its target bit distinguishes their parity perfectly.
Independent preparation is one sufficient way to infer joint-interface
equality from local certificates; it is not required if joint equality is
established directly.

4. Coarsest deterministic Markov interface after adding a coupling

Given a declared observation partition P0 of the microscopic states, repeat:
split each block according to the vectors

  (sum_{j in B} M_{a,y}(i,j))_{a,y,B in current partition}.

The iteration terminates after at most n-|P0| strict refinement rounds. Its
final partition P* is the unique coarsest refinement of P0 for which each
instrument has a quotient satisfying exact deterministic intertwining.

Proof. Each strict step increases the block count. At termination states in
a block give equal transition/outcome mass to every target block, which is
exactly the quotient identity. Any stable refinement of the current partition
must separate states whose displayed signatures differ: a sum over current
blocks is a sum over the finer stable blocks. Induction shows every stable
refinement of P0 refines every iteration, hence refines P*. This proves both
closure and coarseness. A certificate records all forced refinement rounds;
the independent checker verifies their local justification and final closure.

Scope: minimality is among DETERMINISTIC Markov interfaces refining P0 and
valid for all initial preparations. It is not the smallest linear realization,
the coarsest trace-equivalence relation, a minimum sensor-energy architecture,
or a minimum distinguishing experiment. Partition growth describes necessary
state retained by this kind of modular abstraction. Physical access to every
refined block is not asserted merely because the algorithm names it.

5. Exact failure controls and outputs

The four-state CNOT model has state (hidden h, visible o), passive waiting and
projective readout of o. Preparations (h,o)=(0,0) and (1,0) are passively
identical. The coupling (h,o)->(h,o XOR h) is a stochastic permutation; one
coupling and one visible readout distinguish them perfectly. The coarsest
deterministic interface changes from two states to four. This is a finite
logically reversible gate, not a claim of zero hardware energy cost.

A separate counterexample gives two instruments with equal immediate
prepare_hidden outcome laws but different hidden updates. A later readout
distinguishes them perfectly. A calibration statement that checks only
immediate output probabilities would assign zero error and fail; the joint
defect detects the hidden update discrepancy and bounds the record TV by one.

The rational adaptive example has terminal-record TV=3/40 and expected
accumulated defect=1509/8000. It includes outcome-dependent stopping and action
choice, and the code retains every branch. Tensor defects and correlated
preparations are separately checked. No statistics or energy claim is inferred
from these finite logical examples.
