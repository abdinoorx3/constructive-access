Status: independent mathematical and computational review completed for the

Scope
The candidate advance is a constructive, calibrated physical access completion
method. A graph observability formula alone is not a new physical law, nor does
the present review establish historical priority. Existing linear algebra,
network flow duality, graph observability and statistical identification supply
much of the mathematical machinery.

Initial findings
1. For a fixed known oriented multigraph and unrestricted signed steady
   circulations B j = 0, an unmeasured edge target is recoverable from measured
   edge currents exactly when it is a bridge in the unmeasured subgraph. An
   equivalent certificate is a cut whose only unmeasured crossing is the target.
   The multigraph condition matters: a second invisible parallel channel closes
   a two-edge circulation even when the collapsed simple graph is a tree.
2. If only measured currents have independent deterministic error bounds, the
   exact target minimax radius is a weighted separating cut, equivalently the
   dual circulation capacity problem. This follows from familiar row-space
   duality and max-flow/min-cut. It requires the full circulation model class;
   known activity, positivity, kinetic or prior bounds can change the minimax
   value and can make a structurally hidden target bounded rather than unbounded.
3. A nominal graph certificate assumes a complete physical inventory of crossing
   channels. An omitted parallel channel can invalidate identifiability without
   changing any state-level generator. Model adequacy tests cannot necessarily
   expose that omission through the same unchanged state-level data.
4. Ordinary finite-state CTMC edge counts at a fixed elapsed time are not
   independent Poisson observations conditional on terminal occupation times.
   In a two-state chain starting at A, the forward and reverse counts differ by
   zero or one on every path. Independent nondegenerate Poisson counts violate
   this support constraint. The physics agent has adopted source-local-time
   stopping instead, which admits an exact marked-Poisson representation.
5. Source-local-time acquisition requires a fixed time-homogeneous generator,
   observation of each source state's occupation and departure destination, and
   a run prepared in that source. For finite irreducible chains, the local-time
   exposure finishes almost surely and has expected wall time T/pi_i. This is
   an expectation, not a deterministic deadline. A deadline can cause abstention;
   conditional coverage given successful completion is not automatic.
6. Selective perturbations must multiply the intended physical channel by a
   calibrated factor while leaving other channel kinetics invariant. A generic
   reporter, barrier change, or equilibrium gate does not automatically realize
   this intervention. Stationary populations under intervention may change;
   reconstructing a native current must use the native populations.

Mixed aggregate/channel observations
Let parallel channels be consistently oriented in bundles b. Fix the individually
measured channel set S and exactly known aggregate-current bundle set T. Write
r_b for the number of channels not in S and form H with one edge per b outside
T having r_b > 0. Mapping unsensed currents to bundle sums gives a surjection
onto the circulation space of H. Its fiber has dimension sum_b (r_b-1)_+.
Consequently the ambiguity dimension is
    sum_b (r_b-1)_+ + |E(H)| - |V(H)| + components(H).
For an unsensed target in b, identifiability is equivalent to r_b=1 and either
b in T or b being a bridge of H. This decomposition cleanly separates invisible
parallel-channel contrasts from spatial circulation contrasts. It is a direct
linear-network theorem, subject to exact data and a fixed topology.

For single-target minimum-price completion, direct target measurement must either
be excluded explicitly or be compared against the inferred solution. A target
already measured requires no added measurement. Nonnegative prices may be zero;
ties and disconnected graphs must be handled. Whole-network recovery selects
which channels may remain unmeasured: one most expensive channel in each known
aggregate bundle, and most expensive channels on a maximum-weight spanning
forest of unknown-aggregate bundles. Independent exhaustive checks are planned.

Independent review of the stronger pulse-response theorem
Let Q be the native finite irreducible row generator, pi its stationary row,
and L the single physical channel i <-> j with forward/backward rates a,b.
Let s=1-r>0 and Qr=Q-sL be a valid generator. Then
    pi L = J (e_j-e_i),  J=pi_i a-pi_j b,
and Duhamel's identity gives the endpoint response
    Delta p_j(t) = -s J H_ij(t),
    H_ij(t)=integral_0^t [P^r_jj(u)-P^r_ij(u)] du.
This algebra is valid even if Qr is reducible. Write tau_j for the first hitting
time of j starting from i and U_xy(t)=integral_0^t P_xy(u)du. Strong Markov gives
    U_ij(t)=E_i[1_{tau_j<=t} U_jj(t-tau_j)].
By Tonelli,
    H_ij(t)=integral_0^t P_jj(u) Pr_i(tau_j>t-u) du > 0.
Strict positivity follows from finite exit rates: P_jj(u)>=exp(-q_j u)>0
and Pr_i(tau_j>v)>=exp(-q_i v)>0. In particular, for q_i,q_j<=Lambda,
    t exp(-Lambda t) <= H_ij(t) <= t.
An endpoint-specific bound replaces the lower term by
    [exp(-q_i t)-exp(-q_j t)]/(q_j-q_i),
with t exp(-q_i t) as the equal-rate limit. No detailed balance or knowledge
of the rest of the network was used. The bound needs only endpoint exit-rate
ceilings, not an exit-rate ceiling on every hidden state.

The mathematical proof survives adversarial review. Its physical premises are
substantial: native stationary preparation, a genuinely selective channel
perturbation of known sign, and an endpoint-state occupancy observation.
For a biased three-state cycle with clockwise rates 2 and counterclockwise 1,
native pi is uniform and J_AB=1/3. Suppressing A<->B by 1/2 gives native
endpoint-B response derivative -1/6. Starting instead in B gives a matched
perturbed-minus-unperturbed derivative +1/2, reversing the inferred sign.
Slowing ALL rates gives Qr=rQ and exactly zero stationary response despite
nonzero native channel currents.

Protected coarse-observation extension (communicated to root and physics agent)
Let A contain j and not i. If every path from i into A must first hit j,
occupation of A is zero before tau_j. Replace U_jj by U_jA in the same proof:
    H_i,j;A(t)=integral_0^t P_jA(u) Pr_i(tau_j>t-u) du > 0.
The same endpoint-rate lower bound applies because P_jA>=P_jj. Thus a known
entrance-gateway topology can protect a coarse detector. Arbitrary coarse
detectors cannot: the explicit four-state negative control supplied by the
physics agent has a sign-crossing aggregate response while its singleton
destination response remains negative. The independent test certifies this
with exact rational Taylor enclosures at t=1/10 and t=1/2.

Quantum limit and independently checked physical counterexample
Two incoherent channels on |A>,|B> have rates (3k,k) and (k,3k), while H=g X.
The native state is I/2 and native channel-1 current is k. Suppress channel 1
by r. With z=p_A-p_B and y=Tr(rho sigma_y),
    z'=-4 gamma z+2g y+2k(1-r),  y'=-2g z-2 gamma y,
where gamma=k(1+r), initially z=y=0. The endpoint-B shift is -z/2.
For k=1/100,g=1,r=1/2, the independently certified shift is negative at t=1/2
and positive at t=2, the latter approximately 0.0016685828949671655.
The independent implementation uses an 80-term rational matrix Taylor series
with a geometric induced-infinity-norm remainder, not the producer's closed
form or numerical matrix exponential.

For g>gamma/2, the response-kernel derivative is
    exp(-3 gamma t)[cos(Omega t)-(gamma/Omega) sin(Omega t)],
where Omega=sqrt(4g^2-gamma^2). The first integral minimum occurs at
    t*=[pi+atan(Omega/gamma)]/Omega,
and its value is
    [2 gamma-2g exp(-3 gamma t*)]/[4g^2+8gamma^2].
Later minima increase toward the positive limiting value. Thus negative
integrated response occurs exactly when q=g/gamma exceeds the unique root of
    log(q)=3[pi+atan(sqrt(4q^2-1))]/sqrt(4q^2-1).
The independent numerical root is 4.5706645325339026031362132874923976799002717800517;
the composition agent is responsible for a certified rational root enclosure.
For g<=gamma/2, the kernel has at most one zero, from positive to negative;
its integral approaches a positive limit and never becomes negative.

This quantum counterexample is a scope boundary and a potential calibrated
model-class rejection experiment. It is NOT a device-independent witness of
quantumness: nonstationarity, nonselective interventions, coarse classical
detectors, unmodeled hidden states or non-Markovian descriptions can invalidate
the classical null assumptions.

Timing failure control
The pulse theorem requires that the constant suppression remain in force until
the prescribed endpoint readout. A uniform three-state cycle with clockwise
rate 4 and counterclockwise rate 1/10 has native current 13/10. Suppressing one
channel by 1/2 for 1/10 time gives destination displacement about -0.04823335.
Restoring the native generator and waiting another 2/5 time changes that
displacement to about +0.00229266. The independent test encloses both signs by
rational matrix Taylor sums and a stochastic-product error bound. State-dependent
stopping and feedback-modulated controls are also outside the constant-pulse
proof. Independent timing jitter averages positive kernels and preserves sign,
but quantitative amplitude bounds require a calibrated duration range.

Composition and energy review
All 1080 deterministic three-state, two-outcome instrument/initial-partition
instances agree with exhaustive enumeration of stable partition refinements.
The claimed coarsest interface is minimal only among deterministic Markov
interfaces refining the supplied partition, valid for every initial state.
Equal local preparation marginals do not suffice for composition: correlated
00/11 and anticorrelated 01/10 preparations have identical fair-bit marginals
and disjoint joint-readout distributions.

The fixed-cut energy allocation follows from the declared contract
epsilon_e=delta_e+sqrt(q_e^3/E_e), with positive q_e and acquisition energy E_e.
For a cut with Q=sum q_e, D=sum delta_e and desired error A>D, its optimum is
Q^3/(A-D)^2. The support identity
    Q^3/(A-D)^2 = sup_{t>=0}[3t^2 Q-2t^3(A-D)]
justifies min-cut lower bounds. Independent checks compare 105 supporting
bounds on 35 seeded graphs with exhaustive cut optima. The PARTITION reduction
also passes all 340 instances of length one through four with entries one
through four. Its proof establishes weak NP-hardness and, with rational
certificate verification, NP-completeness of the specified decision problem;
it does not establish strong NP-hardness. The precision contract is separately
assumed and calibrated, not derived as a universal thermodynamic law.

Independent statistical review of code/pulse_test.py
The predeclared orientation is early < baseline < late. There are n independent
Bernoulli records in each arm, with the baseline sample reused in the two
contrasts. Let nominal means be p0,p1,p2 and actual mean deviations satisfy
|mk-pk|<=eta_k. Under the protected classical null, at least one of
    p0-p1 <= 0,  p2-p0 <= 0
holds. For each empirical difference of two independent n-sample means,
one-sided Hoeffding gives Pr(Dhat-E Dhat >= h)<=exp(-n h^2), because the sum
of the 2n squared ranges is 2/n. Rejecting only if BOTH contrasts exceed
eta0+etak+h_alpha therefore has size at most alpha when
    n h_alpha^2 >= log(1/alpha).
This is an intersection-union test. The shared baseline does not require an
additional factor of two in the false-rejection guarantee. Searching both
orientations or selecting times/orientation from these same trial data would
need a different argument or an appropriate multiplicity correction.

Under a nominal alternative with both gaps at least g_k, the actual contrast
is at least g_k-eta0-etak. The distance above the null threshold is at least
g_k-2(eta0+etak)-h_alpha. If this is at least h_beta and
    n h_beta^2 >= log(2/beta),
the union bound on the two failure events gives power at least 1-beta.
Thus the doubled calibration penalty in the code is correct. Root was notified
to reject input g1+g2>1, which describes an impossible Bernoulli alternative.
Independent tests evaluate exact rational binomial distributions, conditioning
on the shared baseline count, under boundary-null and adverse-calibration
alternatives; they also reject a forged insufficient sample count.

A calibration failure probability delta can be added once when calibration
and design are fixed before the trials and the trial tail inequalities remain
valid conditional on that calibration history. A calibration event chosen
from the same test data does not automatically preserve this guarantee.
Repeated monitoring, optional stopping, outcome-selected durations, correlated
preparations and drift outside the fixed envelopes are not covered by this
fixed-sample test. Failure to reject is inconclusive, not proof of classicality.

General dephasing criterion review
With gamma=k(1+r) and added dephasing d>=0, let alpha=3gamma+d,
beta=gamma-d, and Omega=sqrt(4g^2-beta^2). In the underdamped case the response
kernel is exp(-alpha t)[cos(Omega t)-beta/Omega sin(Omega t)]. The first maximum
uses theta=atan2(Omega,beta) in (0,pi); the first minimum is at
t*=(pi+theta)/Omega. Its value is
    [alpha-beta-2g exp(-alpha t*)]/[alpha^2+Omega^2].
Later minima increase. For 2g<=|beta|, either the kernel stays positive
(beta<=0) or has at most one positive-to-negative zero followed by convergence
of the integral to a positive limit (beta>0). No sign reversal occurs then.
This independently confirms the producer's stated dephasing extension,
including the atan2 branch that is essential when beta<0.

Short-pulse magnitude and regenerative extension review
For a singleton paired record Z, exact stationary preparation gives
    E Z^2 = 2 pi_j (1-P'_jj(t)) - E Z.
Under |J|<=Jmax and endpoint exit rates<=Lambda, its variance is bounded by
min(1,[2 pmax Lambda+(1-r)Jmax]t). The response-kernel bound gives estimator
bias at most Jmax Lambda t for Zbar/[(1-r)t]. Choosing
t=epsilon/(2Jmax Lambda), assigning half the error to this bias, and using
Bernstein with centered bound 2 gives the producer's O(epsilon^-3) sufficient
record count. Total active pulse time is O(epsilon^-2); fixed preparation and
readout cost still multiplies O(epsilon^-3) records. This is an upper guarantee,
not a sample complexity lower bound or a universal resource law.
An independent exact CTMC test computes the actual paired mean and second
moment and compares them to these bounds at three target precisions.

For a regenerative coarse entrance extension, the required statement is the
factorization of the joint finite-entrance law:
    Pr_{nu_A}(tau_B in dt, X_{tau_B}=x) = F(dt) nu_B(x).
The distribution F may be defective if hitting B is not certain. Generic
agreement of the unconditional entrance-state marginal with nu_B is not enough.
With that factorization and no B occupation before entrance, the strong Markov
proof and strict positive survival convolution remain valid.
