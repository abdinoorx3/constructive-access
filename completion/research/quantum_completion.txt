DRIVEN-QUBIT RESPONSE AND A CLASSICAL SELECTIVE-PULSE BOUNDARY
Abdinoor / 2D Labs / 12 October 2026

Status

Analytical result with exact rational computational certificates. No laboratory
data, external peer review, historical-priority claim, or thermodynamic heat
interpretation is supplied. The intended comparison is the classical stationary
finite-CTMC single-edge selective-suppression theorem developed in this phase.
This is a conditional separation of model classes, not device-independent
certification of quantum behavior. Composition/intertwining support remains in
composition.txt; it should not distract from this sharper physical result.

1. Model and observable

Use the basis states A=|0>, B=|1>, Pauli operators X,Y,Z, hbar=1, and
D[L](rho)=L rho L^dagger - {L^dagger L,rho}/2. Let sigma_plus=|1><0|
and sigma_minus=|0><1|. The native generator is

  L(rho) = -i[g X,rho]
           + sum_e a_e D[sigma_plus](rho)
           + sum_e b_e D[sigma_minus](rho)
           + d D[Z](rho),

where g>=0, d>=0, and sum_e a_e=sum_e b_e=K>0. All these are rates
or angular frequencies in a declared time unit. The native stationary state
rho_*=I/2 is used as the preparation, independently for each record. The selected
physical jump channel has rates (a,b), with 0<=a,b<=K, and native directed
channel current J=(a-b)/2. The Hamiltonian contributes zero current at rho_*.

At time zero retain a fraction r in [0,1) of this channel's two rates, leaving
the Hamiltonian and every other rate unchanged. Let eta=1-r and

  Gamma = 2K-eta(a+b) > 0,       gamma=Gamma/4.

The post-pulse generator is a valid finite-dimensional GKSL generator with
nonnegative rates. When r>0 and all native rates are positive, no channel is
deleted. The observed response is

  Delta_B(t)=Tr(|1><1| rho(t))-1/2.

The jump basis is not the Hamiltonian energy basis. These are driven,
engineered reservoirs. A thermal-bath/local-detailed-balance interpretation is
not assumed, and a jump count is not automatically a work or heat meter.

2. General pulse reduction

Put z=Tr(Z rho), y=Tr(Y rho), and x=Tr(X rho). Native preparation gives
x(0)=y(0)=z(0)=0. After the pulse, x remains zero and

  d/dt [z;y] = A [z;y] + [2 eta J;0],
  A = [-4 gamma, 2g; -2g, -2 gamma-2d].

Consequently

  I(t)=integral_0^t (exp(Au))_11 du,
  z(t)=2 eta J I(t),       Delta_B(t)=-eta J I(t).

In particular Delta_B'(0)=-eta J. If J=0 there is no response at any time.
The expressions follow by evaluating the dissipators on the Bloch coordinates:
the population forcing is post-pulse downward rate minus upward rate =2 eta J;
the longitudinal decay is Gamma; the transverse decay is Gamma/2+2d; and the
Hamiltonian rotates the y,z components with angular frequency 2g.

3. Exact all-time phase boundary without added dephasing

Theorem. Suppose d=0 and J != 0. Define q=g/gamma. There exists a time t>0 at
which Delta_B(t) has the opposite sign to Delta_B'(0) if and only if

  q > q_c,

where q_c>1 is the unique solution of

  log q_c = 3 [pi+atan sqrt(4q_c^2-1)] / sqrt(4q_c^2-1).

The exact rational enclosure produced by the code is

  4.57066453253 < q_c < 4.57066453254.

For q>1/2 put Omega=sqrt(4g^2-gamma^2). Then

  I(t) = {2gamma + exp(-3gamma t)
           [-2gamma cos(Omega t)
            +(Omega+3gamma^2/Omega) sin(Omega t)]}
         /(4g^2+8gamma^2).

The first positive local minimum occurs at

  t_* = [pi+atan(Omega/gamma)]/Omega,

and is

  I(t_*)=[2gamma-2g exp(-3gamma t_*)]/(4g^2+8gamma^2).

When this quantity is negative it is the global minimum on [0,infinity), so
t_* is the greatest reversal amplitude for the fixed physical parameters.
At q=q_c the first minimum touches zero; a strictly reversed response requires
the strict inequality above. This does not optimize over all possible physical
control protocols, preparation procedures, pulse strengths, or energy costs.

Proof. Write A=-3gamma I+B, where B=[-gamma,2g;-2g,gamma] and
B^2=(gamma^2-4g^2)I. In the underdamped case,

  I'(t)=exp(-3gamma t)[cos(Omega t)-(gamma/Omega)sin(Omega t)].

Integrating gives the stated expression. Its critical points satisfy
Omega t=atan(Omega/gamma)+n pi, n>=0. The even n points are maxima and odd n
points are minima. At each odd n point, cos=-gamma/(2g) and
sin=-Omega/(2g). The first minimum therefore has the displayed value and every
subsequent minimum is larger, because its negative exponential term decays.
I(0)=0 and I(infinity)=2gamma/(4g^2+8gamma^2)>0. A negative value exists exactly
when g exp(-3gamma t_*)>gamma.

For 0<g<gamma/2 put h=sqrt(gamma^2-4g^2). Then
I'(t)=exp(-3gamma t)[cosh(ht)-(gamma/h)sinh(ht)]. This derivative has exactly
one zero, from positive to negative. Thus I rises from zero to a maximum and
then decreases to its positive limit; it never becomes negative. For g=0,
I(t)=(1-exp(-4gamma t))/(4gamma)>0. At g=gamma/2 the derivative is
exp(-3gamma t)(1-gamma t), and the same maximum/positive-limit argument applies.

For q>1/2 the negative-minimum condition becomes
log q > 3(pi+atan w)/w, w=sqrt(4q^2-1). It is impossible for q<=1.
For q>1, log q strictly increases, while (pi+atan w)/w strictly decreases:
its derivative with respect to w is
[w/(1+w^2)-pi-atan w]/w^2 <0. The left-minus-right expression is negative at
q=1 and tends to positive infinity. Hence there is exactly one crossing.
This proves all directions, including necessity, and not merely a sufficient
underdamping condition.

4. Added dephasing: exact criterion

Put alpha=3gamma+d, beta=gamma-d, and Omega=sqrt(4g^2-beta^2). If 2g>|beta|,

 I(t)={(alpha-beta)+exp(-alpha t)
       [-(alpha-beta)cos(Omega t)
        +(Omega+alpha beta/Omega)sin(Omega t)]}/(alpha^2+Omega^2).

The first minimum is at

 t_*=[pi+atan2(Omega,beta)]/Omega,
 I(t_*)=[(alpha-beta)-2g exp(-alpha t_*)]/(alpha^2+Omega^2).

Thus reversal occurs exactly when 2g exp(-alpha t_*)>alpha-beta. The atan2
branch lies in (0,pi), which is essential when d>gamma. The same critical-point
calculation gives the result; all later minima are higher. When 2g<=|beta|,
the hyperbolic derivative has no zero if beta<=0 and at most one zero from
positive to negative if beta>0. Its limiting integral is positive because
alpha-beta>0 and det(-A)=8gamma^2+8gamma d+4g^2>0. There is no reversal.

This shows exactly how calibrated pure dephasing changes the criterion. It is
not a guarantee against arbitrary non-Markovian noise, drift, or control errors.

5. Finite rational protocol and exact numerical certificates

Use native channel1(up,down)=(3kappa,kappa), channel2=(kappa,3kappa),
g=1, kappa=1/10, r=1/10, and d=0. Native J=kappa=1/10; the post-pulse
rates are up=13/100, down=31/100, Gamma=11/25, gamma=11/100, q=100/11.
All four channel rates remain positive. Exact Taylor enclosures prove:

  t=1/2:  Delta_B=-0.03416833483914909... <0,
  t=9/4:  Delta_B=+0.01523153262660271... >0,
  t=5/2:  Delta_B=+0.01439706666827132... >0.

The times 1/2 and 9/4 give a directly testable change of response sign. No
knowledge of the microscopic native current orientation is needed to reject
the same-sign consequence, provided both records share the validated native
stationary preparation and physical intervention contract. The purely
incoherent g=0 control retains the same native stationary population and
channel currents and has no sign reversal. Its full native temporal record law
is NOT the same as that of the driven qubit; no such equality is claimed.

Within the explicitly finite menu t=j/4, j=1,...,16, the time 9/4 maximizes both
the positive response and the positive response squared divided by pulse
duration. Both comparisons are made using disjoint exact response intervals.
The second objective is an acquisition-time proxy assuming the appropriate
independent-repeat precision scaling and zero additional per-record overhead.
It is not a full-energy optimum and need not remain optimal with preparation,
measurement, reset, control, bandwidth, and calibration costs included.

For a rational 2x2 matrix A and rational t>=0, the integral is computed as

  S_N=sum_{k=0}^N (A^k)_11 t^(k+1)/(k+1)!.

Let s=t||A||_infinity and choose N+3>s. Submultiplicativity gives

  |I(t)-S_N| <= t s^(N+1)/(N+2)! / [1-s/(N+3)].

This follows by bounding all omitted terms by their matrix norms and a
geometric series whose successive ratios are at most s/(N+3). At N=80 and
t=9/4 the integral radius is less than 4.1e-63. The public JSON rounds the exact
endpoints OUTWARD to 20 decimal places; the underlying routine returns full
rational centers, radii and endpoints.

The threshold bracket uses Machin's identity pi=16 atan(1/5)-4 atan(1/239),
alternating rational atan series, the positive atanh series for log q, and an
integer-square-root enclosure. Evaluation of the monotone threshold function
is strictly negative at the lower endpoint and strictly positive at the upper.
No floating-point root finder is used to certify the threshold.

6. Classical comparison and limits of interpretation

The classical theorem concerns a native stationary finite CTMC, a specified
physical edge channel i<->j whose two rates alone are multiplied by r, and
observation of the particular endpoint state j. Its all-time population shift
has the same sign as its initial derivative. This follows from the positivity
of an integrated return-minus-first-passage kernel. Its exact hypotheses must
be retained in the statement paired with this quantum calculation.

A two-outcome detector does not by itself establish that outcome B is one
microscopic classical state. If B aggregates multiple hidden classical states,
if preparation is nonstationary, if the control perturbs additional channels,
if an external controller has memory, or if its causal action is unknown, a
classical explanation need not satisfy that theorem. Consequently this is not
a device-independent quantumness witness, proof that all classical models fail,
or a thermodynamic arrow-of-time claim. It is a conditional experimental test
of a sharply stated classical selective-channel model.

The possibility of damped coherent response is established open-system physics.
Any historical novelty claim must concern the exact classical/quantum response
boundary and its constructive use, compared directly with prior work, rather
than the existence of Rabi oscillations or underdamped transients.
