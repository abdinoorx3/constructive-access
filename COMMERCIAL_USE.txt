Free use

Personal study and noncommercial projects, and genuine academic research
and teaching, are free. A Company may also use the covered material in its
business for free while its worldwide Group Revenue is at or below
US$10 million. Count the Company and all controlled or common-control
affiliates together, over the preceding 12 months, eliminating internal
transactions. For a younger Group, count actual revenue since formation.
Exactly US$10 million qualifies. There is no employee limit.

Everyone may read, retain and fork the public work, review it, reproduce
its published demonstrations and cite it. Scholarly quotation, attributed
figure reuse and distribution of scholarly articles remain permitted,
including through commercial scholarly publishers, as described in the
license. These permissions support scientific scrutiny and do not grant
unrestricted corporate product or operational use.

When a separate commercial license is needed

If Group Revenue exceeds US$10 million, contact helloworld@2dai.org before
using covered code or other protected material in company operations,
products, proprietary research, customer services or other business uses
outside the universally permitted scholarly activities. The test concerns
the whole Group's revenue, not the revenue attributable to this project.
Using a smaller contractor or an academic institution to perform the same
Company work does not bypass that test. Ordinary independent scholarly
research is not disqualified solely because a company sponsors it.

A commercial agreement can specify scope, price, term and any support.
No fee, percentage of revenue, royalty or share of profits becomes payable
merely because the work is downloaded or a business crosses the threshold.
The choices are to obtain permission for the covered use or to stop that
use. Earlier permitted use is not retroactively charged. A request for
terms is not itself a commercial license.

What is outside this licensing requirement

The license does not reserve mathematical results, scientific ideas,
methods, facts or independent implementations that copy no protected
expression. It does not automatically license or restrict every output
of the software. The original experimental data remains CC BY 4.0, and
other third-party material retains its own terms. Applicable statutory
exceptions remain available. No patent exclusivity is claimed.
