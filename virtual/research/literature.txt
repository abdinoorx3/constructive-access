VIRTUAL LABORATORY: LITERATURE AND ASSUMPTION AUDIT
Audit initiated 2026-10-12 UTC; source access timestamps in sources.json.

1. What simulation establishes
The planned experiment is a verification and operating-characteristic study.
It can establish that an independently generated trajectory law agrees with
an analytical model and that a statistical procedure has expected coverage
or power over declared simulations. It cannot establish that an actual
apparatus is stationary, Markovian, correctly reset, noninvasive, or accurately
calibrated. Oberkampf–Trucano2002 distinguishes numerical verification from
physical validation. Morris–White–Crowther2019 recommends separating aims,
data-generating mechanisms, estimands, methods and performance measures, and
reporting Monte Carlo uncertainty. These are established practices.

Production design, seeds and criteria must be frozen before held-out tests.
Pilot-selected contrasts need independent test data. Pseudorandom seeds and
repeated simulations make results reproducible; their statistical status is
conditional on the random-generator model and simulated data law. A numerical
matrix exponential is not an independently certified interval calculation.
Analytical and separately coded trajectory cross-checks are useful because
they can expose common implementation errors, not because agreement proves
that nature follows either implementation.

A zero observed false-positive count is not proof of zero false-positive
probability. For R independent simulation repetitions and zero failures,
a one-sided 95% exact binomial upper limit is 1−0.05^(1/R). With R=300 this
is about0.995%; with R=1000 about0.299%. The theorem provides universal
coverage under its assumptions; simulations test selected implementations
and regimes. Report uncertainty for power differences using paired outcomes
when comparing methods on the same synthetic records.

2. Closest thermodynamic methodology
Fritz–Ertel–Seifert2025, PRE111044106, arxiv2412.04102v2, directly studies
finite spatial/temporal resolution and finite samples for three entropy
estimators. Its SecIII and conclusion explicitly warn that empirical plug-in
estimates can exceed true entropy production. It simulates a four-state
network and an augmented Michaelis–Menten network. The paper's estimates
require currents or waiting-time events; our fresh binary endpoint contract
lacks those observations. It is a methodological comparison, not an automatic
matched-data competitor. Its temporal coarse-graining still records the
transition sequence; this also differs from missed-event readout.

Skinner–Dunkel2021 PNAS already establishes inference of hidden dissipation
from apparently time-symmetric observed dynamics and applies it to biological
data. Their PRL2021 waiting-time paper supplies a second close baseline.
Do not frame nonzero inference from symmetric records or biological relevance
as a new discovery here. Bebon–Godec2023 gives independent-realization kinetic
first-passage confidence bounds; finite-count kinetic inference is established.

For the current endpoint menu, negative-correlation floor, monotonic revival
constraint, full spectral-moment cone, and a fixed scalar polynomial contrast
are genuinely matched reductions of the same data. A method's improvement
should be reported against these at identical access, confidence level,
record count and either mean exposure or total physical duration as declared.
Increasing the amount of measurement information and optimizing observables
already improves thermodynamic bounds in Dechant–Sasa2021 PRX11041061; their
current–noncurrent correlation TUR has a different observation model.

3. Timing and exposure
The reversible spectral-mixture representation and complete monotonicity are
standard (Aldous–Fill onlinebook Ch3§3.5; Schilling–Song–Vondracek2012 for
Bernstein functions and Bochner subordination). A proposed universal clock
criterion says Eexp(−lambda T_k) must be a Hausdorff moment sequence for every
lambda≥0; sufficiency is positive mixing of elementary spectral modes and
necessity follows from symmetric two-state chains. This is an operational
closure result derived from existing spectral theory, not a newly discovered
subordination theory.

Three clock models must remain distinct:
- IID nonnegative increments: Eexp(Q sumX_j)=(Eexp(QX))^k, preserving the same
  finite stochastic state space and the common-channel dimension contract.
- Common per-record scale T_k=kV: the equilibrium spectral moment cone survives
  by x=exp(−lambda V), even for broad V, but a common n-state channel need not.
- Arbitrary settingwise laws T_k: neither condition follows merely from bounded
  jitter or equal mean timing; an explicit profile/outercone test is needed.

A two-state scalar exponential with a two-point random scale becomes a sum of
two distinct exponentials plus the constant mode, yielding ordinary temporal
rank3 generically. This illustrates why an unobserved clock can contribute
memory resources even if the underlying system has onlytwo states.

Measured timestamps are observations and retaining/discarding them changes
the data contract. Entropy lowerbounds must divide by physical expected lag,
not nominal lag if the two differ. Mean duration is not a pathwise exposure
cap. State-dependent clocks and postselection can break the assumed spectral
comparison; ordinary camera integration is not automatically a random
endpoint observation. Identical nondestructive block detectors require their
own proof and the time-reversal convention matters.

4. Dependent records and reset
The proposed bound with |E[Z_i|past,K_i=k]−p_k|≤delta_k, boundedZ_i, predictable
setting choice and deterministicper-settingquotas is conditionalHoeffding
plus a deterministic drift budget. Its bias penalty is delta_k, not Ndelta_k.
This is established martingale concentration applied to an explicit apparatus
contract. Hoeffding1963 is foundational; Howardetal2021 gives a muchbroader
time-uniform conditional-mgf framework, and Paulin2015 establishes concentration
under Markov mixing and spectral assumptions.

Uniform conditionalbias can be justified by externally known worst-case
mixing after a blind resetgap; it is not licensed by a fitted autocorrelation
time from the same shortrecord. The Doeblinminorization/spectralgap assumptions
must be explicit. Fixedquotas differ from arbitrary data-dependentgroupcounts
or stopping; an optionalstopping claim needs time-uniform confidence or a
separate valid argument. Clopper–Pearson confidence is suitable for iid
Bernoulli observations or independent simulation repetitions, not arbitrary
correlatedphysicalrecords.

5. Dimension witness and physical practicality
Hendrychetal2012 demonstrates actual dimensionwitness experiments with
photons. Proctoretal2020 experimentally detects and tracks processor drift.
Nielsenetal2021 GST jointly characterizes preparations, gates and readout.
These are established approaches, not evidence that our apparatus assumptions
hold. GST and fulltomography estimate a different object with richer access,
so resource comparisons must match inference task as well as sample count.

Fixed preparation/readout errors can be absorbed into unknown fixedstate and
response functions; fixed drive errors change the common dynamics. Such
errors may reduce power while preserving a theorem's null coverage. Setting-
dependent changes, detector memory and quasistatic latentvariables can
invalidate the declaredcommon-channel/state-number model or add hiddenmemory.
Randomizing acquisitionorder helpsdiagnosis but doesnot mathematically
eliminate arbitrary drift. Demonstrating a one-state detector can counterfeit
a desired probability trace under settingdependenterrors is an important
outside-contract control, not a falsepositive under the theorem.

Every numerical rate,decoherence ratio,errorprobability in this phase is
illustrative. No empiricallycalibrated hardwareperformance range is adopted.
No conversion of dimensionless gamma/omega into a hardwarefeasibility claim
is justified without platform-specific coherence, drive, readout, reset and
timing data under the exact intended protocol.

6. Fixed contrast and allocation
For a polynomialcertified linearconstraint a·q≤b on reversibleendpointlaws,
a predeclaredcontrast can avoid the simultaneouscoordinateconfidence penalty.
WeightedHoeffding,Pinsker andweightedCauchy are established. The resulting
bound sigma≥8C²/(sum|a_k|sqrt(t_k))² andits associatedclockweights are a useful
specific consequence requiring an ownproof; no exact priorformula match was
located in this limitedaudit. Optimal sampling allocation proportional to
|a_k|/sqrt(c_k) is the standardcost-adjusted Neyman/stratified-allocation
calculus applied to boundedvariance proxies, not a newallocation principle.
Choosing acontrast from testdata without accounting for selection would
invalidate the singlepredeclaredcontrastconfidence guarantee.

The evidence claim supported by this phase is an explicitly scoped operational
and statistical improvement under testedcontracts. Historical originality,
universalphysicalvalidity andactualapparatusvalidation remain separate tasks.
