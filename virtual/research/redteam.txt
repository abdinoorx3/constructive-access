Abdinoor / 2D Labs, 12 October 2026.

Status

The apparatus, conditional-sampling and fixed-contrast statements below survive
independent derivation under their declared contracts. Simulations test those
contracts and implementations; they are not laboratory validation. Independent
test outputs are in results/independent.json. Historical originality is a
separate question; conditional Hoeffding, positive spectral calculus, Pinsker
and Cauchy-Schwarz are established ingredients.

1. Independent timing and exposure audit

For reversible stationary Q, an endpoint correlation is a nonnegative mixture
of exp(-lambda t), with spectral mass at most one. Thus the stated universal
clock criterion is correct: for every lambda>=0 the sampled Laplace profile
must be a probability moment vector on[0,1]. Sufficiency is closure under
positive mixtures, padded at0; necessity follows from a symmetric two-state
chain with that single relaxation rate. Testing a finite rate grid does not
establish this universal premise.

Common IID nonnegative increments imply one effective channel
P=E exp(QX), with E exp(Q sum_{j=1}^k X_j)=P^k. They preserve both reversible
correlation structure and finite-state common-channel factorization. A random
scale held for an entire waiting period instead gives E exp(Q kV). It preserves
the thermodynamic moment cone but can increase the minimum common-channel
dimension. The independently checked two-state example has occupation
p_k=1/2+(2^-k+4^-k)/4 and a strictly positive3x3 Hankel determinant. The
controller's persistent scale variable cannot be omitted from a memory claim.

Mean timing does not suffice. For equilibrium correlation exp(-t), let T1=1
and T2 be .1 or3.9 with equal probability. Mean times are1 and2, but the
averaged correlation increases. The timing module additionally shows that even
stochastically ordered clocks can violate higher moment constraints.

For identical disjoint length-L detector blocks with reversal-invariant bounded
path functional F, conditional expectation at the left block's final state
equals the expectation at the right block's initial state. Reversibility is
needed for that equality. Therefore the gap-T correlation is <g,exp(ST)g>_pi
with |g|<=1. The full compared path lasts2L+T, not T. Independent detector
noise conditional on physical paths and noninvasiveness remain essential.

An important new adversarial control was contributed during this review.
Take an equilibrium two-state correlation exp(-lambda t); each block reports
a fair random choice of its two endpoint signs. With center/start separation
kDelta>=L, the correlation is A x^k, where
A=cosh(lambda L/2)^2>1 and x=exp(-lambda Delta).
Then C2<C1^2, violating the normalized Hausdorff moment condition even though
blocks are disjoint and the physical process is equilibrium. In terms of the
actual inter-block gap, the amplitude is instead
((1+exp(-lambda L))/2)^2<=1 and the theorem is restored. Consequently finite
exposure is not automatically a harmless timing error, and the time coordinate
must be correct. The timing chapter now includes an exact rational instance.

The independently checked antisymmetric-block example also yields negative
equilibrium correlation. This is outside the reversal-symmetric block contract.
For nonlinear block functionals the stationary detector mean under the actual
process need not equal its mean under the reversible reference. The safe
comparison is the mean-unrestricted cone unless a reference mean is justified.

2. Conditional means, overlap and mixing

Fixed quotas with predictable setting routing admit the stated concentration
bound. If each current record has conditional mean in p_k+/-delta_k given the
entire past and the selected setting, center each increment at that conditional
mean and apply the conditional Hoeffding lemma. The predictable indicator of
setting k gives exactly n_k increments at termination. Adding n_k delta_k
controls deviation from the fixed target p_k; a union bound supplies the joint
box. No independence assumption is needed under this stronger conditional
premise. Data-dependent stopping or selective record inclusion is not covered.

Marginal stationarity is not that premise. The extreme control Z_i=B for one
fair bit B has Bernoulli(.5) marginals but a sample mean always0 or1. A
four-state stationary equilibrium process in the production simulation gives
a less artificial version: shared endpoints retain a slowly changing sector,
causing severe undercoverage and false equilibrium exclusions if declared IID.

A uniform hidden-state total-variation mixing envelope controls the complete
next pair record by Markov-kernel contraction. The additive-reversibilization
spectral gap gives the stated L2 contraction and uniform TV upper bound
(1/2)sqrt((1-pi_min)/pi_min) exp(-gap*blind_time). It is valid for nonreversible
Q as well, provided the gap is that of its stationary symmetrization. This is
an externally justified contract, not something established by fitting the
unknown target data. Detector/controller memory must be included in the mixed
state or separately reset. Calibration failure allowances must refer to valid
external/pretest contract events, not postselected favorable observations.

The blind-gap correction has a major real cost. The frozen example compares
approximately3.6million model-time units with1800 units of pair evolution.
Fresh stationary preparations have their own unmodeled cost. These outcomes
do not support a claim that waiting is universally efficient.

3. Fixed-contrast inference and time optimization

For a fixed coefficient vector a and rigorous reversible support b, write
C=a dot p-b>0. For every reversible q, a dot(p-q)>=C. Bernoulli Pinsker and
weighted Cauchy-Schwarz imply
  J_w >= 2 C^2 / sum_k(a_k^2/w_k).
The stationary path comparison therefore gives
  sigma >= 8 C^2 /[(sum_k w_k t_k)(sum_k a_k^2/w_k)].
The denominator is minimized by w_k proportional to |a_k|/sqrt(t_k), with
minimum (sum_k |a_k|sqrt(t_k))^2. Zero coefficients can be omitted. This is an
optimization of a mathematical mixture; it is not a claim that the existing
physical sample allocation had those proportions.

For fixed quotas n_k, the one-sided contrast radius is
sqrt[.5 log(1/alpha) sum_k a_k^2/n_k]. A conditional-mean or calibration radius
delta_k costs sum_k |a_k|delta_k. This proof also works with predictable
fixed-quota routing. The contrast, support and quotas must be fixed before
held-out data; choosing them from an independent pilot is valid. Optimizing
the virtual mixture after this one simultaneous contrast event is harmless.
Optimizing the coefficient vector after looking at those same outcomes is not.

Durations must bound the expected full compared path for the target record
laws, including exposure blocks. Blind mixing before a stationary pair is an
additional experimental cost, even if it is not part of the path used for the
stationary endpoint divergence comparison.

The strongest time-independent Pinsker-derived record-KL bound uses rational
weights |a_k|/sum|a| and denominator(sum|a|)^2. This improvement was requested
and incorporated. Review also identified a narrowed-reference-mean issue in
the first contrast API: its preparation/housekeeping interpretation needed
the same explicit stationary-reference calibration guard as the fourth
edition. That guard is now present; default mean-unrestricted outputs were
valid throughout.

The refined cubic contrast has a useful exact meaning. For0<=s<=1,
  a_s=(-s^2,2s,-1), b_s=-(1-s)^2/2
is a tight reversible support certificate because -x(x-s)^2<=0 on[0,1].
Writing C_k=2p_k-1, its violation is
  .5(-s^2 C1+2s C2-C3).
Its unconstrained optimum is s=C2/C1 when C1>0, giving
.5(C2^2/C1-C3), a Hankel log-convexity violation. An independent pilot may
propose that location and then freeze a rational contrast. The second-loop
contract instead freezes the particular rational s=2/3 before fresh outcomes;
knowledge of first-loop outcomes is appropriately disclosed.

4. Simulator independence and interpretation

The thermodynamic laboratory generates ring states from independent forward
and backward Poisson jump counts, then generates readouts from physical
endpoint states. The inference receives agreement counts, not true currents
or hidden states. Independent tests compare its probabilities with matrix
exponentials, and compare its prepared-equilibrium direct Gillespie paths
with an independently evaluated two-state transition law. This avoids using
the inference's certificate formula to manufacture its observations.

The dimension laboratory uses Bloch evolution and separately checks a full
Lindblad representation. Independent review supplies a third calculation via
Rodrigues rotations with depolarization. Its ordinary-noise engine averages
the one-step channel and raises that common channel to successive powers;
its retained shots draw a fresh error label at each step. Quasi-static labels
are explicitly separated as controller memory. Fixed errors can reduce power
without invalidating the common-channel theorem; setting-dependent detector
response can manufacture the witness on a one-state object and is retained
as an outside-contract control.

The saved dimension shot stream is an independently generated stream, not the
aggregate binomial Monte Carlo data. Its post-generation random permutation
of independent shots does not establish drift-robust physical acquisition.
Record counts and physical evolution durations are reconstructed in the
independent tests. Shape/integer checks were requested for plan certification,
classification and numerical power; missing settings can no longer be silently
truncated by zip. Explicit grid-size propagation was also corrected.

5. Comparison quality and unresolved empirical work

The old frozen full-cone score is not the best prior-framework baseline.
The root added numerically proposed optimized scores with exact continuous
checking. The fixed-contrast procedure sacrifices unrestricted post-data
contrast selection for better targeted concentration; it is not universally
superior. Records, timing and access are matched within each held-out dataset.

The dimension pilot costs260,000 records in addition to18,300 production
records. Review requested an equal-total-budget comparator, now implemented.
It shows ordinary extra averaging wins some regimes, while common-step
retiming repairs phase-offset failures that the original fixed region cannot
handle well. There is no universal total-cost optimization win. Reuse of a
pilot over future batches additionally assumes stable apparatus behavior.

Monte Carlo intervals quantify uncertainty in simulated rejection frequencies.
Dimension and thermodynamic laboratories report marginal Clopper-Pearson
intervals; the dependence demonstration explicitly labels Wilson intervals.
Zero simulated rejections are not zero false-positive risk. These numerical
intervals and solver checks are distinct from the exact rational certificates.

The remaining physical bottlenecks are genuine experimental justification of
stationarity or preparation budgets, noninvasive fixed observation, detector
memory control, timing compatibility and physical reversal parity. None is
established merely by a successful virtual experiment or a fitted Markov model.

6. Exact confidence-box countermodels and a remaining calibration lever

The new reversible_box_witness API constructs rational x in(0,1) and A in[0,1]
such that q_k=(1+A*x^k)/2 lies in every reported interval. Independent review
verifies the asserted probabilities using matrix exponentiation of the symmetric
two-state generator with rate -log(x)/(2*Delta), stationary weights(1/2,1/2),
and independent stochastic detector means(+sqrt(A),-sqrt(A)). This is a genuine
zero-stationary-dissipation model at deterministic indexed lags. Tests also bind
the exact witness to its lag/interval contract and reject altered probabilities,
amplitude, decay and incomplete settings. An unsuccessful rational-grid search
is correctly reported as inconclusive, never as proof of infeasibility.

The consequence must remain precise: a positive bound cannot hold uniformly
over every process represented by that whole rectangular admissible set when
it contains this countermodel. This does not prove that the numerical box has
destroyed the counts or that every inference receiving the box must fail. A
different simultaneous confidence construction, including the fixed-contrast
method, can use the same data differently. The midpoint can retain essentially
the original count information. The obstruction concerns the use of the entire
box as the asserted confidence set, not an information-theoretic loss from its
file representation.

For a known nondegenerate clock distribution, the same q_k is always a point
in the outer reversible moment cone, but need not be realized by this two-state
generator under that exact clock law. In particular a common random scale
produces E[x^(kV)], not x^k. The API correctly distinguishes comparison-cone
membership from the deterministic-clock physical countermodel. A calibrated
detector restriction can also exclude the proposed stochastic detector; no
additional detector calibration is assumed silently.

A promising next experiment, not implemented in this release, is independent
zero-lag paired-readout calibration of the stationary detector second moment
E_pi[g^2]. It constrains the total decaying spectral mass and may exclude some
low-amplitude two-state countermodels that the present mean-unrestricted cone
allows. For fixed noninvasive endpoint observations, Q and its additive
reversibilization share pi and g, so this calibration transfers to the comparison
model. Independent uncertainty must be carried into the inference. For nonlinear
finite-exposure observations the analogous block statistic does not automatically
transfer and needs a new argument. This is an additional access/calibration
assumption, not an improvement obtainable from the current three counts alone.
