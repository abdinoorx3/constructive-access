Abdinoor / 2D Labs. 2026-10-12.
Status: exact concentration/physical-envelope derivations; numerical trajectory
stress test under a frozen synthetic contract. No physical laboratory evidence.

1. FAILURE ADDRESSED

Stationary one-record marginal probabilities do not make consecutive records
independent, and do not make their conditional means stationary. In particular,
overlapping or shared-endpoint pairs from one trajectory can have severely
dependent counts. An iid confidence calculation can then falsely reject a
reversible physical system. Conditional-mean control is a sufficient replacement
that is weaker than independence and can be supported by an explicit mixing gap.
Its error penalty is an additive bias delta, not record_count times delta.

2. EXACT STATISTICAL CONTRACT AND THEOREM

There are m settings and deterministic quotas n_k>0, with N=sum n_k. Before
outcome Z_i is generated, select an unfinished setting K_i using past records
and optionally fresh randomization. Reveal the selection before generating Z_i.
No outcome-dependent acceptance, rejection or skipping of the current record
is allowed. The procedure stops after every quota is filled, so N is fixed.
Each Z_i lies in[0,1]; for count data it is a binary whole-record indicator.

For fixed target probabilities p_k, let mu_i=E[Z_i | all preceding information,
selected setting]. The physical contract requires almost surely
  |mu_i-p_{K_i}| <= delta_{K_i}.
This conditions on all recorded past information, not merely the previous
state average or an unconditional stationary law. Let p_hat_k=S_k/n_k.
For statistical radii r_k satisfying
  2 n_k r_k^2 >= log(2m/alpha),
the simultaneous confidence statement is
  P(for every k: |p_hat_k-p_k| <= r_k+delta_k) >= 1-alpha.
The radius remains valid with arbitrary dependence obeying this conditional
mean contract. It is not a theorem about arbitrary stationary trajectories.

Proof. For each setting k and real lambda, conditional Hoeffding's lemma gives
  E exp(lambda 1{K_i=k}(Z_i-mu_i) -lambda^2 1{K_i=k}/8 | past) <=1.
The centered random variable has conditional range length1. Multiplication
gives a nonnegative exponential supermartingale. Since the final number of
selected records is the deterministic n_k, exponential Markov inequality and
lambda=4r_k give either tail <=exp(-2n_k r_k^2). Add the accumulated drift,
whose magnitude is at most n_k delta_k, and divide by n_k. A union bound over
both tails and m settings proves the result. The proof accommodates predictable
setting selection because the setting indicator is known before its outcome.
It does not justify stopping when a confidence result first looks favorable.

A uniform whole-record apparatus radius eta_k is added to the half-width. If
the required external calibration/conditional-mean envelope holds except on an
event of externally certified probability beta, total failure is at most
alpha+beta. This is not a license to postselect apparently well-behaved data or
infer beta from a fitted target model without a separate confidence argument.

The implementation checks 2n_k r_k^2 against an outward rational enclosure of
log(2m/alpha), inherited from the exact continuous-moment module. Its radius
designer computes an outward dyadic square root by integer arithmetic. It then
passes the simultaneous probability box to the full continuous reversible
moment certificate. Scores may be chosen after this simultaneous box is
constructed because the deterministic certificate minimizes over the entire
box. A fixed-score direct test can use a tighter directional confidence bound,
but requires its scores to be fixed before the held-out outcomes.

3. PHYSICAL CHRONOLOGY THAT JUSTIFIES CONDITIONAL BIAS

One valid sequential apparatus chronology is:
  finish previous pair;
  select next setting and a blind mixing gap using only past information;
  evolve without observing during that gap;
  read the starting detector, wait the chosen lag, read the ending detector;
  retain the whole same/not-same pair indicator and repeat.
The detector must be noninvasive for the retained generator interpretation.
Any hidden detector/controller memory must also mix, reset, or be included in
the hidden state governed by the envelope.

If Q has stationary pi and the full hidden-state semigroup satisfies
  sup_x TV(delta_x exp(G Q),pi) <= delta,
then the state at the next pair start, conditional on all pre-gap history, is
within delta of pi. The complete pair is a stochastic channel of its initial
state, including independent readout noise and the lag. Total-variation
contraction therefore bounds its conditional mean shift by delta. Observing
the first member is part of the pair; the conditional expectation in the
statistical theorem is taken before this current pair, not after its first bit.

Two checkable envelope forms are implemented:
(a) Doeblin: if P_h(x,.)>=epsilon nu(.) uniformly over hidden x, then
    sup_x TV(P_{jh}(x,.),pi)<=(1-epsilon)^j.
(b) Additive-reversibilization gap: if pi_min<=min_x pi_x and
    gap(S)>=lambda>0 for S=(Q+Q^dagger)/2, then
    sup_x TV(P_G(x,.),pi)
      <= min(1, .5 sqrt((1-pi_min)/pi_min) exp(-lambda G)).
    Q itself need not be reversible. For a zero-mean density perturbation,
    differentiating its squared L2(pi) norm gives the quadratic form of S;
    the spectral gap contracts that norm at rate lambda. Cauchy--Schwarz gives
    total variation, and a point mass has L2 norm sqrt(1/pi_x-1).
The exponential upper bound uses an exact reciprocal of a finite positive
Taylor lower bound for exp(lambda G). The square root is rounded outward.

These are external model/calibration contracts. A mixing rate estimated only
inside a favored low-dimensional model does not bound the mixing rate of all
hidden models compatible with coarse evidence. The routines validate algebra,
not that the actual unknown apparatus has the asserted uniform envelope.

4. FROZEN VIRTUAL-LABORATORY CONTRACT

contracts/sampling.json was written before the production simulation. Seed
918273;4000 independent replicate trajectories;600 records at lag1 followed
by600 records at lag2; alpha1/20; statistical radius61/1000. No postproduction
retuning occurred. Each replicate uses an equilibrium four-state CTMC with
state(sector,bit), sector flip rate1/1000, bit flip rates1/50 in sector0 and2
in sector1. Every transition has the same reverse rate. Stationary pi is uniform
and stationary entropy production is exactly zero. The binary detector reads
the bit perfectly and noninvasively.

The stationary same-outcome targets are approximately
  p(lag1)=0.744650555321741,
  p(lag2)=0.7305180840365182.
Although each shared-endpoint record has the correct stationary marginal,
slow sector switching strongly correlates its conditional agreement rate.

Three chronologies use identical per-menu record counts:
(a) stationary initialization once, then reuse each pair's endpoint as the next
    start; analyze the same counts both naively(delta0) and honestly(delta3/4);
(b) fixed initial state0, then a blind gap3000 before every pair, with
    declared conditional bias1/400;
(c) ideal fresh stationary preparation before every pair, with delta0.
For(b), Dirichlet comparison against independent bit flips at rate1/50 and
sector flips at rate1/1000 proves spectral gap>=1/500. With pi_min1/4, the
exact uniform gap envelope is below0.002146663<1/400.

CTMC snapshot transitions were generated from matrix exponentials, an exact
transition-law simulator up to the stated numerical solver precision. Pair
semigroups were independently checked by81-term uniformization; discrepancies
were <=5.28e-15. The3000-gap semigroup was independently checked by symmetric
eigendecomposition with max discrepancy1.19e-13. The lag allocation, statistical
radii, model, number of replicates and seed were frozen before generation.

5. HELD-OUT RESULTS, INCLUDING FAILED REGIME AND TIME COST

Shared-endpoint pairs treated as iid:
  simultaneous probability-box coverage0.023;
  approximate95% Wilson Monte Carlo interval[0.018792,0.028124];
  false reversible-monotonicity exclusions0.247;
  approximate95% Wilson interval[0.233882,0.260604].
The advertised95% confidence box therefore covers only2.3% of replicate truths,
and an equilibrium process is falsely excluded24.7% of the time.

The same dependent counts with the honest delta3/4 correction:
  coverage1.0, approximate95% Wilson lower0.9990406;
  zero false exclusions, approximate95% Wilson upper0.00095945.
The intervals are deliberately broad; honest inference can be uninformative.

Blind-gap design with the externally proved delta1/400 correction:
  coverage0.999, approximate95% Wilson interval[0.997431,0.999612];
  zero false exclusions, approximate95% Wilson upper0.00095945.

Ideal independent stationary pairs:
  coverage0.99875, approximate95% Wilson interval[0.997077,0.999466];
  zero false exclusions, approximate95% Wilson upper0.00095945.
The conservative Hoeffding boxes need not attain exactly95% coverage.

Measured model-evolution time per replicate is1800 without gaps and3601800
with blind gaps. The ideal independent-pair benchmark does not model the
duration or energy of its external stationary preparation, so its1800 is not
a complete hardware-resource comparison. The blind-gap design is sufficient,
not time-optimal. Markov-specific concentration or calibrated regenerative
sampling may use the available time more efficiently; neither is established
by the present benchmark.

The false-exclusion decision uses lower confidence p(lag2)>upper confidence
p(lag1), which is impossible for the complete stationary reversible moment
class. It is a valid necessary-condition rejection only when the probability
box has its declared coverage. No exact binomial test is applied to the
dependent records. Wilson intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty and are
approximate, distinct from the exact rational inference thresholds.

6. SOFTWARE, REPLAY AND POSITIONING

code/sampling.py supplies confidence_radius, confidence_box, certify_counts,
doeblin_mix_bound, spectral_mix_bound, and the frozen simulator. The JSON output
retains the contract SHA256, per-scenario raw-count histograms with exact
multiplicities, count-array digests, coverage/false-exclusion summaries, model
times, solver cross-checks and mixing certificate. Invalid quotas, inadequate
radii, invalid probabilities and unsupported stopping designs are rejected.
The caller cannot make a false physical assertion true by passing a validator.

Producer checks:9 tests pass, including exact arithmetic, bias added once,
agreement with the old iid certificate at delta0, loss of a spurious positive
bound at the honest bias, adaptive predictable-routing conditional mgf,
mixing for an irreversible cycle, and a stationary-marginal counterexample.

Underlying concentration and mixing results are established mathematics.
Relevant sources: Hoeffding1963 (JASA58,13--30); Howard et al.2021
(Annals of Statistics49,1055--1080, DOI10.1214/20-AOS1991); Paulin2015
(Electronic Journal of Probability20 no.79, DOI10.1214/EJP.v20-4039).
The contribution here is integrating explicit sampling chronology, dependency
correction and exact physical inference, and demonstrating a consequential
failure/correction in a reproducible equilibrium virtual laboratory. Historical
novelty of that integration is not assumed; no new concentration theorem is
claimed.
