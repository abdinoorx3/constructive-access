1. Precise question
Which randomized schedules and finite-duration detectors can use the existing
classical realization and stationary entropy certificates without creating a
false witness at equilibrium? Timing laws that preserve the thermodynamic cone
need not preserve a fixed hidden-state dimension. Likewise an apparently
symmetric exposure detector can violate a normalized moment constraint if the
wrong time coordinate is used.

2. Universal clock criterion
Fix positive integer settings k=1,...,K, and probability laws T_k>=0 independent
of hidden dynamics and detector noise. Write
  H_K={ (integral x^k mu(dx))_k : mu a probability measure on [0,1] }.
The following are equivalent:
(a) For every finite stationary reversible continuous-time Markov chain and every
fixed noninvasive binary endpoint detector with independent endpoint noise, the
correlations C_k=E[Y_0 Y_{T_k}] belong to H_K.
(b) For every lambda>=0 the Laplace profile
    ( E exp(-lambda T_k) )_k belongs to H_K.
Sufficiency: a reversible chain has a positive spectral expansion
C(t)=sum_l b_l exp(-lambda_l t), b_l>=0, sum b_l<=1. Substitute the representing
measure for each Laplace profile and put unused mass at x=0. Only positive k
are used. Necessity: take the stationary symmetric two-state chain with both
rates lambda/2 and detector +/-1. Its correlation is exactly exp(-lambda t).
This criterion also handles irregular finite index sets by replacing H_K with
the corresponding sampled moment set. Checking finitely many lambda values
numerically does not certify the universal clock contract.

If a calibrated stationary mean m is available for a fixed endpoint detector,
its atom m^2 at x=1 is retained, giving the earlier mean-conditioned cone.
Finite signed forward differences are necessary but not a complete test of
membership in a finite Hausdorff moment cone.

3. Safe clock families and a useful distinction
- Common iid nonnegative increments X_j: T_k=sum_{j=1}^k X_j. Then
  E exp(-lambda T_k)=(E exp(-lambda X))^k, and for ANY finite homogeneous Q,
  E exp(Q T_k)=P^k with P=E exp(QX). P is stochastic. This preserves the same
  hidden-state factorization used by the dimension witness, as well as the
  reversible moment cone. No exponential clock, small jitter or rate cap is needed.
- Common random scale: T_k=k V, with one nonnegative scale law used independently
  on each record. Then x=exp(-lambda V) gives the full reversible moment cone.
  There need not be a single channel P on the same hidden state space.
- Mixtures over clock families that satisfy the scalar criterion preserve the
  thermodynamic cone by convexity. A mixture can add persistent hidden clock
  memory and therefore cannot automatically preserve a dimension bound.

Exact distinction: an underlying two-state chain and common scale V in {1,2}
with probabilities 1/2, at exp(-lambda)=1/2, yield prepared occupations
  p_k=1/2+(2^(-k)+4^(-k))/4.
Their 3-by-3 Hankel determinant is 9/32768>0. A two-dimensional common-channel
representation is impossible although the physical chain has only two states.
A three-state absorbing representation is explicitly supplied, so its minimum
common-channel dimension is exactly three. The mixed record includes an
uncontrolled scale variable; this is not a physical violation of a dimension
certificate whose common-channel contract is enforced.

4. Entropy accounting at random times
Assume the even-state stationary classical model, common noninvasive detector,
and additive reversibilization S=(Q+Q*)/2 from the earlier entropy chapter.
Retain the full joint record (setting k, actual clock time T_k, full path).
Clock laws are identical under Q and S and independent of the process. Therefore
  KL(joint Q || joint S) = E[T] d(Q,S) <= E[T] sigma/4.
Discarding actual times only decreases KL. If J is any certified lower bound on
the KL separation of observed binary records from the clock-compatible reversible
moment cone, then sigma>=4J/E[T], with E[T]=sum_k w_k E[T_k]. The relevant mean
must be finite and positive. If it is only upper bounded, use its calibrated
upper bound in the denominator. Mean duration alone does not establish clock
compatibility. For iid increments E[T_k]=k E[X]; for common scale it is k E[V].
Nonstationary preparation keeps the earlier preparation/housekeeping alternative,
but the expected housekeeping integral must extend to the actual random horizon.

5. Arbitrary jitter can manufacture equilibrium witnesses
All examples below use a stationary symmetric two-state chain with correlation
C(t)=2^(-t), so its physical entropy rate is exactly zero.
(a) Ordered means do not preserve monotonicity. T_1=1; T_2=0 with probability4/5
and10 with probability1/5. Their means are1 and2, but correlations are1/2 and
4097/5120, an increase1537/5120.
(b) Even stochastic ordering and exactly commensurate means do not preserve the
full cone. Couple clocks as (T_1,T_2,T_3)=(1,1,2) with probability9/10 and
(1,11,12) with probability1/10. Means are1,2,3 and each clock is pointwise later.
Correlations are1/2,9217/20480,9217/40960. They decrease, but their second
forward difference is -7171/40960, contradicting moment convexity.
These are genuine apparatus counterexamples, not evidence of thermodynamic
irreversibility. Common additive jitter with negative support retains some
shape inequalities on positive lags but need not retain the unshifted normalized
moment cone. An explicit nonnegative anchor and shifted indices are required.

6. Disjoint symmetric block detectors
Let each block have fixed duration L and a common conditional binary mean
F(path) in [-1,1], invariant under reversal of the path inside that block.
Blocks are noninvasive; their output noise is independent conditional on the
physical paths. Two blocks occupy [0,L] and [L+T,2L+T], with T>=0 independent
of the process. For the reversible reference S, define
  g(x)=E_S[F(X_[0,L]) | X_0=x].
Stationary path reversal gives the same function conditional on X_L=x.
The Markov property then gives
  E_S[Y_left Y_right | gap=T] = <g,exp(ST)g>_pi.
Since |g|<=1, this has a positive spectral measure of mass at most one.
Any clock family satisfying the criterion in section2 preserves the positive-gap
moment cone. No approximation to a point detector is required.

The total path used to generate the observation spans 2L+T. Consequently the
stationary entropy certificate is sigma>=4J/(2L+E[T]), not 4J/E[T]. If one uses
known stationary mean information, it must apply to the reversible reference:
for a general nonlinear block functional the means under Q and S can differ.
The mean-unrestricted outer cone with baseline1/2 and all conserved mass included
is safe; it does not assume a physically zero stationary mean. For a symmetric linear average
F=(integral_0^L w(u) f(X_u)du), w>=0, integral w=1 and w(u)=w(L-u), the mean is
pi f under either generator; the calibrated-mean refinement is then valid.
This covers rectangular exposure at the level of its bounded conditional mean.
It does not automatically cover invasive photon collection or arbitrary detector
memory, overlapping windows, or nonsymmetric exposure weighting.

7. Exposure failures, all at equilibrium
Use the bounded symmetric block channel F=(X_start+X_end)/2, realized by an
independent fair choice of one endpoint sign. For decay base x=1/2, length L and
start separation D, its exact correlation is
  [2x^D+x^(D+L)+x^|D-L|]/4.
For D=L+T>=L this equals ((1+x^L)/2)^2 x^T, exactly the corrected gap spectrum.
- Overlap: L=4, D=2 or4 gives49/256 and289/1024, a revival93/1024. One pair of
  windows overlaps, so the disjoint-block factorization is unavailable.
- Wrong time coordinate despite disjoint windows: L=2, start separations2 and4
  give25/64 and25/256. Calling these the first and second moments produces
  C_2-C_1^2=-225/4096<0. The true gaps are0 and2, not one and two common positive
  steps. Gap-zero correlations have spectral mass<=1 and should not be assigned
  the normalization C_0=1 of an ideal noiseless endpoint detector.
- Reversal-antisymmetric channel F=(X_end-X_start)/2: with L=2 and gap1, correlation
  is-9/128 and P(same)=119/256<1/2. The detector itself is odd under reversal;
  a positive autocorrelation spectral measure was never guaranteed.
