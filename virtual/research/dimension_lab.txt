confusion, fixed detuning/drive bias, and independent four-point amplitude/time
noise refreshed EACH repeated step. Averaging these IID channels gives one
homogeneous channel, preserving the target-independent classical theorem.

LIMITS
Monte Carlo uncertainty is conditional on the simulator. Device drifts, colored
noise, calibration failure and physical energy cost are not empirically bounded.
A state-count exclusion is not a proof that every classical explanation fails.
Same-record comparisons with an extra pilot are not free total-budget gains.
This work identifies a practical failure mechanism and a valid adaptive repair;
it is not a discovered physical law or claimed historical-first framework.
