Research question
Can an actual stationary physical model realize the apparent minimum entropy,
rather than merely attain an observational divergence relaxation?

Surviving conditional theorem
For every irreducible circulant n-state CTMC, fix its FIRST Fourier eigenvalue
-gamma+i omega. Among all nonnegative displacement rates, the minimum ordinary-
reversal stationary entropy is
  2 omega / sin(2pi/n) * atanh[(omega/gamma) tan(pi/n)].
Finite entropy is feasible iff |omega|/gamma < cot(pi/n). The nearest-neighbor
ring realizes the minimum. Complete proof is in manuscript/physical_minimum.tex:
pair jump angles, express each cost as gamma_j F_xj(u_j), use monotonicity in x
and convexity in u, include antipodal damping as a zero-u contribution.
This is a model-class optimization, not a universal inverse result.

Exact example and physical meaning

principal omega=.5504934894. Minima for n=16,20,40,100,1000 are approximately

Each ring realizes the WHOLE correlation function, not just two moments.
The circle is a physical candidate and therefore an upper bound on the infimum
in larger compatible-model classes containing it. It is NOT an upper bound on
the actual unknown entropy. All numerical values here are floating-point

Aliasing obstruction
Even full circle-state observations at grid spacing Delta have identical entire
sampled-record laws for omega+2pi k/Delta, while entropy diverges quadratically.
Three pairwise irrational lags determine |omega| from EXACT cosine values when
gamma,A are known; two fail at gaps 1,sqrt2 for pi(sqrt2 +/-1).
For ANY finite positive gap menu and bounded number of observations, simultaneous
Diophantine approximation gives unbounded frequency nearaliases whose full-state
record laws converge in TV. Sequential coupling also allows adaptive gap choice
from the finite menu. Thus a uniformly 1-alpha covered entropy upper bound must
be infinite with probability >=1-alpha at each reference model. Continuous random
timing with exact retained time and external bandwidth calibration are outside
this theorem. This separates exact identifiability from stable physical bounding.
Pell p^2-2q^2=1 gives a concrete two-gap nearalias with entropy 8.751688e12 and
whole two-interval state-record TV <=1.119197e-6; enormous rates are mathematical
model alternatives, not claims about available laboratory drive power.
