1. DIRECT FINITE-LAG SCALAR CERTIFICATE
Candidate supplied by spectral_dynamics:
  P=P_t, H=(P+Pdagger)/2, B=(P-Pdagger)/2,
  Delta_s=-s^2 C(0)+2s C(t)-C(2t)
         =||Bf||_pi^2-||(H-sI)f||_pi^2.
For a real stationary state observable with range R, conditional Pinsker and
endpoint/path data processing give sigma >= 8 positive(Delta_s)/(R^2 t).
The nonlinear version uses half-Jeffreys and gives sigma >= 2 z atanh(z)/t,
z=2 sqrt(positive(Delta_s))/R. These are candidate results pending the independent
review gate; this audit assesses prior art, not substitutes for a proof review.

Nearest established ingredients and differences:
- Reversible autocorrelation spectral positivity and complete monotonicity are
  classical (Aldous-Fill; existing reference). The equilibrium inequality
  C(t)^2 <= C(0) C(2t) is a familiar Cauchy-Schwarz consequence, not new.
- Vu, Vo, Saito, Phys Rev Research6,013273(2024), new key paVu2024, Eq13:
  |delta C_ba(t)|^2/(D_a(t)+D_b(t)) <= t sigma ||a^2+b^2||infinity
  (also a cycle-geometric improvement). AppendixD Eqs112-117 explicitly use
  endpoint/path KL contraction and a current-square bound. Setting a=b yields0.
  Our proposed scalar contrast does not require measuring a second observable
  or its cross-asymmetry: semigroup composition at t and 2t supplies a lower
  bound on an inaccessible antisymmetric response. This is a precise access
  difference, not a claim that the KL mechanism itself is new.
- Ohga/Ito/Kolchinsky2023, rfOhga2023, bounds cross-correlation asymmetry by
  cycle affinity (not stationary entropy). Shiraishi2023, rfShiraishi2023,
  gives a short-time entropy bound for two observables; Vu2024 extends finite t.
- Raux/Maier/Seifert2026, rfRaux2026, SecIII Eq30 bounds entropy using two-state-
  observable cross-asymmetry at arbitrary lag. It also vanishes for one common
  observable. Preprint status retained. It does not establish the scalar Delta
  identity or our calibration/finite-record construction in the text inspected.
- Variance sum rule Di Terlizzi et al2024, ciVSR2024/ciVSRProofs2024, NJP Sec3
  Eqs11-15, already extracts dissipation from time-symmetric position fluctuations.
  Its curvature lower bound involves second derivatives at t=0 and calibrated
  diffusion/mobility, with force-assisted exact formulas when full access exists.
  Therefore do not claim first scalar-correlation dissipation inference or first
  detection of strong nonequilibrium by a time-symmetric statistic.
- Sason2015, paSason2015, Eq17 defines J=(KL(P||Q)+KL(Q||P))/2; Proposition4,
  Eq19 proves min_{TV=z} J=2z atanh(z). This exactly supplies the nonlinear
  information inequality. Applying it to conditional transition rows followed by
  convexity in squared response is the present use; the sharp divergence inequality
  is established prior work. Existing Falasco2022 and Guntuboyina2014 are relevant.

Assessment: the focused primary-source search has not located the precise direct
single-observable, three-lag Delta_s entropy bound or its joint analog confidence
implementation. It is reasonable to present and independently review these specific
results as contributions of this work, with historical novelty explicitly unsettled.
The possible significance is a computable physical lower bound linear in the
certified quadratic-semigroup defect, removing an avoidable squaring introduced
by a generic observation-distance bound. It is not a proof of optimal physical
attainability and may remain loose for a given model.

2. CIRCULANT DYNAMICS: WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN
Gu2026, rfGu2026, Phys Rev E113,064130; primary arXiv2606.05498:
  SecII Eq10 sigma >= eta omega^2/gamma,
  eta=||v||^2_pi/||v||^2_infinity.
  SecV.1 Eqs33-39 explicitly covers arbitrary translation-invariant jumps on Z_n,
  gives exact Fourier eigenvalues, and proves eta=1 for every Fourier mode.
  SecV.2 Eqs40-43 gives the nearest-neighbor biased-ring eigenvalue formulas.
  SecV.3 Eqs44-48 gives circle drift diffusion, exact sigma=omega^2/gamma, and
  saturation by the increasingly fine nearest-neighbor ring.
Thus the continuum envelope and its saturation are established, not discoveries
of this phase. The proposed finite-n minimization over ALL displacement rates at
one prescribed fundamental eigenpair may be an additional statement. The inspected
Gu text does not state that sharper optimization; it must be proved rather than
inferred from the nearest-neighbor formula.

Barato-Seifert2017 rfBarato2017 bounds coherence by cyclic affinity and topology;
the conjectured affinity/coherence statement was proved by Ohga2023. Distinguish
this from the OBS entropy/eigenvalue conjecture, which is different and false in
general. Kolchinsky2026 paKolchinsky2026 proves a dissipation-coherence result
under a condition on phase-current fluctuations; weak-noise Gaussian regimes
satisfy it. It is not an unconditional finite-state spectral theorem.

Shiraishi2026 rfShiraishi2026, arXiv2609.34352, supplies an explicit small-entropy
counterexample to sigma >= omega^2/gamma using a rare driven layer. Primary full
text inspected, preprint status retained. Observable amplitude and stationary
weight cannot be discarded. The scalar Delta_s keeps these factors; a frequency
and decay inferred from an arbitrarily weak mode are not universal dissipation
lower bounds by themselves.

3. PHYSICAL REALIZATION OPTIMIZATION
Ehrich2021 ciEhrich2021, Sec4, fits masked discrete-time Markov models and minimizes
compatible entropy. It assumes the correct hidden-state number; the full text
explicitly discusses the nonlinear inverse problem and difficulty beyond the small
example. Nitzan/Ghosal/Bisker2023 ciNitzan2023, SecIII.1.2 Eqs16-18 and III.4,
minimizes CTMC entropy over a specified topology using observed transition statistics
and waiting-time moments. The authors explicitly leave universality of replacing
the true hidden topology by a smaller one open. A numerical local minimum in an
assumed model is therefore not a universal entropy lower bound. Feasible generators
give upper bounds on the compatible-model minimum, not on the true system entropy.
The broad optimization idea and moment hierarchy are prior art. Value here must
come from a provably solved class, a certificate valid without that architecture,
or a new observable/resource tradeoff.

4. TIME RESOLUTION AND ALIASING
Yue/Thunberg/Ljung/Yuan/Goncalves2021 paYue2021, IEEE TAC66,5788-5801,
DOI10.1109/TAC.2020.3042487; primary arXiv1605.08590 SecIII and Theorem6:
matrix-exponential inversion admits system aliases unless the generator's imaginary
spectrum is appropriately restricted. The critical spectral strip and principal
logarithm are standard system-identification results. Use a strict strip when
formulating a new theorem, because branch-boundary eigenvalues require care.
The circle example omega -> omega+2pi k/Delta makes all regular-grid transition
kernels identical and sigma unbounded, a thermodynamic specialization of aliasing.
Simultaneous Diophantine approximation gives arbitrarily high near-aliases for a
finite fixed menu of lags. The latter needs the protocol length, observed record
law and continuity estimate specified: pointwise moment similarity alone does not
prove indistinguishability for unbounded, adaptive or continuously randomized clocks.
The existing rfAznagulov2026 preprint uses randomized clocks and fully observed
snapshot dynamics to address a different observation class; do not state a no-go
that includes those protocols without an additional proof.

5. JOINT ANALOG INFERENCE
Rao-Blackwellization, bounded-variable concentration, moments of reversible
semigroups and the law of total variance are established. A common noisy reading
squared estimates B=E Z^2 >= A=E(E[Z|X])^2, and is not an independent latent-state
replicate. A negative coefficient on B can turn this distinction into a conservative
one-sided joint inequality. The contribution should be the exact calibrated
statistical/physical interface, its assumptions and reproducible acquisition-matched
comparison, not an invented general statistics principle or noiseless-equivalence
claim. A noisy squared reading can destroy power; retain that failure case.

PRESENTATION RECOMMENDATION
Lead with a finite-lag entropy certificate from one bounded observable, its exact
operator identity, and a validated analog acquisition recipe. Present the solvable
circulant minimum as a complementary physical reference, not the universal optimum.
Present aliasing as a theorem explaining what access must be added for upper bounds.
Retain unresolved historical priority and actual laboratory validation explicitly.
