1. Direct finite-lag theorem: survives within its stated contract.

For a real L2(pi) stationary homogeneous semigroup, write P=P_t, H=(P+P*)/2,
B=(P-P*)/2. The adjoint acts in the stationary measure, not the counting
measure. Direct expansion gives
  <f,P^2 f> = <P* f,Pf> = ||Hf||^2 - ||Bf||^2,
  Delta_s = -s^2 C0+2s C1-C2 = ||Bf||^2-||(H-sI)f||^2.
For range R, each row obeys |Bf(x)| <= R TV(P_x,P*_x)/2. Hence, writing
d=[Delta_s]+ and u=4d/R^2, u <= E_pi TV_x^2. The stationary endpoint joint
law and its transpose have equal KL divergences in both directions. Their
shared first marginal pi makes the endpoint KL equal the pi-average of
half-Jeffreys row divergences. The established sharp scalar inequality gives
  D_endpoint >= E_pi[2 v_x atanh(v_x)]
             >= Phi(E_pi v_x^2) >= Phi(u),
  Phi(u)=2 sqrt(u)atanh(sqrt(u))=2 sum_{k>=1}u^k/(2k-1).
Convexity and monotonicity follow from this positive series. Full ordinary
forward/reversed stationary path KL is t*sigma, and endpoint data processing
therefore yields sigma >= Phi(u)/t >= 8d/(R^2 t). This is full reversal,
not the additive-reversible comparison with path KL t*sigma/4 used earlier.
Interchanging those constants would be wrong.

The Hilbert-valued version also survives. A signed probability-row difference
of total variation v has vector integral v times a difference of barycenters.
Its norm is at most v times the image diameter R, since convex hulls preserve
diameter. The operator identity extends componentwise. Joint diameter is the
right constraint; summing separate scalar entropy certificates is not justified.
Unit-circle (cos,sin) access doubles the cosine-only defect at unchanged
diameter 2, but changes the observation channel and its detector/readout costs.

The rational Phi partial sum and geometric remainder enclose the lower-bound
formula, not actual dissipation. Independent 100-digit atanh evaluations check
multiple u and truncations including u=999/1000. The implementation correctly
flags u>=1 instead of clipping it; the flag is conditional on the confidence
event and alone does not falsify the apparatus. A zero range must not be used
as a denominator. Full hidden-state ordinary reversal, valid global detector
range, stationarity, calibrated time and memoryless noninvasive independent
endpoint noise are essential. Sample extrema do not establish a range bound.

Proof statement corrected during review: a clipped g=max(0,L) does NOT satisfy
2g<=Delta when Delta<0. The correct conclusion is 2g<=[Delta]+ (or the signed
L<=Delta/2 before clipping). Constant f=1 gives Delta=-(1-s)^2. Root and the

entropy implication was unaffected.

2. Endpoint sharpness is not continuous-time physical attainability.

The four-cycle discrete kernel with probabilities (1+z)/2 clockwise and
(1-z)/2 counterclockwise, uniform pi and f=(1,1,-1,-1), has s=0,
Delta=z^2, R=2 and endpoint KL=Phi(z^2). It saturates every endpoint step.
However det(P)=-z^2<0, whereas det(exp(tQ))=exp(t trQ)>0. It cannot be a
finite-CTMC transition matrix. Its sharpness does not establish the physical
rate bound's sharpness or the unrestricted physical minimum. Finite hidden
state dimension is not bounded by the theorem, but this is a different claim.

(b) A reversible generator plus nonstationary preparation is insufficient.

(c) Even a stationary reversible output can fail the memoryless-observable
contract. Let X be stationary symmetric telegraph noise with C_X(v)=exp(-|v|),
underlying physical EPR0, t=log2,h=2t. Define the causal filtered observation
Y_u=X_u-2X_(u-h)+X_(u-2h), range8. Its covariance is

The centered palindromic filter X_(u+h)-2X_u+X_(u-h) commutes with time
reversal, and Y is a time shift of it; the entire observed output is reversible.
It is nevertheless not an instantaneous observable of the system Markov
state. This counterexample concerns inference about the underlying system;
it does not assert that a physical memory-bearing detector has zero cost.

4. Finite-n circulant optimization and a noncirculant countermodel.

For paired displacement angle theta, x=tan(theta/2), damping gamma_j and
frequency ratio u_j=omega_j/gamma_j, entropy equals gamma_j F_x(u_j), where
F_x(u)=u(1+x^2)atanh(xu)/x. F is even and convex in u with
F_x''(u)=2(1+x^2)/(1-x^2 u^2)^2, and nondecreasing in x at fixed u. Replacing
x by x_min=tan(pi/n), including any entropy-free antipodal damping at u0,
then applying Jensen proves the claimed all-circulant finite-n minimum.
Nearest-neighbor positive rates attain it iff |omega|/gamma<cot(pi/n).
At the boundary only one-way active motion can attain the frequency ratio,
giving infinite ordinary-reversal entropy. The continuum value omega^2/gamma
is approached by finite rings and attained by the ordinary-even circle
diffusion; the broader delocalized-mode bound and circle saturation are prior
work. This is an architecture-scoped result, not a universal physical minimum.

The new gated-hub construction explicitly defeats such an unrestricted claim.
Four active ring states have f=(1,1,-1,-1), total stationary mass A=.1;
a hub has f0 and mass.9. Exit rate kappa=.01, balanced return total A*kappa/(1-A),
and active ring rates(.745,.245) give exact C(t)=.1 e^-t cos(.5t) for all t.
Hub fluxes are balanced and active EPR is .1*.5 log(.745/.245)=.05560630039,
strictly below the circle value .25. Independent matrices verify stationarity,
full covariance on33 time points, and unordered-flux entropy. A single-mode
correlation law alone therefore does not establish the circulant architecture.

5. Aliasing and the upper-confidence impossibility theorem.

Grid observations of the full circle state have exactly the same law under
omega and omega+2pi*k/Delta, with entropy diverging quadratically. For a fixed
finite positive gap menu and bounded number of intervals, simultaneous
Diophantine approximation gives unbounded shifts with every wrapped phase
increment approaching0. A shifted unwrapped Gaussian comparison gives
TV<=|wrapped_shift|/(2sqrt(pi gamma t)); wrapping contracts TV. Sequential
coupling applies also to adaptive choices from the same finite menu, because
on matched histories both experiments select the same next gap. Bounded
independent replication multiplies the TV bound. Thus arbitrarily high entropy
models approach every reference record law in total variation.

If U has uniform1-alpha upper coverage over all these frequencies, for every
finite M the nearby high-entropy laws imply P_ref(U>=M)>=1-alpha. Letting M
increase gives P_ref(U=+infinity)>=1-alpha. The proof survives independent
adversarial review. Finite menu, bounded experiment and unrestricted frequency
are material. It says nothing about exact continuous-random observation times,
unbounded horizons or an externally calibrated frequency cap.

The three-pairwise-irrational-lag exact identification claim is also correct
when amplitude A and damping gamma are known: among three signs in cosine
equalities, two agree; their irrational time ratio forces both winding integers
to vanish, leaving omega'=+/-omega. Two lags do not suffice in general:
pi(sqrt2-1) and pi(sqrt2+1) agree at times1,sqrt2. Exact identification does
not provide a stable finite-precision upper bound. The independent suite
integrates the actual wrapped heat kernel and checks Pell near-aliases.
