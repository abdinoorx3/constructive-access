# Calibrated inference research

Inherit ../AGENTS.md. Owner: Abdinoor / 2D Labs. Preserve all five prior releases.

Goal: determine how much hidden physical dissipation can be certified from
calibrated, restricted observations, and identify which further access or
assumptions are required for stronger conclusions. Improve practical statistical
efficiency without concealing calibration, dependence, clock or reset costs.

Every branch must distinguish established ingredients, proved conditional
statements, numerical evidence and empirical validation. Freeze new evaluation
designs before generating held-out samples. Keep unsuccessful comparisons and
counterexamples. No claim of a new universal physical law from simulations.

Agents own distinct named files under code/, tests/, research/, manuscript/,
contracts/ and results/. Root owns shared integration, logs and release. Packaged source is ../src; use PYTHONPATH=src from the release root.
The development ancestor was ../virtual_laboratory/release/src; it is not
required to run this self-contained release and must not be modified.

Physical cost means declared evolution time and calibrated energy where justified.
Calibration, preparation and detector overhead must be stated separately when
not measured. An unmeasured overhead is not zero.

No external messages, publication, purchases or physical apparatus operations.
