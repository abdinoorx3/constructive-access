Goal
Preserve useful analog amplitude information while retaining the existing
finite-data physical certificate, and determine whether avoiding optional
encoding noise improves a complete inference procedure at matched access.

Established ingredients and claimed contribution
Conditional expectation/Rao--Blackwellization, convex order under a martingale
coupling, Hoeffding concentration and data processing are established. The work
does not claim to discover them. The derived apparatus result connects them to
the calibrated reversible moment class and stationary path-KL bound, provides
an exact rational mean/sum API, and tests the entire inference procedure under
a frozen matched acquisition design. There is no claim of a new physical law.

Proof
For bounded Z from a fixed memoryless noninvasive channel with conditional
mean f and independent endpoint noise, W=(1+Z0*Zt)/2 has mean
p=(1+<f,exp(Qt)f>_pi)/2. The virtual common kernel B|analog~Bern(W)
has Bernoulli marginal p. Data processing under Q and its additive reversible
reference S therefore gives d_Ber(pQ||pS) <= KL(analogQ||analogS) <= pathKL.
One may infer p from W itself without drawing B. Calibration uses m=EZ and
A=E f^2 <= Bobs=E Z^2; Bobs is an upper bound, not an equality in general.

For a fixed contrast a and quotas N, bounded-score conditional Hoeffding gives
radius sqrt(log(1/alpha)*sum(a_k^2 width_k^2/N_k)/2). Subtract exact calibrated
support, sum |a_k| quantization errors and sum |a_k| conditional-mean drift
radii. On the science and calibration confidence events, positive remaining
gap C yields sigma >= 8 C^2/(sum |a_k| sqrt(full_duration_k))^2. The API uses
exact outward rational bounds. Means may be arbitrary rationals, not counts/N.
Selection must be predeclared or independent-pilot, and quotas are fixed.

Given the entire analog bank, the direct contrast D equals E[encoded H|bank].
Conditional Jensen proves convex-order improvement for every convex test
function. For independent records Var(H)-Var(D) is exactly
sum a_k^2 E[W_k(1-W_k)]/N_k. Threshold rejection indicators are not convex;
universal rejection-power dominance is neither proved nor asserted. Physical
record dependence does not spoil the conditional-expectation identity, but
requires a valid inference sampling contract.

Quantization
Stored finite floating values are interpreted as exact digital readings.
Each W is formed in Fraction arithmetic and rounded to the nearest grid1/M.
The resulting empirical mean has arithmetic error <=1/(2M); no record-count
growth. This does not bound sensor or physical-model error. Endpoint errors
eta0,eta1 within [-1,1] imply score error <=(eta0+eta1)/2. A descriptive helper
does not infer stationarity, Markovianity, independence or physical entropy.

Scope/failure cases
Direct scores do not repair detector memory, correlated endpoint noise,
nonstationarity, wrong reversal convention, same-sample score selection,
unbounded analog values or invalid independent-record calibration. Finite
exposure needs the earlier exposure contract and full-duration accounting;
arbitrary nonlinear exposures need not preserve calibrated m,A under S.
Different sensor channels f1,f2 give cross moment E f1 f2, not E f1^2. Shared
noisy single reading encodings estimate E Z^2, not A. No universal test-power
dominance or universal entropy upper bound is asserted.
