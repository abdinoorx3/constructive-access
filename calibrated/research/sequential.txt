The theorem uses conditional Hoeffding and Ville with predictable rational
coefficients, supports and bets. It does not claim a new concentration result.
At the true stationary entropy rate, exp(S-H sqrt(sigma/8)) is a nonnegative
supermartingale. Inverting at that true parameter yields simultaneous coverage;
no union over the continuum of candidate entropy values is required.

The conditional mean envelope must cover each readout given all previous
readouts, including its own batch. Stationary marginals are insufficient.
Independent prerun calibration is the supported calibration case. Dynamically
updated calibration needs a separate simultaneous-good-event/stopped-process
argument and is not inferred merely by conditioning on a favorable event.

future bits. All methods share the same retained bank. Exact policy replay
checks integer vectorization and stopping. Subsequent code hardening propagates
the frozen alpha/duration values explicitly and rejects integer-overflow ranges;
it does not alter that frozen design or its predictions.
