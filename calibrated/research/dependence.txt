Goal
Replace a long blind mixing gap before every restricted pair measurement by
a useful rigorously justified dependent-record confidence calculation. Account
for physical evolution time, initial preparation/mixing, detector operations
and the external status of all mixing calibration. A result is allowed to fail
outside its resource or calibration regime.

Established ingredients
Fan, Jiang and Sun (2021), JMLR22(139), Theorem1, gives the stationary
absolute-L2-contraction Hoeffding MGF for deterministic time-dependent bounded
state functions. Conditional Hoeffding controls record noise as a martingale.
Holder combines the two without an independence assumption. The integrated
whole-record bound, contract, acquisition design and physical inference API are
the contribution here; concentration itself is not claimed new.

Proof and interpretation
With externally calibrated gap lambda of -(Q+Qadjoint)/2, starts of common
H-duration blocks have absolute L2 contraction rho<=exp(-lambda H). For a
fixed setting in each block, g_i(x)=E[Z_i|X_i=x] is deterministic, Z_i in[0,1].
The state-function proxy is A=(1+rho)/(1-rho), and the conditional martingale
proxy is1. Holder yields K=(sqrt(A)+1)^2. For deterministic c_i,
 E exp(theta sum c_i(Z_i-E Z_i)) <= exp(theta² K sum c_i²/8).
One-sided radius sqrt(K log(1/alpha) sum c_i²/2). For fixed group quotas,
sum c_i²=sum a_k²/n_k. Whole-record calibration errors add sum|a_k|delta_k.
A probability box follows by two-sided union bound. Fixed-contrast selection
must precede held-out outcomes; a valid joint box may support later selection.
The theorem does not cover adaptive routing, irregular block durations, or
unmodeled detector memory. Fresh conditional detector noise is required at a
shared endpoint unless all detector memory is included in calibrated state.
K tends4, not1, as rho tends0: no claim of recovering sharp iid concentration.

Initial distance
One blind initial interval G gives TV<=0.5sqrt((1-pmin)/pmin)exp(-lambda G).
This is transferred once to the entire future law, giving failure alpha+beta.
It is not an observed reset-success event, per-record drift or a calibration
failure probability. A separate valid external calibration failure gamma can
be added once. Coarse data alone do not establish lambda or pmin; all synthetic
validity checks compare actual model gap against the externally declared one.

Fundamental slow-mixing illustration
Stationary2-state models with common0->1 ratekappa and1->0 rates3kappa or
kappa/3 have stationary occupation1/4 or3/4. The full continuous record has
overlapping constant-path masses >=(1/4)exp(-kappa T) and
>=(1/4)exp(-3kappa T). Equal-prior Bayeserror >=(exp(-kappa T)+
exp(-3kappa T))/8. Thus stationary occupation estimation with error<1/4 under
both hypotheses cannot reach a fixed sufficiently small failure target
without order1/kappa time, even with complete trajectory observation. This
is a standard testing-overlap argument, not a proof current radius is optimal.

Open boundary
A more efficient concentration bound might exploit known covariance or a
detector-specific spectral structure. It must not silently use an unknown
full generator. Current K is conservative; sparse acquisition trades margin
for detector operations. Physical deployment requires credible whole-state
mixing and detector calibration and a measured cost model. These conditions,
not additional sampling of the same uncalibrated record, control transfer.
