1. QUESTION AND RESULT
Can calibrated partial observations upper-bound total physical dissipation without
a calibrated reverse-rate or affinity restriction? Even fixed graph, four states,
known positive exit rates, known stationary distribution, known activity and
observation of every jump time do not suffice in general. A simple four-cycle
exhibits exact coarse-record equivalence with entropy spanning [0,infinity).
Distinct known exit-rate tags restore structural identification from the same
binary detector, yet finite physical exposure cannot uniformly resolve the
singular entropy near zero reverse rates. This second obstruction survives
complete microscopic observation and is therefore not merely coarse-graining.

2. MODEL AND EXACT CALCULATIONS
States i=0,1,2,3 modulo four; exit rates r_i>0; 0<q<=1/2.
Q(i,i+1)=r_i(1-q), Q(i,i-1)=r_i q, Q(i,i)=-r_i.
Define Z=sum_i 1/r_i. Then pi_i=1/(Z r_i), K=4/Z, and
sigma=K(1-2q) log((1-q)/q).
Stationary edge fluxes are (1-q)/Z and q/Z. The graph is simple, connected and
irreducible. Every transition flips the detector parity. There is no independent
spectator, no parallel edge and no missed jump time.

If r_i=s, parity is exactly the symmetric rate-s two-state CTMC, independent q.
Its complete timed paths and e^(-2st) binary autocorrelation agree for all q.
Stationary zero-lag amplitude is exactly 1. Bounded observed-history feedback
multiplying both exit directions equally preserves equivalence. Knowing exit
rates does not mean knowing the microscopic forward/reverse split.

3. KINETIC TAGS AND THE ACCESS REPAIR
Write f_i(t)=r_i exp(-r_i t). At a typical stationary even jump entry (Palm law),
state 0/2 is uniform and two successive holding times have density
  g_q(u,v)=(f0+f2)(u)(f1+f3)(v)/4
           +(1-2q)(f0-f2)(u)(f1-f3)(v)/4.
Therefore
  TV(g_q,g_q')=|q-q'| TV(Exp(r0),Exp(r2)) TV(Exp(r1),Exp(r3)).
Both within-parity rate contrasts nonzero imply structural identification of q
within this calibrated family. The Palm law is not automatically the first even
entry after an arbitrary clock origin; successive pairs are not declared IID.

For r=(1+eta,1+2eta,1-eta,1-2eta), 0<eta<1/2, pair TV is order eta^2.
For the binary event u<=1,v<=1 the absolute probability derivative is
2 exp(-2) sinh(eta)sinh(2eta) = 4 exp(-2) eta^2+O(eta^4).
An independently sampled version has conservative Hoeffding record demand
eta^-4 at fixed bias separation. Preparing independent Palm samples is additional
access, with reset/time costs not supplied by this algebra.

For total exposure L, the complete coarse record obeys
TV(P_q^parity,P_q'^parity) <= min(1,L max(|r0-r2|,|r1-r3|)).
Each filtered parity jump intensity lies within the two corresponding exit rates.
Couple these intensities while observed histories agree. Initial parity laws
coincide because the stationary law is independent q. This quantitative bound
shows why weak tags do not automatically provide affordable inference.

4. FINITE-EXPOSURE NO-GO WITH FULL MICROSCOPIC RECORDS
Let R=max r_i. Uniformize both models with one rate-R Poisson clock, common
acceptance uniforms, and common direction uniforms. Until a mismatch, directions
differ with probability |q-q'| at an accepted transition. Therefore
  TV(P_q,P_q') <= min(1,R L |q-q'|).
This bounds full state/direction/time records and thus any coarsening. Independent
resets with a common initial law and a pathwise total exposure bound are allowed.
Bounded controls preserving the direction parameter and exit cap also fit.

For any L>0, epsilon in (0,1), and M>0 choose
q0=min(1/4,epsilon/(2RL)); then choose q1>0 sufficiently small. Both finite models
have the same known graph, state count, activity, exit rates and stationary law,
record TV <=epsilon/2, yet |sigma(q1)-sigma(q0)|>M. Thus no estimator has uniform
finite additive error over this family. A two-point argument gives error
probability >=(1-epsilon)/2 at absolute-error threshold half the entropy gap for
at least one of the models. This concerns finite experimental resources, not
failure of structural identifiability for all observation times.

A uniformly valid upper confidence bound U, with coverage 1-alpha for every
positive q, must satisfy
  P_q(U=infinity) >= max(0,1-alpha-RLq).
Proof: introduce the q=0 law only as a limit. TV(Pq,P0)<=RLq. Along q_j down to0,
coverage and sigma(q_j) up to infinity imply P0(U=infinity)>=1-alpha by continuity
from above. Transfer that event to the finite-q law. No infinite-affinity model is
used as either physical member of the finite-gap construction.

This does NOT imply every data-dependent upper is always infinite. Observed
reverse events can exclude small q. It rules out uniformly finite guaranteed
upper bounds across the unrestricted positive-q family.

5. REPAIRS AND PHYSICAL ACCOUNTING
One binary edge-identity tag on a known physical edge repairs structural
identifiability: parity before/after identifies its direction, and stationary
edge fluxes give sigma=4(Fplus-Fminus)log(Fplus/Fminus). This is established
unicyclic inference, not a new theorem of visible-edge reconstruction. Counts on
that selected edge are dependent; no finite-time binomial law is asserted.

With every direction resolved, a fixed quota of n jump marks gives IID
Bernoulli(q). Exact two-sided binomial inversion yields [qminus,qplus]; intersect
with q<=1/2 and an optional calibrated floor q>=delta. Monotonicity gives entropy
interval [sigma(qU),sigma(qL)], with infinity when qL=0. A positive qL from
observed reverse events can make this finite even if delta=0. Empty intersection
is returned as model incompatibility, not as a certified physical interval.
Conditioning on N jumps by a fixed time is NOT interchangeable when exit rates
vary with visited state. Fixed jump quota has random duration.

With rmin=min r_i, E(T_n)<=n/rmin. Timeout2n/rmin has probability at most
(2/e)^n <=(3/4)^n, by exponential holding-time domination and Chernoff. A timeout
returns the full prior interval. Completed-only selection cannot be treated as
an unbiased conditional binomial sample. Detector/reset/preparation costs remain
unmeasured additional overhead.

For a known stationary edge-flux affinity cap |log(F_e/B_e)|<=A, the sharp bound
sigma<=K A tanh(A/2) follows by writing each contribution as
(F_e+B_e) a_e tanh(a_e/2). The homogeneous-flux ring attains it. This is a flux
ratio, not automatically a bare kinetic rate ratio. If only
|log(Qij/Qji)|<=B is known, stationarity cancels the population potential and the
safe general bound is sigma<=KB. Individual rate bounds m<=Qij<=M imply
sigma<=K log(M/m); total exit-rate bounds alone do not.

All entropy is natural entropy per declared model time. Conversion to heat or
work requires an appropriate physical reservoir/local-detailed-balance model.
One compatible embedding has dimensionless E_i=log r_i and clockwise driving
work log((1-q)/q). The large ambiguity corresponds to unbounded uncalibrated
affinity, not a source of thermodynamic energy from nothing.
